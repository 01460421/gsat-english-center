T3　中式英文陷阱 20 條

練習 A　改錯（每題括號內為陷阱編號）

T3-01 My sister went to the night market and bought a pair of sneakers last Friday.／My sister went to the night market to buy a pair of sneakers last Friday.（陷阱 1　雙動詞）went 和 bought 都是限定動詞，中間沒有連接詞。表示先後兩個動作用 and；強調「去的目的」用 to＋原形（to buy，不是 to bought）。原句屬於句構錯誤，這個語意單位通常會被重扣。其他寫法：My sister bought a pair of sneakers at the night market last Friday.（只用一個動詞，最不容易錯）
T3-02 It rained all morning, so the baseball game was postponed until Sunday.（陷阱 3　逗號連接兩句）兩個完整子句只靠逗號相接。可加 so，或改成 Because it rained all morning, the baseball game …，或用句號分成兩句。常見的錯誤改法：…, therefore, the game … 仍然錯，therefore 是副詞，前面要用句號或分號。
T3-03 Our teacher gave us a lot of useful advice before the speech contest.（陷阱 19　不可數名詞）advice 不可數，不加 s。想強調「好幾條」：several useful tips、some useful suggestions、a few pieces of advice。注意 advise 是動詞，不要混用。
T3-04 Although the bubble tea shop is far from our school, it is always crowded after class.／The bubble tea shop is far from our school, but it is always crowded after class.（陷阱 5　although … but）兩個連接詞只能留一個，刪 but 或刪 although 都可以。也可用 Even though 加強對比。雙連接詞屬結構錯，扣分比單字錯重。
T3-05 Most of my classmates agree that the school uniform should be more comfortable.（陷阱 6　多餘的 be）agree 是一般動詞，前面不加 be。Most of my classmates 有 my，用法正確，不要改。其他寫法：Most of my classmates think the school uniform should be more comfortable.
T3-06 The weather in Taipei is getting hotter and hotter these days.（陷阱 8　「越來越」直譯）hot 是單音節形容詞，比較級是 hotter（t 要重複），不是 more hot。其他寫法：It is getting hotter and hotter in Taipei these days.／Taipei is getting increasingly hot these days.
T3-07 My grandfather bought me an electric scooter as a graduation gift.（陷阱 17　冠詞漏用）scooter 是可數單數名詞，前面一定要有限定詞；electric 以母音開頭，用 an。只寫 a 也算錯一處，同樣會被扣分。
T3-08 Since I joined the school band two years ago, I have practiced the trumpet every weekday.（陷阱 16　時態不一致）since＋過去時間點，主要子句要用現在完成式（have practiced 或 have been practicing）。since 子句本身用過去式 joined 是對的，不要改。其他寫法：拿掉 since，改成 I joined the school band two years ago, and I practice the trumpet every weekday.（兩個子句各有自己的時間，也成立）
T3-09 My older brother often helps me with my physics homework.（陷阱 9　help 誤用）help＋人＋with＋名詞。help 後面不能接兩個名詞受詞。其他寫法：My older brother often helps me do my physics homework.（help＋人＋原形動詞）
T3-10 The long train ride made me feel tired, so I fell asleep as soon as I got home.（陷阱 10　「讓我覺得」）let 是「允許」，火車不會「允許」你累；「使得」用 make＋人＋原形動詞，不加 to。其他寫法：The long train ride tired me out, …／I was exhausted after the long train ride, …
T3-11 Our neighborhood has a small park where elderly people do tai chi every morning.／In our neighborhood, there is a small park where elderly people do tai chi every morning.（陷阱 11　「有」直譯）介系詞片語 In our neighborhood 不能當 has 的主詞。改成以 neighborhood 當主詞用 has，或保留地點片語、改用 there is。where 引導的關係子句修飾 park，正確。
T3-12 There were a lot of tourists in Jiufen on the long weekend.／There were many tourists in Jiufen on the long weekend.（陷阱 12　「很多的」堆疊）a lot of 和 many 只能選一個。想更生動：Jiufen was packed with tourists on the long weekend.
T3-13 One of my favorite singers is going to hold a concert in Taichung next month.（陷阱 18　單複數）one of 後面接複數名詞 singers；真正的主詞是 One，所以動詞 is 是對的，不要改成 are。is going to 表示已經安排好的未來，正確。
T3-14 When we arrived at the station, we found that the last train had already left.（陷阱 2　缺主詞）主要子句的 found 沒有主詞，補上 we。had left 是過去完成式，表示火車開走發生在「我們發現」之前，正確。其他寫法：…, we realized that we had missed the last train.
T3-15 Our class discussed the graduation trip at the class meeting yesterday.／Our class talked about the graduation trip at the class meeting yesterday.（陷阱 20　介系詞誤用）discuss 是及物動詞，直接接受詞。名詞 discussion 才可以加 about：Our class had a discussion about the graduation trip.
T3-16 Bicycles made in Taiwan are popular in Europe.／Taiwan-made bicycles are popular in Europe.（陷阱 13　「的」連用）「臺灣製造的腳踏車」：長修飾語 made in Taiwan 要放在名詞後面。若要放前面，必須寫成加連字號的複合形容詞 Taiwan-made。這裡泛指一般的臺灣製腳踏車，不加 the 更自然。
T3-17 The school festival took place last Saturday, and many parents came.／The school festival was held last Saturday, and many parents came.（陷阱 7　主動與被動混淆）happen 是不及物動詞，沒有被動，was happened 一律錯。活動「舉行」用 take place（主動）或 be held（被動）。只把 was 刪掉寫成 happened 文法上成立，但 happen 多指意外或突發的事，有計畫的學校活動用 took place／was held 更恰當。
T3-18 Because my phone battery died, I couldn't call my mom after cram school.／My phone battery died, so I couldn't call my mom after cram school.（陷阱 4　because … so）because 和 so 只留一個。如果想保留兩個意思分成兩句：My phone battery died. As a result, I couldn't call my mom after cram school.
T3-19 People in my hometown are very friendly to tourists.（陷阱 14　「人們」濫用）people 本身就是複數，不加 s；peoples 只在指「各民族」時使用。更精準的寫法：The residents of my hometown are very friendly to tourists.／Locals in my hometown are very friendly to tourists.
T3-20 During the Lantern Festival, Pingxi Old Street was packed with people.（陷阱 15　字面直譯成語）「人山人海」翻意思：非常擁擠。其他寫法：… Pingxi Old Street was extremely crowded.／During the Lantern Festival, huge crowds filled Pingxi Old Street. 逐字直譯等於沒翻出這個語意單位。

練習 B　綜合改錯

T3-21 I love basketball, but since I entered senior high school, I have seldom had time to play it because my schedule is full of tests.共 3 個錯。(1) Although 和 but 同時出現（陷阱 5），刪掉其一。(2) since 子句後的主要子句要用現在完成式：had → have had（陷阱 16）。(3) 最後的 my schedule is full of tests 是完整子句，只靠逗號接上（陷阱 3），加 because 或用句號另起一句。其他寫法：Although I love basketball, I have seldom had time to play it since I entered senior high school. My schedule is full of tests.
T3-22 More and more students use their smartphones to do their homework because it is very convenient.共 3 個錯。(1) There are more and more students use …：there are 之後又接動詞 use，形成雙動詞（陷阱 1、8、11），直接以 More and more students 當主詞。(2) to doing → to do：表目的的 to 後面接原形（陷阱 1 修正法第 2 招）。(3) it is very convenient 只用逗號接上（陷阱 3），改用 because。其他寫法：An increasing number of students do their homework on their smartphones because they find it convenient.
T3-23 My mother always says that eating breakfast is good for our health.共 3 個錯。(1) 主詞 My mother 是第三人稱單數：say → says（陷阱 18）。(2) eat 不能當主詞，改成動名詞 eating（主詞是動名詞片語，動詞 is 用單數）。(3) very help for 錯：help 不是形容詞，「對…有益」用 be good for／be beneficial to（陷阱 9）。其他寫法：My mother always tells us that having breakfast benefits our health.
T3-24 Last weekend, my family went to Hualien by train. The scenery along the coast made us feel very relaxed.共 3 個錯。(1) Last weekend 是過去時間：go → went（陷阱 16）。(2) 兩個完整子句只用逗號接（陷阱 3），用句號分開或加 and。(3) let us feel → made us feel（陷阱 10）。scenery 不可數，原句用單數是對的，不要改成 sceneries。其他寫法：…, and the scenery along the coast was very relaxing.
T3-25 The new library, which opened last month, has a lot of information about local history.共 2 個錯。(1) was opened 和 has 是兩個限定動詞卻沒有連接詞（陷阱 1），把前者改成關係子句：which opened／that opened／which was opened。(2) informations → information（陷阱 19）。其他寫法：The new library that opened last month has many books on local history.（換成可數的 books，就要用 many）
T3-26 This temple in Lukang was built more than two hundred years ago, and every year it attracts thousands of tourists from abroad.共 3 個錯。(1) 廟是「被建造」：built → was built（陷阱 7）。(2) thousands of 後面接複數名詞：tourist → tourists（陷阱 18）。(3) abroad 是副詞，from abroad 是固定說法，不加 the（陷阱 17 亂加冠詞）。其他寫法：Built more than two hundred years ago, this temple in Lukang attracts thousands of visitors from overseas every year.（分詞構句，句子更緊湊）

練習 C　避坑翻譯

T3-27 Although the MRT station is far from my home, I get to school on time every day.陷阱：雖然…但（陷阱 5）只能留一個連接詞；「每天」是習慣，用現在簡單式（陷阱 16）；「到校」是 get to school 或 arrive at school，不是 arrive school（陷阱 20）。語意單位：讓步部分（捷運站離家遠）、主要部分（每天準時到校）。其他寫法：The MRT station is far from my home, but I arrive at school on time every day.／Even though I live far from the MRT station, I am never late for school.
T3-28 More and more students spend their lunch break studying in the library.陷阱：「越來越多」（陷阱 8），主詞是複數，動詞 spend 不加 s（陷阱 18）；「利用…讀書」兩個動作，不能寫成 use … study（陷阱 1），用 spend＋時間＋V-ing 或 use＋時間＋to V。其他寫法：An increasing number of students use their lunch break to study in the library.／A growing number of students study in the library during their lunch break.
T3-29 This graduation trip taught me how to work with my classmates.陷阱：「讓我學到」不要寫 let me learn（陷阱 10），用 teach 最精簡；畢業旅行已結束，用過去式（陷阱 16）；「如何合作」用 how to＋原形。其他寫法：During this graduation trip, I learned how to cooperate with my classmates.／On this graduation trip, I learned how to work together with my classmates.
T3-30 Near our school, there is a breakfast shop that is very popular with students.陷阱：「在…附近有」是地點＋有，用 there is，不是 In … has（陷阱 11）；「很受學生歡迎的早餐店」長修飾語放名詞後，用關係子句（陷阱 13）；popular 是形容詞，前面要 is（陷阱 6）。popular with／among students 都可以。其他寫法：A breakfast shop near our school is very popular among students.（不用 there，句子更短）
T3-31 Since I entered senior high school, I have rarely had time to eat dinner with my family.陷阱：「自從」→ 主要子句用現在完成式（陷阱 16）；中文前半「上了高中」省略主詞，英文 since 子句要補 I（陷阱 2）；這裡「有時間」的主詞是人，用 have 正確（對照陷阱 11）。rarely／seldom 放在 have 和 had 之間。其他寫法：Since starting senior high school, I have seldom been able to have dinner with my family.／I have rarely eaten dinner with my family since I started senior high school.
T3-32 Because a typhoon is approaching, the school has decided to cancel tomorrow's field trip.陷阱：因為…所以只留一個（陷阱 4）；「即將來襲」用現在進行式表示正在逼近（陷阱 16），decided 用過去式也可以；typhoon 是可數單數，前面要 a（陷阱 17）。「校外教學」是 field trip。其他寫法：A typhoon is coming, so the school has decided to call off tomorrow's field trip.／Due to the approaching typhoon, the school has canceled tomorrow's field trip.
T3-33 This bridge was built during the Japanese colonial period, and it is still used by many residents today.陷阱：中文沒有「被」，但「建於」和「被使用」都要用被動（陷阱 7）；「建於」是過去式，「至今仍」是現在式，一句兩種時態（陷阱 16）；兩個子句用 and 連接，不能只用逗號（陷阱 3）。其他寫法：Built during the Japanese colonial period, this bridge is still used by many local residents.／This bridge, which dates back to the Japanese colonial period, is still used by many residents.
T3-34 You can find a lot of information about this city online, but not all of it is accurate.陷阱：「在網路上可以找到」沒有主詞，補 you 或改被動（陷阱 2）；information 不可數，後面用 it、is，不是 them、are（陷阱 19）；online 是副詞，前面不加 on，或寫 on the Internet（陷阱 20）。not all of it 表示「並非全部」。其他寫法：A great deal of information about this city can be found on the Internet, but not all of it is correct.／There is plenty of information about this city online, but some of it is not reliable.
