Economists call the medium bag a decoy, and the shift it causes is known as the decoy effect. 1 What it does instead is change how the other choices look: placed beside it, the large bag seems to offer a great deal of extra popcorn for very little extra money.

2 We are rarely sure how much a bag of popcorn, a phone, or a holiday is really worth, so we judge each option by comparing it with whatever happens to be next to it. In the cinema, the medium bag costs almost as much as the large one but holds far less, so choosing the large bag suddenly feels like an obvious, easy decision.

Almost no one chose print only, yet most went for the combined deal; when the print-only choice was removed, however, most picked the cheaper online-only offer instead.
