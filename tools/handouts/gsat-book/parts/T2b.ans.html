T2b　高頻句型 11–20

T2-21 Having gotten off at the wrong station, we were twenty minutes late for school.關鍵結構：前後主詞都是 we，可以省略連接詞與主詞；「下錯站」發生在「遲到」之前，用 Having + p.p. 最精確。美式英文 get 的過去分詞是 gotten。常見扣分：寫 Get off 或 Got off（變成原形或過去式，句子出現兩個主要動詞）；寫 Getting off at the wrong station, our class was late（主詞換掉就不能用分詞構句）。其他寫法：Getting off at the wrong station（兩個動作緊接發生時也可接受）；不用分詞構句也完全正確：Because we got off at the wrong station, we were twenty minutes late for school.
T2-22 The heavy rain lasted all afternoon, leaving many students stuck at school.關鍵結構：「使得…」是前面整件事造成的結果，用逗號加 leaving + O + 形容詞／p.p.。「被困」用 stuck 或 trapped，是形容詞性的過去分詞。常見扣分：…, made many students stuck（逗號後直接接過去式，雙動詞）；…, it made many students …（逗號連接兩個完整句）；「暴雨」寫成 violent rain 搭配不自然。其他寫法：The rainstorm went on for the whole afternoon, which left many students trapped at school.／It rained heavily all afternoon, and as a result, many students were stuck at school.
T2-23 It is impossible for new students to remember all their classmates' names in the first week.關鍵結構：It is + adj. + for 人 + to V；impossible 描述的是「這件事」，所以用 for。「新生」也可寫 freshmen、first-year students。常見扣分：New students are impossible to remember …（意思變成「新生不可能被記住」，語意錯誤）；It is impossible of new students（of 用於形容人的個性）。其他寫法：It is almost impossible for freshmen to …；New students can't possibly remember …。
T2-24 It is said that the egg pancakes at this breakfast shop are the best in the city, so there is a long line outside every morning.關鍵結構：「據說」用 It is said that + 子句；後半用 so 連接結果。「大排長龍」用 there is a long line 或 people line up。常見扣分：It said that …（漏 is，意思變成「它說」）；a long dragon（成語直譯）；the best eaten、the most delicious in the whole city's 之類的結構錯誤；兩句之間只用逗號而沒有 so。其他寫法：The egg pancakes at this breakfast shop are said to be the best in town, so people line up outside every morning.／People say that … 也正確。蛋餅可寫 egg pancakes、egg crepes，或 dan bing (egg pancakes)。
T2-25 It took us nearly a month to perfect our dance for the school anniversary.關鍵結構：It took + 人 + 時間 + to V；已經發生的事用過去式 took。「將近」用 nearly 或 almost，放在 a month 前面。常見扣分：It spent us …（spend 主詞只能是人）；It takes us …（時態錯）；took us near a month（near 是「靠近」，不是「將近」）。其他寫法：It took us almost a month to …；換主詞：We spent nearly a month practicing our dance until it was perfect.
T2-26 It takes time and patience to learn a new language, so don't expect to make progress overnight.關鍵結構：「需要…才能」用 It takes + 名詞 + to V；一般事實用現在式 takes。「一夜之間」用副詞 overnight。常見扣分：Learn a new language needs …（原形動詞當主詞）；don't expect progress in one night 意思可懂但不自然；make a progress（progress 不可數，不加 a）。其他寫法：Learning a new language takes time and patience, so you shouldn't expect to improve overnight.／… so don't expect overnight progress.
T2-27 It was not until I moved out that I understood how much housework my parents did every day.關鍵結構：It was not until + 子句 + that + S + V，that 後面用正常語序；後半是名詞子句 how much housework + S + V，也是正常語序。常見扣分：It was until I moved out that …（漏 not）；Until I moved out, I understood …（意思相反）；how much housework did my parents do（名詞子句誤用疑問句語序）。其他寫法：I didn't understand how much housework my parents did every day until I moved out.／Not until I moved out did I understand …（倒裝，見句型 18）。
T2-28 It was a talk given by a graduate who came back to our school that made me decide to study nursing in college.關鍵結構：強調「讓我決定」的原因是那場分享：It was + 名詞片語 + that + made …；make + O + 原形 V。「分享」在這裡指演講或經驗談，用 talk、speech、story about her experience，不要直譯成 sharing。常見扣分：漏掉 that（It was a talk … made me decide）；let me decide（let 是允許，不是促使）；made me to decide；It is（事情已發生，要用 was）。其他寫法：It was a former student's talk at our school that inspired me to study nursing in college.／It was a talk by an alumna that made me decide to major in nursing.
T2-29 It was such a funny video that I kept laughing even after watching it five times.關鍵結構：空格後有名詞 video，所以用 such + a + adj. + 單數名詞 + that。常見扣分：so a funny video；such funny video（可數單數名詞漏 a）；such a fun video 意思偏「好玩」而非「好笑」，語意略有偏差。其他寫法：換句首可用 so：The video was so funny that I kept laughing …；such a hilarious video。
T2-30 The backpack was too heavy for my sister to carry, so I carried it to the classroom for her.關鍵結構：too + adj. + for 人 + to V，本身已含「背不動」的否定；carry 的受詞就是主詞 backpack，所以後面不再加 it。常見扣分：too heavy for my sister to carry it（重複受詞）；too heavy that my sister couldn't carry it（too 不接 that）；too heavy for my sister not to carry（雙重否定，意思相反）。其他寫法：The backpack was so heavy that my sister couldn't carry it, so I helped her carry it to the classroom.／… so I took it to the classroom for her.
T2-31 I always close the door gently when I come home late at night so as not to wake up my family.關鍵結構：否定目的「為了不…」用 so as not to V 或 in order not to V，not 放在 to 前面。常見扣分：for not waking up my family；in order to not wake …（考場建議用標準語序）；so as not to wake up my family's sleep（中文直譯，多餘）。其他寫法：in order not to wake my family；so that I won't wake up my family；so that my family won't be woken up。
T2-32 To do well in the inter-school tournament, members of the basketball team arrive at school at six every morning to practice.關鍵結構：句首 To V …, 表「為了」；句尾 to practice 是「到學校（為了）練球」，也是表目的的不定詞。比賽的「拿到好成績」用 do well、get good results，不用考試的 get good grades。習慣性動作用現在式。常見扣分：For getting good results、For win the game；arrive school（漏 at）；every morning at six o'clock in the morning（重複）；主詞與動詞不一致（the team members arrives）。其他寫法：In order to get good results in the inter-school competition, the basketball players come to school at 6 a.m. every day to practice.
T2-33 The coach makes us run around the track three times before every practice.關鍵結構：「要（我們）做…」是要求、強制，用 make + O + 原形 V；也可用 has us run（安排、叫）。教練的固定要求用現在式 makes。常見扣分：makes us to run、makes us running；lets us run（let 是允許，語意錯誤）；make us run（主詞 the coach 是第三人稱單數，漏 s）。其他寫法：has us run；asks us to run；gets us to run。
T2-34 I heard someone calling my name loudly in the hallway, and when I turned around, I saw that it was my best friend from junior high.關鍵結構：「聽到有人正在叫」用 hear + O + V-ing；「轉過頭才發現」用 when 子句加主要子句。「國中最好的朋友」用 my best friend from junior high，介系詞片語放在名詞後面。常見扣分：heard someone called my name（called 變成第二個過去式動詞）；heard someone to call；兩個完整句只用逗號連接。其他寫法：I heard my name called loudly in the hallway. When I turned around, I found it was my best friend from junior high school.（受詞是被叫的名字，用 p.p.）
T2-35 Only by practicing regularly can you write a clear and fluent essay on test day.關鍵結構：Only + 副詞片語 放句首，主要子句倒裝：情態助動詞 can 移到主詞 you 前面。「透過」用 by + V-ing 或 through + 名詞。常見扣分：Only by practicing regularly you can write …（未倒裝）；Only by practice regularly（by 後接原形）；can you wrote。其他寫法：Only through regular practice can you write …；不倒裝的安全寫法：You can write a clear and fluent essay on test day only if you practice regularly.
T2-36 Not until I went abroad as an exchange student did I truly realize how precious the food from my hometown was.關鍵結構：Not until + 子句（正常語序）+ did + S + 原形 V；句尾名詞子句 how precious + 主詞 + was 用正常語序。過去的經驗，主要動詞時態靠 did 表現。常見扣分：did I truly realized；Not until did I go abroad …（倒錯子句）；how precious was the food（名詞子句誤用疑問句語序）；「交換學生」寫成 change student。其他寫法：It was not until I became an exchange student overseas that I truly appreciated the food back home.／I didn't truly realize how precious my hometown food was until I went abroad as an exchange student.
T2-37 After I moved to Taipei, it took me several months to get used to taking crowded MRT trains every day.關鍵結構：「習慣（的過程）」用 get used to + V-ing，to 是介系詞。前面的 it took me … to 的 to 才是不定詞，接原形 get。常見扣分：get used to take（最常見錯誤）；used to take（意思變成「過去常常搭」）。另外，「花幾個月才習慣」強調由不習慣到習慣的變化，用 get used to 比 be used to（已經處於習慣的狀態）更貼切。其他寫法：get used to riding；become accustomed to taking；get used to squeezing onto（擠上車）。
T2-38 Besides memorizing new words every day, Mei also improves her listening skills by watching TV shows in English.關鍵結構：Besides + V-ing（除了…還）、by + V-ing（藉由），兩個介系詞後面都接動名詞；主詞 Mei 是第三人稱單數，用 improves。常見扣分：Besides memorize、by watch；Except memorizing（except 是「除了…以外都不」，語意不同）；「加強聽力」寫成 add her listening、strong her listening（strong 是形容詞，不能當動詞）。其他寫法：In addition to memorizing vocabulary every day, Mei also watches English-language TV series to improve her listening.
T2-39 I was satisfied with my midterm results, but the math questions were really confusing.關鍵結構：第一格描述「我」的感受用 -ed，並搭配介系詞 with；第二格描述「題目」的性質用 -ing。常見扣分：satisfying（變成「我很令人滿意」）；satisfied about、satisfied to my results（介系詞錯）；the math questions were really confused（題目不會「感到困惑」）。其他寫法：pleased with、happy with；really puzzling、really tricky。
T2-40 The marine biologist's talk was not boring at all; in fact, after hearing it, many students became interested in ocean conservation.關鍵結構：演講本身「令人無聊」用 boring；同學「感到有興趣」用 became interested in。分號連接兩個意思相關的完整句，後面接 in fact。常見扣分：was not bored at all（演講不會感到無聊）；interesting in；「產生了興趣」直譯成 produced interest；… at all, in fact, …（逗號連接兩個完整句）。其他寫法：The talk by the marine biologist wasn't boring at all. In fact, many students got interested in protecting the ocean after listening to it.／… many students developed an interest in marine conservation.
