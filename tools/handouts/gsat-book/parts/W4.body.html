W4　同一段落三個等級示範

這一章把「同一段內容」寫成三個等級，讓你親眼看到：從 10 分走到 15 分、再從 15 分走到 19 分，到底改了什麼。你會發現，高分版不是比較長而已，也不是字比較難，而是每一句都在做事：交代資訊、提供畫面、說明原因、推進到下一句。

一、先看懂：三個等級差在哪裡

從近年統計看，作文拿到 15 分以上的考生約只有 5%–8%，17 分以上更只有 1%–2%（113 年 1.8%、114 年 1.1%、115 年 1.0%）。13 分以上也只有 15%–20%。不少程度不錯的考生卡在 13–14 分，原因通常不是文法太差，而是內容籠統、句子單調、段落沒有中心。換句話說，只要學會「升級」的方法，你就能跟大多數人拉開差距。

作文看四個面向（本站概述）：內容、組織、文法句構、字彙拼字。

  • 內容：有沒有回答題目的每一項要求？細節是否具體、可信、和主旨相關？
  • 組織：段落有沒有中心？句子的順序合不合理？前後怎麼連起來？
  • 文法句構：錯誤多不多？句型有沒有變化、是否用得自然？
  • 字彙拼字：用字精不精準？搭配對不對？拼字有沒有錯？
面向基礎（約 10–12 分）穩健（約 14–16 分）高分（約 18–20 分）
內容有回答但很籠統，常用 very、a lot、many things 帶過；幾乎沒有例子回答完整，有一兩個具體細節（時間、數字、人物）每個要點都有能想像的畫面，並交代原因或結果；細節經過挑選，全部服務主旨
組織一句接一句排列，缺主題句或收尾句；常把第二段該寫的看法寫進第一段有主題句、順序清楚；轉折詞正確但偏常見（However、Therefore）段落有明確中心；靠句意和指涉詞（this、that night）自然銜接，轉折詞少而準；收尾句能呼應或替下一段鋪路
文法句構以簡單句為主；常見主詞動詞不一致、時態跳動、兩句用逗號硬接錯誤很少；會用 when、because、although、so … that幾乎無錯；句子長短交錯，自然用到分詞構句、名詞子句、同位語，但不為炫技而硬塞
字彙拼字基本字為主，同一個字重複出現用字正確，偶有好的搭配動詞精準、搭配道地（packed with commuters、sign up for），不堆砌艱深字

學測作文是整篇評分。本章說的「約幾分程度」，意思是「如果整篇都維持這個水準，大約落在這個分數區間」，不是單一段落的實際給分。

這一章的正確讀法：每個示範先只讀「題目」和「基礎版」，用手遮住下面，自己在草稿紙上試著升級，寫完再往下看穩健版和高分版。先自己動手，再看示範，效果差很多。

二、示範一　看圖描述段（看圖題第一段）

題目與本段任務

題目（本站自擬）：下面是某個平日傍晚捷運車廂裡的情景。請寫一篇英文作文，文分兩段：第一段描述圖片中的人、事、物以及正在發生的事；第二段說明你對這個景象的看法。（本示範只寫第一段）

圖片描述：下班下課時段的捷運車廂，座位全滿，多數乘客低頭看手機，有人戴著耳機閉眼睡著。門邊一位拄著拐杖、另一手提著一袋菜的老太太站著，車廂晃動時站不太穩。離她幾步遠的博愛座上，一位穿高中制服的男生專心打手機遊戲，沒有抬頭。車廂另一頭，一位穿運動服的女學生拿下耳機、站起來，向老太太揮手請她過去坐。

本段必答：時間與地點；車廂整體狀況；主要人物在做什麼；正在發生的事件（老太太站著、誰沒注意、誰讓座）。看法留到第二段。

基礎版（約 10–12 分程度，79 字）

This picture is about a MRT. There are many people in the MRT, and most of them are playing their phones. An old woman is standing near the door. She has a lot of things and she looks very tired. A boy sit on the priority seat, he is playing games. He don't see the old woman. Then a girl stands up and wants to give her seat to the old woman. I think the girl is very kind.

基礎版裡的錯誤

誤a MRT（MRT 開頭念 /ɛm/，是母音開頭）

正an MRT car（車廂要說 car 或 train；MRT 單獨用是指整個捷運系統）

誤playing their phones

正looking at their phones 或 playing games on their phones

誤A boy sit on the priority seat, he is playing games.（主詞動詞不一致＋逗號硬接兩句）

正A boy sitting in a priority seat is playing games.

誤He don't see the old woman.

正He does not notice the old woman.

穩健版（約 14–16 分程度，118 字）

The picture shows a crowded MRT car in the evening, when many people are on their way home from work or school. All the seats are taken, and most passengers are looking down at their phones. Near the door, an elderly woman with a cane is holding a heavy bag of vegetables. She is trying hard to keep her balance as the train moves. However, a high school boy sitting in a priority seat does not notice her because he is busy playing a game on his phone. At the other end of the car, a girl in gym clothes takes off her earphones and stands up. She waves at the old woman and offers her the seat.

高分版（約 18–20 分程度，142 字）

It is rush hour on a weekday evening, and the MRT car in the picture is packed with commuters heading home. Every seat is taken. Most passengers are staring at their phones, while a few have dozed off with their earphones still in. By the door, an elderly woman leans on a cane, a bag of groceries in her other hand, struggling to stay on her feet each time the train sways. Just a few steps away, a high school boy in a priority seat is so absorbed in his mobile game that he never looks up. The only person who seems to notice her is a girl in gym clothes at the far end of the car. Pulling out her earphones, she rises from her seat and waves the woman over. The contrast between the two teenagers could hardly be sharper.

同一個點，三種寫法（並排對照）

位置基礎穩健高分
開頭This picture is about a MRT.The picture shows a crowded MRT car in the evening, when …It is rush hour on a weekday evening, and the MRT car … is packed with commuters heading home.
老太太She has a lot of things and she looks very tired.… is holding a heavy bag of vegetables. She is trying hard to keep her balance …… leans on a cane, a bag of groceries in her other hand, struggling to stay on her feet each time the train sways.
男生He don't see the old woman.… does not notice her because he is busy playing a game …Just a few steps away, … is so absorbed in his mobile game that he never looks up.
結尾I think the girl is very kind.（看法跑錯段）（沒有收尾句，停在動作上）The contrast between the two teenagers could hardly be sharper.（替第二段鋪路）

升級清單：基礎 → 穩健

  • 內容：補上時間（傍晚、下班下課）、車廂狀況（座位全滿）、人物特徵（拐杖、一袋菜）；男生沒注意到老太太，補上原因（忙著打遊戲）。
  • 組織：開頭不再寫空洞的 This picture is about，直接交代場景；依空間順序寫：門邊 → 博愛座 → 車廂另一頭；用 However 帶出「她站不穩，男生卻沒注意」的反差；刪掉 I think the girl is very kind，因為看法屬於第二段。
  • 句構：改掉所有錯誤；加入關係副詞 when、分詞片語 sitting in a priority seat、原因子句 because …、時間子句 as the train moves。
  • 用字：old woman → elderly woman；has a lot of things → is holding a heavy bag of vegetables；looks very tired → is trying hard to keep her balance（寫看得到的動作，不寫空泛形容詞）。

升級清單：穩健 → 高分

  • 內容：加入有畫面的細節：dozed off with their earphones still in、each time the train sways；用 Just a few steps away 點出「離得很近卻沒看見」，讓對比更強，閱卷者讀一次就記得。
  • 組織：第一句同時交代時間、地點、氣氛；最後加收尾句 The contrast … could hardly be sharper.，把兩個青少年的反差當成第二段的起點，兩段自然接上。
  • 句構：獨立分詞構句 a bag of groceries in her other hand；分詞片語 struggling to …；so absorbed … that …；強調用的 The only person who …；句首分詞構句 Pulling out her earphones。長句之後接一個短句（Every seat is taken.），節奏有變化。
  • 用字：crowded → packed with commuters；trying hard to keep her balance → struggling to stay on her feet；offers her the seat → waves the woman over（更具體的動作）。注意：沒有一個字超出高中範圍。

看圖描述段的時態：全段統一用現在式與現在進行式，好像你正在看著圖說話。高分版用了大量簡單現在式（leans、rises、waves），讀起來比一路 is V-ing 更俐落，兩種都可以，但不要混進過去式。

三、示範二　經驗段（兩段式第二段）

題目與本段任務

題目（本站自擬）：很多人都有「原本覺得做不到，後來卻做到了」的經驗。請寫一篇英文作文，文分兩段：第一段說明這件事是什麼、你當初為什麼覺得做不到；第二段描述你如何克服困難，以及這次經驗帶給你的改變。（本示範只寫第二段）

第一段已交代：高一加入熱音社，被學長姐安排在校慶音樂會上彈吉他，但我從沒上台表演過，非常怯場。

本段必答：怎麼克服（方法、過程）；結果如何；帶給你什麼改變（要具體）。

基礎版（約 10–12 分程度，69 字）

I practiced very hard every day. Before the show, I was very nervous and can't sleep. My friends helped me a lot. They told me don't worry. On the day of the show, I played the guitar on the stage. I made some mistakes, but everyone clapped. I was very happy. From this experience, I learned that practice makes perfect. I think everything is possible if we work hard.

基礎版裡的錯誤與問題

誤I was very nervous and can't sleep.（時態跳回現在）

正I was so nervous that I couldn't sleep.

誤They told me don't worry.

正They told me not to worry.

另外，practice makes perfect 是格言式收尾，而且和內容矛盾：你明明還是出錯了，怎麼會是 perfect？收尾必須和自己的經驗吻合。

穩健版（約 14–16 分程度，108 字）

To get ready for the show, I practiced for an hour every day after school for two months. At first, my fingers hurt and I kept forgetting the chords, but my club members encouraged me and pointed out my mistakes. The night before the performance, I was so nervous that I could hardly sleep. When I finally walked onto the stage, my hands were shaking. Although I missed a few notes in the middle of the song, the audience still cheered for us. This experience taught me that I am braver than I thought. Now I am more willing to try new things, even if I might fail.

高分版（約 18–20 分程度，140 字）

For the next two months, I spent an hour in the club room every day after school, practicing until my fingertips were sore. What helped most, however, was not the extra practice but a tip from our club leader: record every rehearsal on my phone and watch it on the bus home. Seeing my mistakes from the audience's point of view made them far less frightening. Even so, when the curtain opened at our school's anniversary concert, my hands were trembling. I missed two chords in the second verse, yet no one seemed to notice, and the cheers at the end were louder than I had ever imagined. Since that night, I no longer treat nervousness as a reason to say no. Last semester, I even signed up for the debate team, something the old me would never have done.

同一個點，三種寫法（並排對照）

位置基礎穩健高分
怎麼練I practiced very hard every day.… for an hour every day after school for two months.… practicing until my fingertips were sore.
誰幫忙My friends helped me a lot.… encouraged me and pointed out my mistakes.… a tip from our club leader: record every rehearsal …
結果… everyone clapped. I was very happy.… the audience still cheered for us.… the cheers at the end were louder than I had ever imagined.
改變… practice makes perfect.Now I am more willing to try new things …Last semester, I even signed up for the debate team …（用行動證明）

升級清單：基礎 → 穩健

  • 內容：very hard 換成可量化的「每天放學一小時、連續兩個月」；寫出真正的困難（手指痛、忘和弦）；朋友「怎麼」幫忙（幫我找錯），取代 helped me a lot。
  • 組織：嚴格照時間順序：準備 → 前一晚 → 上台 → 結果 → 改變；收尾從空泛格言改成「我自己的改變」。
  • 句構：改正 can't 和 told me don't worry；加入 so … that、When …、Although …，讓因果和讓步關係清楚。
  • 用字：刪掉三個 very；I was very happy 改成寫觀眾的反應 cheered for us，讓讀者自己感受到開心。

升級清單：穩健 → 高分

  • 內容：加入一個「關鍵方法」（錄影、在公車上看），讓克服困難有可信的原因，這是全段最讓人記得的細節；改變用「之後的行動」（報名辯論隊）證明，而不是只說 more willing。
  • 組織：What helped most … 一句成為全段中心，前面是過程，後面是效果；最後兩句是「領悟 → 證據」，收得很穩。
  • 句構：名詞子句當主詞 What helped most；not A but B；動名詞主詞 Seeing my mistakes …；過去完成式 than I had ever imagined；句尾同位語 something the old me would never have done。
  • 用字：fingers hurt → fingertips were sore；shaking → trembling；I am braver than I thought → I no longer treat nervousness as a reason to say no（把抽象感想變成具體的行為準則）。

經驗段的時態地圖：經過用過去式；比經過更早的事用過去完成式（had ever imagined）；「到現在的改變」用現在式或現在完成式（Since that night, I no longer …）。時態跟著時間走，閱卷者一眼就看得出你掌握得很穩。

四、示範三　提案信段（書信題第一段）

題目與本段任務

題目（本站自擬）：你的學校福利社每天賣出大量瓶裝飲料，教室外的垃圾桶常被塑膠瓶塞滿。請寫一封英文信給校長（信末署名 Alex），文分兩段：第一段說明問題並提出你的建議；第二段說明這些建議的好處，以及學生可以如何配合。（本示範只寫第一段）

本段必答：你是誰、為什麼寫信；問題是什麼（要有場景）；你的具體建議。好處與配合方式留到第二段。

基礎版（約 10–12 分程度，67 字）

Dear Principal,

I am a student in our school. I want to tell you a problem. Our school have too many plastic bottles. Every day many students buy drinks in the school store. After they drink, they throw the bottles away. The trash cans are always full. It is very bad for the environment. So I think our school should put water fountains, students can bring their own bottles.

基礎版裡的錯誤

誤I want to tell you a problem.

正I am writing to tell you about a problem.（tell 某人「關於」某事要加 about）

誤Our school have too many plastic bottles.

正Our school has a serious plastic bottle problem.

誤… should put water fountains, students can bring …（put 用字不精準，且逗號硬接兩句）

正… should install more water fountains so that students can bring …

穩健版（約 14–16 分程度，95 字）

Dear Principal,

My name is Alex, and I am an eleventh grader in Class 205. I am writing to tell you about a problem I see every day on campus. During lunch break, many students buy bottled drinks at the school store, and by the afternoon, the trash cans near the classrooms are full of plastic bottles. Some bottles even fall on the floor. This not only makes our campus look dirty but also wastes a lot of plastic. Therefore, I suggest that the school install more water fountains and encourage students to bring their own bottles.

高分版（約 18–20 分程度，141 字）

Dear Principal,

I am Alex, an eleventh grader in Class 205, and I am writing to you about something I walk past every day on my way back from lunch: the trash cans outside our classrooms, overflowing with plastic bottles. Most of these bottles come from the school store, where a cold drink is the easiest choice after P.E. class. On hot afternoons, the cans fill up long before the students on cleaning duty can empty them, and loose bottles end up rolling across the hallway floor. This is not only an ugly sight but also a waste that we could easily avoid. I would therefore like to propose two simple changes: adding a second water refill station on each floor, since the current one always has a long line at lunchtime, and starting a monthly "Bring Your Own Bottle" challenge between classes.

同一個點，三種寫法（並排對照）

位置基礎穩健高分
自我介紹I am a student in our school.… an eleventh grader in Class 205.用同位語交代身分，並和寫信目的放進同一句
問題The trash cans are always full.… by the afternoon, the trash cans near the classrooms are full of plastic bottles.… fill up long before the students on cleaning duty can empty them …
原因（沒有）（只說學生買飲料）… where a cold drink is the easiest choice after P.E. class.
建議… should put water fountains …… suggest that the school install more water fountains …兩個具體建議，各附一個可行的理由

升級清單：基礎 → 穩健

  • 內容：自我介紹具體到年級和班級；問題有時間（午休、下午）和地點（教室旁）；說明為什麼這是問題（校園髒亂、浪費塑膠）。
  • 組織：信件第一句先說寫信目的（I am writing to …）；按照「問題 → 影響 → 建議」排列；用 Therefore 引出建議。
  • 句構：not only … but also …；suggest that 後接原形動詞 install；改正 have、tell you a problem、逗號硬接。
  • 用字：put → install；very bad for the environment → wastes a lot of plastic（具體說出壞在哪裡）。

升級清單：穩健 → 高分

  • 內容：說出問題的根源（體育課後冷飲是最方便的選擇），建議才有說服力；建議從一個籠統的方向變成兩個具體做法，而且每個都附理由（現有飲水機大排長龍），讓校長看得出你真的觀察過。
  • 組織：第一句用冒號直接帶出問題畫面，一句完成「我是誰＋為什麼寫信＋問題是什麼」；建議放在段尾，下一段談「好處」就能直接接上。
  • 句構：同位語 an eleventh grader in Class 205；冒號引出說明；關係副詞 where …；分詞 overflowing with …；end up V-ing；平行結構 adding … and starting …。
  • 用字：suggest → propose（提案信更貼切）；look dirty → an ugly sight；I would like to … 比 I want to … 更有禮貌，適合寫給校長。

書信題的語氣：寫給師長或機關，用 I am writing to …、I would like to …、I would appreciate it if … 這類客氣說法；寫給朋友就輕鬆自然。不論寫給誰，都不要用 How are you? I am fine. 這種和題目無關的寒暄浪費字數。信末署名一定照題目指定的名字。

五、三個示範的共同規律：升級五步

回頭看三個示範，升級的順序其實都一樣。練習 C 請照下面五步做，不要一開始就想著「放一個漂亮句型」。

  1. 先改錯：主詞動詞一致、時態、冠詞、單複數、逗號硬接兩句。錯誤不改，其他升級都會被扣回去。
  2. 補內容：看到 very、a lot、many things、good、bad，就問自己「多少？什麼樣子？為什麼？」，用答案取代它。
  3. 排順序：主題句 → 支持句（依時間、空間或重要性）→ 收尾句；刪掉不屬於這一段的句子。
  4. 變句型：每段用 2–3 個複句、分詞構句或名詞子句就夠，句子長短交錯。不要每句都升級。
  5. 換用字：把 make、get、have、do 換成更精準的動詞或搭配，但只用你確定用法的字。
常見的基礎寫法升級方向本章出現過的高分寫法
This picture is about …直接交代時間、地點、氣氛It is rush hour on a weekday evening, and …
She looks very tired.寫看得見的動作struggling to stay on her feet each time the train sways
I was very happy.寫別人的反應或結果the cheers at the end were louder than I had ever imagined
My friends helped me a lot.寫出「怎麼」幫a tip from our club leader: record every rehearsal …
I learned that practice makes perfect.用之後的行動證明改變Last semester, I even signed up for the debate team …
It is very bad for the environment.說清楚壞在哪裡、原因是什麼a waste that we could easily avoid＋具體原因

五種「假升級」，反而會被扣分

  • 堆砌艱深字：把 many 換成 a plethora of，搭配不對、語氣怪，字彙分不升反降。
  • 套語開頭或格言收尾：Every coin has two sides.、As the saying goes, …，閱卷者一看就知道是背的，內容分不會加。
  • 句子太長而出錯：硬把三句併成一句，結果少了動詞或連接詞。寧可兩個正確的中長句。
  • 加了和題目無關的細節：細節多不等於內容好，每個細節都要服務本段任務。
  • 把下一段的內容提前寫：描述段寫成看法、問題段寫成好處，會造成兩段份量失衡、組織分被扣。

六、練習

練習 A　判斷等級（6 題）

判斷每段文字最接近哪個等級（基礎／穩健／高分），並寫出一個判斷理由。

W4-01Yesterday I went to the library. There was many students. I studied very hard there and I felt very tired.
W4-02The library was very crowded, so I had to share a table with other students. Although it was a little noisy, I still finished my history notes before it closed.
W4-03By seven o'clock, every table in the library was taken, and the only empty chair I could find was next to a boy quietly eating a rice ball behind his textbook. Squeezed between him and the window, I managed to finish two chapters of history notes before the closing announcement came on.
W4-04In my opinion, students should be allowed to wear casual clothes to school once a week. First, it would let us express our personalities. Second, comfortable clothes can help us focus, especially on hot days.
W4-05Every coin has two sides. Phones have good points and bad points. We should use phones in a correct way.
W4-06Letting students wear their own clothes once a week would cost the school nothing, yet it could change how we feel on a Friday morning. On the first casual Friday our class tried, even the boy who usually sleeps through math showed up early, proud of the vintage jacket he had found at a flea market.

練習 B　單句升級（6 題）

依照每題的情境與指定的升級方向，把基礎句改寫成高分句。每題都要先確定句子沒有文法錯誤。

W4-07【內容具體化】情境：看圖描述颱風過後的街道。把下面的句子改成「讀者看得到畫面」的一句，至少寫出兩樣具體的東西。

There are many things on the street.

W4-08【合併句子】情境：經驗段，放學回家。把三句合成一句，至少用一個分詞或 when 子句，並加一個看得出狗狗心情的細節。

I opened the door. I saw my dog. He was waiting for me.

W4-09【用結果代替形容詞】情境：經驗段，一次考試。用 so … that 或 such … that 寫成一句，用一個「看得見的行為」取代 sad。

The test was very hard. I was sad.

W4-10【精準用字】情境：介紹家人。改寫下句，不准用 very、good、a lot，並加一個具體例子。

My grandmother is very good at cooking, and she helps me a lot.

W4-11【讓步轉折＋結果】情境：班級淨灘活動。用 Although、Even though 或 Despite 把兩句連成一句，並補上活動的結果。

Our class planned to clean the beach. It rained all morning.

W4-12【收尾句升級】情境：經驗段結尾，主題是「第一次一個人搭高鐵到臺南看外婆」。把格言式收尾改成和自己有關的具體改變。

From this experience, I learned that we should be independent.
