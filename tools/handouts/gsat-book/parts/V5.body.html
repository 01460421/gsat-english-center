V5　易混淆字

易混淆字就是「兩個都背過，放在一起卻不敢選」的字。學測不會直接問 adapt 和 adopt 差在哪裡，而是把它們一起放進選項，或者在翻譯和作文裡等你自己寫錯。這個單元把最常混的 80 組字分成五類，每組給一行差別和各一個例句；每一類後面有 8 題二選一，一共 40 題。

一、易混淆字怎麼考、怎麼分

1. 考在哪裡

大題怎麼考例子
詞彙題、綜合測驗四個選項裡放拼字相近或字義相近的字adapt／adopt、rise／raise
文意選填、混合題填空同一個字族，要填對詞性和字形affect／effect、succeed／success
閱讀測驗看錯一個字，整句的意思就走樣sensible／sensitive、invaluable
中譯英、英文作文中文同一個詞，英文要分；拼錯、用錯都會扣分借 borrow／lend、花 spend／cost／take

2. 三個檢查，照順序做

  1. 看詞性：空格要名詞、動詞、形容詞還是副詞？很多組在這一步就分出來了（affect 是動詞、effect 是名詞；hard 和 hardly 是兩個不同的副詞）。
  2. 看句型：後面有沒有受詞（rise 沒有、raise 有）？接哪一個介系詞（result in／result from）？主詞是人還是物（spend／cost）？
  3. 看字義和搭配：前兩步分不出來，才比意思，並且看它習慣和誰一起出現（economic growth、an economical car）。

3. 表格怎麼讀、怎麼背

  • 每一列是一組：左邊是字和意思，右邊第一行是分法，下面是例句，粗體字就是那一組的字。三態容易寫錯的不規則動詞，三態直接寫在字的後面。
  • 兩個字要一起背，各記一個最短的搭配（adapt to、adopt a policy），不要只背中文。
  • 讀完一類，把右邊第一行的分法遮起來，看著例句說得出差別，才算會了。

一天讀一類（14 到 20 組），當天做完那一類的 8 題；答錯的組在表格的編號上做記號，考前只看有記號的。

二、拼字相近（第 01–20 組）

這一類的字意思多半差很遠，只是長得像。考場上的做法是：選項裡出現長得像的字，先一個字母一個字母比出不同的地方，再用表格裡的分法確認意思。

字根相同、字首不同的字（-sume、-tribute、-serve）最常一起出現在詞彙題的選項裡。記住字首的意思比硬背中文可靠，字首字根的整理見 V2。

組字與意思怎麼分與例句
01adapt 適應；改編
adopt 採用；領養		a 想 adjust（調整自己），常接 to；o 想 option（選一個來用），直接接受詞。
Desert plants have adapted to long dry seasons.
The city adopted a new recycling policy.
02abroad 在國外、到國外
aboard 在（船、飛機、車）上		abroad 裡有 road，出國；aboard 裡有 board，登機上船。abroad 是副詞：go abroad，不寫 go to abroad。
Many graduates plan to study abroad.
All the passengers are now aboard the plane.
03accept 接受
except 除了…之外
expect 預期；期待		accept 和 expect 是動詞，except 是介系詞（ex- 表示排除）。expect 是「認為會發生」，常接 to V。
She accepted the invitation at once.
The museum is open every day except Monday.
We expect the typhoon to arrive tonight.
04access 使用（權）；通道；存取
assess 評估
excess 過量；過多的		have access to＋名詞；assess 的受詞是損失、風險、能力；excess 來自 exceed（超過）。
Every student has access to the online library.
Experts are assessing the damage caused by the flood.
Excess sugar is stored in the body as fat.
05altitude 高度、海拔
attitude 態度
aptitude 天分、才能		alt- 是「高」；attitude 接 toward 或 to；aptitude 接 for。
The plane is flying at an altitude of 10,000 meters.
He has a positive attitude toward failure.
She showed an aptitude for languages at an early age.
06assume 假定；承擔
consume 消耗；吃、喝
resume 重新開始		字根 -sume 是「拿」：as-（拿來當真）、con-（全部拿光）、re-（再拿起來）。
I assumed the shop was open, but it was closed.
This old air conditioner consumes a lot of electricity.
Classes will resume after the typhoon passes.
07attribute A to B 把 A 歸因於 B
contribute to 貢獻；促成
distribute 分發、分配		字根 -tribute 是「給」：at-（歸給某個原因）、con-（一起給）、dis-（分開給）。
She attributes her success to her parents' support.
Heavy traffic contributes to air pollution.
Volunteers distributed food to the flood victims.
08conserve 節約；保育
preserve 保存、維護
reserve 預訂；保留		conserve 是省著用（水、能源）；preserve 是不讓它變壞或消失（食物、古蹟、傳統）；reserve 是先留起來（座位、房間）。
Taking shorter showers helps conserve water.
People once used salt to preserve meat.
We reserved two rooms at the hotel.
09contact 聯絡；接觸
contract 合約
contrast 對比、對照		contact 當動詞直接接人，不加 with；in contrast to「和…成對比」。
Please contact us by email.
The singer signed a three-year contract.
In contrast to the noisy city, the village is peaceful.
10costume 戲服、特殊場合的服裝
custom 習俗
customs 海關		costume 是穿在身上的；custom 是一群人的習慣；固定加 s 的 customs 是「海關」。
The children wore animal costumes in the school play.
Giving red envelopes is a Lunar New Year custom.
It took us an hour to get through customs.
11daily 每天的
dairy 乳製品（的）
diary 日記		dairy 和 diary 只差 a、i 的順序：日記寫的是 I（我），所以 diary 的 i 在前面。
Jogging is part of her daily routine.
Milk and cheese are dairy products.
He has kept a diary since junior high school.
12desert 沙漠；拋棄
dessert 甜點		甜點想多吃一份，所以多一個 s。「沙漠」重音在前；dessert 和動詞 desert（拋棄）重音在後。
Few plants can survive in the desert.
We had mango ice cream for dessert.
13emigrate 移居外國（移出）
immigrate （從外國）移入
migrate 遷徙、遷移		e- 是「出」，常接 from；im- 是「入」，常接 to；migrate 指候鳥、動物或人口的大規模移動。
His grandparents emigrated from Italy in the 1950s.
Many engineers immigrated to Canada for better jobs.
These birds migrate south every winter.
14later 後來；較晚
latter （兩者中的）後者		later 是 late 的比較級，講時間；latter 講順序，和 former（前者）相對。
She called back ten minutes later.
Between tea and coffee, I prefer the latter.
15loose 鬆的、寬鬆的
lose (lost, lost) 失去；輸掉		loose 多一個 o，空間多所以「鬆」，是形容詞，字尾念 [s]；lose 是動詞，字尾念 [z]。
Wear loose clothes when you exercise.
Don't lose your ticket, or you will have to pay again.
16personal 個人的、私人的
personnel 全體人員；人事		personal 是形容詞，重音在第一音節；personnel 是名詞，兩個 n，重音在最後。
Never share your personal information online.
Only medical personnel can enter this area.
17principal 校長；主要的
principle 原則；原理		principal 字尾有 pal（夥伴），是人；principle 和 rule 一樣以 -le 結尾，是規則。
The principal gave a speech at the graduation ceremony.
Honesty is one of her most important principles.
18quiet 安靜的
quite 相當
quit 戒除；辭職		quiet 念兩個音節，是形容詞；quite 字尾的 e 不發音，是副詞，放在形容詞前面；quit 是動詞。
The reading room is quiet enough to study in.
The math exam was quite difficult.
My uncle quit smoking two years ago.
19receipt 收據
recipe 食譜；配方		receipt 來自 receive（收到），p 不發音；recipe 念三個音節。
Keep the receipt in case you want to return the shoes.
This soup recipe needs only five ingredients.
20though 雖然
through 穿過；透過
thorough 徹底的		三個字都是 th…ough：中間沒有東西是 though，夾 r 是 through，夾 or 是 thorough。
Though it was raining, the game went on.
The train goes through a long tunnel.
The police made a thorough search of the building.

三、字義相近（第 21–38 組）

這一類的字中文意思很接近，差別多半不在意思，而在句型：及物還是不及物、受詞是人還是物、後面接什麼。

看到字義相近的選項，先問三件事：

  1. 後面有沒有受詞？（rise／raise、lie／lay、arrive／reach）
  2. 受詞是人、物，還是事？（rob／steal、win／beat、injure／damage）
  3. 後面接 to V、V-ing，還是介系詞？（refuse／reject、prevent／avoid、result in／from）
組字與意思怎麼分與例句
21rise (rose, risen) 上升
raise (raised, raised) 舉起；提高；募（款）
arise (arose, arisen) （問題）發生、出現		後面有受詞用 raise，沒有受詞用 rise；主詞是 problem、question、difficulty 用 arise。
Sea levels are rising faster than expected.
The bakery raised its prices by ten percent.
Several problems arose during the experiment.
22lie (lay, lain) 躺；位於
lay (laid, laid) 放置；下（蛋）
lie (lied, lied) 說謊		有受詞用 lay，沒有受詞用 lie。最容易錯的地方：「躺」的過去式剛好是 lay。
He lay on the grass and watched the clouds.
She laid the baby gently on the bed.
He lied about his age to get the job.
23rob 搶劫（人、地方）
steal (stole, stolen) 偷（東西）		受詞不同：rob＋人或地方＋of＋物；steal＋物＋from＋人或地方。
Two men robbed the jewelry store last night.
Someone stole my bike from the parking lot.
24treat 治療
cure 治好
heal （傷口）癒合		treat 是過程，不一定治得好；cure 是結果；heal 用在傷口、骨折，可以不接受詞。
The doctor treated him for a high fever.
The disease can be cured if it is found early.
The cut on my finger healed within a week.
25injure （在意外中）使受傷
wound （用刀槍等武器）使受傷
damage 損壞		injure 和 wound 的受詞是人或身體部位；damage 的受詞是物品、健康、名譽，不是人。
Three passengers were injured in the bus accident.
The soldier was wounded in the leg.
The earthquake damaged hundreds of houses.
26win (won, won) 贏得
beat (beat, beaten) 打敗		win 的受詞是比賽、獎項；beat（＝defeat）的受詞是對手。
Our school won the national debate contest.
We beat last year's champions by two points.
27remember 記得
remind 提醒；使想起		remember 的主詞是記得的人；remind 的主詞是「讓人想起」的人或物：remind＋人＋of＋事，或＋人＋to V。
I can't remember where I put my keys.
This song reminds me of my childhood.
28prevent 預防、阻止
avoid 避開		prevent 是讓事情不發生：prevent＋受詞＋from V-ing；avoid 是躲開：avoid＋名詞或 V-ing，不接 to V。
The heavy rain prevented us from going hiking.
I left early to avoid the rush-hour traffic.
29discover 發現
invent 發明		discover 的東西本來就存在，只是沒人知道；invent 的東西原本不存在。
Scientists discovered a new kind of frog in the mountains.
No one knows who invented the wheel.
30replace A with B
substitute B for A
都是「用 B 取代 A」		意思相同，受詞順序相反：replace 後面是被換掉的，substitute 後面是換上來的。
We replaced the old bulbs with LED lights.
You can substitute honey for sugar in this recipe.
31consist of 由…組成
be composed of 由…組成		consist 是不及物動詞，只用主動，不寫 is consisted of；compose 表示「由…組成」要用被動。
The team consists of five students.
Water is composed of hydrogen and oxygen.
32result in 造成（＋結果）
result from 起因於（＋原因）		in 後面是結果（＝lead to、cause）；from 後面是原因。
The typhoon resulted in serious flooding.
His illness resulted from a lack of sleep.
33arrive 到達（不及物）
reach 到達；達到（及物）		arrive at＋小地點、arrive in＋城市或國家；reach 直接接受詞。home、here、there 前面不加介系詞。
We arrived at the station just in time.
The climbers reached the top before sunrise.
34accident 意外、事故
incident 事件		accident 是沒有人故意、常造成傷害的意外（車禍、摔傷）；incident 是一起事件，常指不愉快的衝突或糾紛。
He broke his leg in a car accident.
The police are looking into the incident at the night market.
35effective 有效的
efficient 有效率的		effective 是達到想要的結果；efficient 是花的時間、力氣、能源少。
The new drug is effective in reducing pain.
The new engine is efficient and uses very little fuel.
36alone 獨自
lonely 寂寞的		alone 是客觀狀態（只有一個人），不放在名詞前面；lonely 是主觀感受，可以放在名詞前面（a lonely child）。
My grandmother has lived alone for years.
He felt lonely during his first month abroad.
37ensure 確保
assure 向（人）保證
insure 投保		看受詞：ensure＋事或 that 子句；assure＋人＋that 子句；insure＋財物或人。
Please ensure that all the windows are closed.
The doctor assured me that the operation was safe.
The painting is insured for two million dollars.
38refuse 拒絕（做）
reject 拒絕接受、否決
decline 婉拒		refuse 可以接 to V；reject 只接名詞（提議、申請、想法），不接 to V；decline 語氣客氣。
He refused to answer any questions.
The committee rejected our proposal.
She politely declined the invitation.

四、詞性易錯（第 39–52 組）

這一類是同一個字族的不同詞性，或是長得像副詞、其實意思完全不同的字。混合題的填空、翻譯和作文最容易在這裡失分。

先判斷空格的詞性，再選字：

  • 空格前面是冠詞、形容詞、所有格，後面沒有名詞 → 填名詞（a bad effect、some useful advice）。
  • 主詞或助動詞後面缺動詞 → 填動詞（will affect、can't breathe）。
  • 子句裡已經有動詞 → 不能再填動詞（walked past，不是 walked passed）。
組字與意思怎麼分與例句
39affect v. 影響
effect n. 影響；效果		a 開頭是動詞（想 action），直接接受詞，不加 on；名詞用 have an effect on。
Lack of sleep affects your memory.
Lack of sleep has a bad effect on your memory.
40advice n. 建議
advise v. 建議、勸告		-ce 是名詞、-se 是動詞。advice 不可數：a piece of advice，不寫 an advice、advices。
She gave me some useful advice on choosing a major.
The doctor advised him to eat less salt.
41breath n. 呼吸；一口氣
breathe v. 呼吸		字尾加 e 是動詞，發音也不同：breath [brɛθ]、breathe [brið]。同類：bath（名詞）／bathe（動詞）。
Take a deep breath and relax.
It is hard to breathe on high mountains.
42weigh v. 重達；秤重
weight n. 重量；體重		「重幾公斤」用動詞 weigh＋數字；lose weight、gain weight 用名詞。
The baby weighed 3.2 kilograms at birth.
He lost weight by jogging every morning.
43pass v. 經過；通過
past prep./n./adj. 經過；過去（的）		子句裡還沒有動詞，用 passed；已經有動詞（walk、run、drive），用 past。
We passed the post office on our way to school.
We walked past the post office.
44die v. 死
dead adj. 死的
death n. 死亡		die 是一瞬間的動作，不和「for＋一段時間」連用；「死了十年」要用 has been dead for ten years。
Her grandfather died ten years ago.
Her grandfather has been dead for ten years.
His sudden death shocked the whole town.
45succeed v. 成功；繼任
success n. 成功		succeed in＋V-ing，不接 to V；success 中間兩個 c、字尾兩個 s。形容詞見第 59 組。
She succeeded in passing the driving test.
Hard work is the key to success.
46hard adj./adv. 硬的；難的；努力地；猛烈地
hardly adv. 幾乎不		hard 的副詞還是 hard；hardly 是否定詞，句子裡不再加 not。
It rained hard all night.
I could hardly hear him because of the noise.
47late adj./adv. 晚的；遲
lately adv. 最近		late 的副詞還是 late；lately＝recently，常和現在完成式連用。同類：high（高）／highly（非常）、near（近）／nearly（幾乎）。
He stayed up late to finish the report.
I haven't seen her lately.
48most 大多數（的）
almost adv. 幾乎
mostly adv. 大多、主要地		most 直接接名詞，或 most of the＋名詞；almost 是副詞，不直接接名詞，中間要有 all、every、no。
Most students take the bus to school.
Almost all the tickets have been sold.
The visitors are mostly tourists from Japan.
49everyday adj. 日常的
every day 每天（副詞）		寫在一起是形容詞，放在名詞前面；能換成 each day 的就分開寫。
Smartphones are part of everyday life.
She practices the piano every day.
50maybe adv. 也許
may be 可能是（助動詞＋be）		句子已經有動詞，用 maybe（多放句首）；主詞後面缺動詞，用 may be。
Maybe he is stuck in traffic.
He may be stuck in traffic.
51alive 活著的
living 活的；在世的
live [laɪv] 活的；現場的		alive 放在 be 動詞後面，不放名詞前面；living、live 放名詞前面。asleep、alone、afraid、awake 也都不放名詞前面。
The fish was still alive when we bought it.
She is one of the greatest living writers.
The restaurant has live music on weekends.
52emphasize v. 強調
emphasis n. 強調；重點		動詞直接接受詞，不加 on；名詞才用 put emphasis on。同類：discuss、mention、lack 當動詞都不加介系詞。
The coach emphasized the importance of teamwork.
Our school puts great emphasis on reading.

五、形容詞字尾不同，意思不同（第 53–66 組）

同一個字根接上不同的形容詞字尾，意思就不一樣。這一類不能靠詞性分，要看它修飾的名詞：是人、是東西，還是數量。

字尾本身就是線索：-able／-ible 是「可以被…、值得…」（respectable、comprehensible）；-ful 是「充滿…」（respectful）；-ive 是「有…的性質」（imaginative、sensitive）；這幾組裡，字尾是 -ate、-ous 的那個字都用來形容人（considerate、literate、industrious）。

組字與意思怎麼分與例句
53economic 經濟（上）的
economical 省錢的、節約的		修飾 growth、policy、crisis 用 economic；講東西省錢省油、人節儉用 economical。
Tourism is important to the island's economic growth.
Small cars are more economical because they use less gas.
54sensible 明智的、合理的
sensitive 敏感的		sensible 來自 sense（判斷力）；sensitive 常接 to，指容易受影響或容易察覺。
It was sensible of you to bring an umbrella.
A baby's skin is sensitive to sunlight.
55considerable 相當大（多）的
considerate 體貼的		considerable 修飾數量和程度（amount、number、damage）；considerate 形容人。
The project took a considerable amount of time.
It was considerate of him to offer me his seat.
56respectable 正派的、體面的
respectful 恭敬的
respective 各自的		-able 是「值得尊重」，指人正派、體面；-ful 是對別人充滿敬意；respective 後面接複數名詞。
She comes from a respectable family.
The students were quiet and respectful during the ceremony.
After the meeting, they returned to their respective offices.
57historic 有歷史意義的
historical 有關歷史的；史實的		historic 是重要到會寫進歷史；historical 只是和歷史有關。
The first moon landing was a historic event.
The novel is based on historical facts.
58classic 經典的；典型的
classical 古典的		classic 指禁得起時間考驗，也當名詞「經典作品」；classical 指古典音樂，或古希臘羅馬的文化。
This book is a classic children's story.
She prefers classical music to pop music.
59successful 成功的
successive 連續的		succeed 有兩個意思，各有一個形容詞：成功 → successful；接續 → successive。
The charity concert was highly successful.
The team has won five successive games.
60imaginary 虛構的、想像中的
imaginative 富有想像力的
imaginable 想像得到的		imaginary 形容不存在的東西；imaginative 形容人或作品；imaginable 常和 every、最高級連用。
Many young children have imaginary friends.
She is an imaginative designer.
We tried every imaginable method.
61industrial 工業的
industrious 勤勉的		industry 有「工業」和「勤勉」兩個意思：-al 是工業的；-ous 形容人勤勞（＝hardworking）。
The river was polluted by industrial waste.
Ants are industrious insects.
62childish 幼稚的
childlike 天真的、像孩子一樣的		childish 是負面的，批評人不成熟；childlike 是正面的，形容純真。
It was childish of him to hide his sister's phone.
The old painter still has a childlike curiosity.
63favorite 最喜愛的
favorable 有利的；贊同的		favorite 本身已經是「最」，前面不加 most；favorable 修飾 condition、review、impression。
What is your favorite subject?
The movie received favorable reviews.
64literal 字面上的
literary 文學的
literate 識字的		literal 接 meaning、translation；literary 接 work、prize；literate 形容人，反義字是 illiterate。
The literal translation of the phrase sounds strange.
She won a major literary prize.
In the past, only a few villagers were literate.
65comprehensive 全面的、完整的
comprehensible 可以理解的		-ive 取 comprehend「包含」的意思，內容很全；-ible 是「可以被理解」。
The website offers a comprehensive guide to the island.
She used simple words to make her talk comprehensible to children.
66valuable 貴重的；寶貴的
invaluable 無價的、極寶貴的
worthless 沒有價值的		invaluable 的 in- 不是「沒有」，而是貴到無法估價（＝priceless）；「沒價值」是 worthless。
Don't leave valuable items in the classroom.
Her advice was invaluable when I chose a college.
The painting turned out to be a worthless copy.

六、中文同譯、英文不同（第 67–80 組）

中文的一個詞，英文會依照方向、對象、句型分成好幾個字。這一類在中譯英最容易扣分，因為中文題目不會提醒你要分。

翻譯時看到「借、帶、說、看、花、參加、穿、適合、懷疑、除了、忘了帶」，先停一秒，問自己：東西往哪個方向走？主詞是人還是物？後面接的是人、物，還是地點？

組中文與英文怎麼分與例句
67借
borrow 借入
lend (lent, lent) 借出		主詞把東西拿進來，用 borrow（from＋人）；主詞把東西給出去，用 lend＋人＋物。
May I borrow your dictionary?
Could you lend me your dictionary?
68帶
bring 帶來
take 帶去
fetch 去拿來		朝說話者或聽話者的方向，用 bring；離開說話者，用 take；去了再帶回來，用 fetch。
Remember to bring your student ID tomorrow.
Take an umbrella with you when you go out.
The dog ran to fetch the ball.
69說
say 說（內容）
tell 告訴（人）
speak 說（語言）；發言
talk 交談		say 後面接說的話，要接人得加 to；tell 後面先接人；speak 接語言；talk to＋人＋about＋事。
He said that he would be late.
He told me that he would be late.
She speaks three languages.
We talked about our summer plans.
70看
look 看（動作）
see 看見（結果）
watch 觀看		look 是把眼睛轉過去，接受詞要加 at；see 是看到了；watch 是盯著會動、會變化的東西看。listen／hear 的分別和 look／see 一樣。
Look at the blackboard, please.
I looked around but saw nobody.
We watched the sun set over the sea.
71花
spend （人）花時間、錢
cost （物）花某人多少錢
take （事）花多少時間		人 spend＋時間或錢＋V-ing（或 on＋名詞）；物 cost＋人＋錢；It takes＋人＋時間＋to V。
I spent two hours cleaning my room.
The concert ticket cost me 3,000 dollars.
It took me two hours to clean my room.
72參加
attend 出席
join 加入
take part in 參與		attend 接會議、課、典禮，不加介系詞；join 接社團、隊伍或人，成為其中一員；take part in（＝participate in）接活動、比賽。
All seniors must attend the graduation ceremony.
He joined the school band last year.
Over 500 runners took part in the race.
73穿
put on 穿上
wear 穿著；戴著
dress 給（人）穿衣服		put on 是動作；wear 是狀態；dress 的受詞是人，不是衣服（be dressed in＋衣服）。
Put on your jacket before you go out.
She is wearing a red scarf today.
The mother dressed her baby in a warm sweater.
74適合
fit 合身
suit 適合（某人）
match 相配		fit 講尺寸大小；suit 講款式、顏色、時間適合某人；match 是兩樣東西彼此搭配。
These shoes don't fit me; they are too tight.
Short hair suits you.
The curtains match the sofa perfectly.
75懷疑
doubt 不太相信
suspect 猜想（是）		doubt 是認為「不是真的、不會發生」；suspect 是認為「可能是真的」。兩句意思相反。
I doubt that he will come.（我想他不會來。）
I suspect that he is lying.（我想他在說謊。）
76除了
besides 除了…還有
except 除了…以外都		besides 是加進去（＝in addition to）；except 是排除在外。少一個 s 的 beside 是「在…旁邊」。
Besides English, she speaks French and Japanese.
Everyone except Tom passed the test.
77聲音
sound 聲音
voice 嗓音
noise 噪音		sound 是各種聲音的總稱；voice 是人說話、唱歌的聲音；noise 是吵雜、讓人不舒服的聲音。
Light travels faster than sound.
The singer has a sweet voice.
The noise from the street kept me awake.
78忘了帶
forget 忘記
leave (left, left) 把…留在（某處）		句子裡有地點，就用 leave＋物＋地點；forget 後面不接地點。
Oh no, I forgot my wallet!
I left my umbrella on the bus.
79問題
question 提問
problem 難題、麻煩		question 是要回答的：ask／answer a question；problem 是要解決的：solve a problem。
May I ask you a question?
Air pollution is a serious problem in big cities.
80費用
price 價格
fee （服務的）費用
fare 車資、票價		price 是商品的標價，用 high／low 形容，不用 expensive；fee 是入場費、學費、手續費；fare 是搭交通工具的錢。
The price of eggs has gone up again.
The entrance fee is 100 dollars for adults.
Children under six don't have to pay the bus fare.

七、自我檢核

  • 遮住每一組的分法，我能看著例句說出 80 組裡每一組的差別。
  • 看到 rise／raise、lie／lay，我會先看後面有沒有受詞，而且寫得出它們的三態。
  • 選項裡出現 -sume、-tribute、-serve 這類同字根的字，我能用字首說出各自的意思。
  • 做混合題填空前，我會先判斷空格要名詞、動詞還是形容詞（affect／effect、advice／advise）。
  • 看到字尾成對的形容詞（economic／economical、sensible／sensitive），我會先看它修飾的名詞。
  • 翻譯寫到「借、帶、花、參加、穿、懷疑、忘了帶」，我會停下來確認方向和句型。
