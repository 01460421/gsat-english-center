T2a　高頻句型 1–10

T2-01 have been in（或 have been a member of）關鍵結構：「已經…兩年」是從過去延續到現在，用現在完成式＋for 一段時間。join 是瞬間動詞，不能接一段時間，要改用表狀態的 be in／be a member of。扣分：寫 have joined the club for two years，時態對但動詞語意錯，這個語意單位會被扣分；寫 joined（過去式）則時態也錯。其他寫法：I joined the street dance club two years ago.／It has been two years since I joined the street dance club.
T2-02 This breakfast shop across from our school has been open for more than twenty years, and it is still popular with students today.關鍵結構：主幹 This breakfast shop has been open；「開了二十多年」的「開」是營業中的狀態，用形容詞 open；「至今仍然」用現在式 is still。「對面」是 across from；「受…歡迎」是 popular with/among。扣分：has opened for twenty years（瞬間動詞接一段時間）；opened … for twenty years（過去式表示已經不開了，語意相反）；popular by students（介系詞錯）。其他寫法：This breakfast shop opened across from our school over twenty years ago and is still popular among students.／This breakfast shop has been in business across from our school for more than two decades, and students still love it.
T2-03 was postponed（或 has been postponed、was put off）關鍵結構：園遊會是「被」延期的一方，用被動 be + p.p.；postpone … until/to 表示延到某天。扣分：postponed（主動，等於園遊會自己延期）、was postpone（漏 -ed）都屬動詞結構錯誤；was canceled 語意錯（取消不是延期）；was delayed 偏向「誤點、拖延」，用在活動改期較不精準。其他寫法：The school fair was moved to next Saturday because of the typhoon.
T2-04 After these plastic bottles are recycled, they will be made into clothes and schoolbags.關鍵結構：兩個被動：are recycled（時間子句用現在式代替未來式）、will be made into（被做成）。be made into 是「原料變成品」；be made of/from 是「成品由某原料製成」，方向相反。扣分：After … will be recycled（時間子句用 will）；they will make into clothes（漏 be，變主動）；be made of clothes（搭配方向錯）。其他寫法：Once recycled, these plastic bottles will be turned into clothes and backpacks.／These plastic bottles will be recycled and made into clothes and schoolbags.
T2-05 more and more crowded關鍵結構：骨架以「圖書館」為主詞，「人越來越多」要轉成「越來越擁擠」；crowded 是多音節形容詞，用 more and more + 原級。扣分：crowdeder、more crowded and more crowded（形式錯）；more and more people（與骨架的 is getting 接不上，句子不通）。其他寫法：increasingly crowded；若不受骨架限制，也可寫 As final exams approach, there are more and more people in the library.
T2-06 Today's smartphones are much lighter and thinner than those of ten years ago, yet they have many more functions.關鍵結構：比較對象要同類：手機比手機，用 those of ten years ago 或 than they were ten years ago；「…得多」用 much 修飾比較級；可數名詞的「多很多」用 many more + 複數 N；「卻」用 yet 或 but。扣分：very lighter；more lighter；much more functions（functions 可數，應用 many more）；只寫 than ten years ago 在考場上有被視為比較對象不對等的風險。其他寫法：Smartphones today are far lighter and thinner than they were ten years ago, but they can do a lot more.
T2-07 one of the most memorable experiences關鍵結構：one of the + 最高級 + 複數名詞。扣分：experience（單數）；one of most memorable experiences（漏 the）；the most memorable experience（漏掉「之一」，語意單位不完整）。其他寫法：one of the best memories；one of the most unforgettable experiences 也常見，但 unforgettable 本身已是「忘不了」，有些老師認為不宜再加 most，用 memorable 最保險。
T2-08 This is the most challenging competition I have ever taken part in, but it is also the one I have learned the most from.關鍵結構：the + 最高級 + N + (that) S have ever p.p.；主要子句用 is 搭配 have ever p.p.，若想強調比賽已結束而用 was，嚴格來說子句要配 had ever p.p.；take part in 的 in 要留在子句尾；後半用 the one 代替 competition，避免重複；「學到最多」是 learn the most from。扣分：the most challenge competition（名詞當形容詞）；I have ever taken part（漏 in）；the most challenging competition which I have ever taken part in it（重複受詞）。其他寫法：This is the toughest contest I have ever entered, but it has also taught me the most.（美式口語也常見 I ever entered，考場建議用完成式。）
T2-09 who arrive at school early to practice（或 who come to school early to practice）關鍵結構：「每天一大早就到學校練球的」修飾「隊員」，用主格關係子句 who + V，放在名詞後面；先行詞 members 是複數，動詞用 arrive／come。「練球」用 to practice 表目的。扣分：the team members come to school …（漏 who，一句兩個動詞）；who arrives（一致性錯）。其他寫法：that get to school early to train。（本書一律用美式拼法 practice；英式 practise 不算錯，但全篇要統一。）
T2-10 The sneakers I ordered online yesterday arrived this morning, which really surprised me.關鍵結構：「我昨天在網路上訂的」是受格關係子句，關係代名詞可省略（the sneakers (that) I ordered）；「這讓我非常驚訝」指前面整件事，用逗號＋which。扣分：The sneakers I ordered them（重複受詞）；, that really surprised me（非限定子句不可用 that）；which made me very surprising（情緒形容詞誤用，見 T2b 句型 20）。其他寫法：The sneakers that I ordered online yesterday were delivered this morning, and I was really surprised.
T2-11 the place where we often go running（或 the place where we often run）關鍵結構：the place + where + 完整子句。we often go running 本身完整，所以用 where。扣分：the place which we often run（子句完整卻用 which）；where we often run there（where 已含地點，重複）；where we often run at（多餘介系詞）。其他寫法：where we often go jogging；the place we often go running（place 後的 where 可省）。
T2-12 I still remember those childhood days when my parents took me to the beach every weekend.關鍵結構：「…的那段日子」用 the days when + 完整子句；回憶過去的習慣用過去式 took；「記得（過去的事）」用 remember + N。「小時候」可用 childhood 修飾 days，或加 when I was little。扣分：the days which my parents took me to the beach（子句完整卻用 which）；remember to go to the beach（remember to V 是「記得要去做」，語意錯）；take me to the beach（時態錯）。其他寫法：I still remember the days when my parents took me to the beach every weekend during my childhood.／I still remember how my parents used to take me to the beach every weekend when I was a kid.
T2-13 What you see關鍵結構：「你看到的（東西）」＝the things that you see，濃縮成名詞子句 what you see 當主詞，動詞用單數 is。扣分：That you see（that 後面要接完整句，see 缺受詞）；The thing you see it（重複受詞）；Which you see（which 不能這樣單獨表示「所…的東西」）。其他寫法：Everything you see（搭配 is）。若寫 The things you see，動詞要改為 are，與骨架不合。
T2-14 Before choosing a major, many students have never thought about what they are really good at.關鍵結構：「自己真正擅長的是什麼」是 think about 的受詞，用名詞子句 what they are really good at，直述語序，at 留在句尾；「從來沒有想過」到選科系前為止，用 have never thought；Before + V-ing 的主詞與主要子句相同（都是學生）。扣分：what are they really good at（倒裝）；what they really good at（漏 be）；never think 在本句可接受（陳述普遍現象），但 never thought（過去式）會讓語意變成只講過去某次。其他寫法：Many students never think about what they are truly good at before they choose a major.／… have never considered where their real strengths lie.
T2-15 watched movies, played board games, and made pizza together關鍵結構：骨架已有 stayed，後面三個動作都用過去式，形成 A, B, and C 的平行結構。扣分：watching movies, play board games（形式不一）；watched movies, played board games, made pizza（漏 and）。其他寫法：watched movies, played board games, and even made pizza together（用 even 譯出「還」，語氣更生動）。
T2-16 The goal of this club is to help students learn about local history, protect old buildings, and share these stories with more people.關鍵結構：主幹 The goal … is to V；help students 後面三個原形動詞並列（learn、protect、share）。「把 A 分享給 B」是 share A with B。扣分：learn …, protecting …, and to share …（三種形式混用）；share these stories to more people（介系詞錯）；The goal of this club is let students …（漏 to，一句兩個動詞）。其他寫法：This club aims to help students get to know local history, protect old buildings, and pass these stories on to more people.
T2-17 Having dinner with my family（或 Eating dinner with my family）關鍵結構：「吃晚餐」這件事當主詞，用動名詞片語，動詞用單數 is（骨架已給）。扣分：Have dinner with my family is …（原形動詞當主詞，等於一句兩個動詞，屬結構錯誤）；Having dinner with my family are …（動名詞主詞應配單數動詞）。其他寫法：Sharing dinner with my family；To have dinner with my family 文法正確，但描述日常習慣時動名詞較自然。
T2-18 Choosing a study method that suits you and keeping a regular schedule are more important than the number of hours you spend at cram school.關鍵結構：兩個動名詞片語以 and 連接當主詞，動詞用複數 are；「適合自己的讀書方法」用關係子句 a study method that suits you；比較對象是「時數」，寫成 the number of hours (that) you spend。扣分：Choose … and keep … is（原形動詞當主詞）；a study method suits you（漏 that，一句兩個動詞）；than cram school（比較對象不對等）。其他寫法：Finding a way of studying that works for you and following a regular routine matter more than how many hours you spend in cram school.
T2-19 are many parents lining up to park（或 are a lot of parents waiting in line to park their cars）關鍵結構：「有人正在做某事」用 There are + 複數 N + V-ing；「等著停車」用 to park 表目的。扣分：are many parents line up（there be 後接原形動詞，雙動詞）；have many parents …（there 不接 have）；is many parents（一致性錯）。其他寫法：are many parents waiting for a parking space；若不受骨架限制，Every morning, many parents line up in front of the school gate to park. 更簡潔。
T2-20 There is a small park in our neighborhood, and it has an area just for dogs.關鍵結構：第一個「有」是存在，用 There is … in our neighborhood，或以社區為主詞 Our neighborhood has …；第二個「有」是公園擁有某區域，用 it has 或 with。「專門讓狗玩的」用 just for dogs 或關係子句 where dogs can play。扣分：In our neighborhood has a small park（介系詞片語當主詞）；There has a park；an area which dogs can play（子句完整卻用 which）。其他寫法：Our neighborhood has a small park with a special area where dogs can run and play.
