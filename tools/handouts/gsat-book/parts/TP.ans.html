TP　中譯英練習 12 回

TP-01
1. For my foreign friend Mark, it is not hard at all to read a Chinese menu.
也可以寫：For my foreign friend Mark, reading Chinese menus is not difficult at all.「對某人來說，做某事（不）難」最常用虛主詞句型：It is (not) + 形容詞 + for + 人 + to + 原形動詞，把真正的主詞 to read a Chinese menu 放到句尾；「對…來說」也可以放句首：For my foreign friend Mark, it is not hard at all to read a Chinese menu.；也可以用動名詞 reading Chinese menus 當主詞。不能寫成 My foreign friend Mark is not hard to …（人不是「難」的主詞）。「一點也不」是 not … at all；「看懂」用 read 或 understand；「菜單」menu，泛指時用 a Chinese menu 或複數 Chinese menus。「外國的」是形容詞 foreign，不是名詞 foreigner。
2. He ate snacks, played games, and bought some clothes at the night market.
也可以寫：At the night market, he had some snacks, played games, and also bought some clothes.「吃了小吃、玩了遊戲，還買了…」三個動作並列，英文用平行結構：三個動詞都用過去式 ate／played／bought，前兩項用逗號隔開，最後一項前面加 and（「還」可譯 also）。可數名詞要用複數 snacks、games；clothes 本身就是複數形，不能寫 clothe 或 a clothes。「在夜市」at the night market，放句首加逗號或放句尾都可以。
TP-02
1. On Qixi, it is not easy for couples to book a table at a popular restaurant.
也可以寫：On Qixi, it is hard for couples to get a table at a popular restaurant.「情侶們要…並不容易」是說「這件事」不容易，英文用虛主詞 It 開頭，真正的主詞（不定詞片語）放後面：It is not easy for couples to book a table …；人不能當 easy 的主詞（× Couples are not easy to …）。餐廳「訂位」是 book／reserve a table，order 是點餐。
2. However, a simple date can be as romantic as an expensive dinner.
也可以寫：But a simple date can also be as romantic as an expensive dinner.「和…一樣＋形容詞」用原級比較 as＋原級形容詞＋as：as romantic as。比較的兩邊要對等：a simple date 對 an expensive dinner。「也可以」寫 can（also）be，can 後面接原形 be；expensive 以母音開頭，冠詞用 an。
TP-03
1. The first week of school is the time when clubs are busy looking for new members.
也可以寫：The first week of the school year is the time when every club is busy finding new members.「…是…的時候」說的是一段時間，英文用 the time when ＋完整子句；when 在子句裡當時間副詞（等於 in which）。「忙著找」是 be busy + V-ing：are busy looking for。主詞 clubs 是複數，be 動詞用 are；如果寫 every club，就要改成單數 is。
2. So far, more than fifty new students have joined the dance club.
也可以寫：Up to now, over fifty new students have already joined the dance club.「到目前為止」（so far／up to now）加上「已經」，表示從開學到現在累積的結果，要用現在完成式 have joined。「五十多位」是 more than fifty（或 over fifty），名詞用複數 students。join 是及物動詞，直接接社團：join the dance club。
TP-04
1. More and more classes design their own class T-shirts for sports day.
也可以寫：More and more classes are designing their own T-shirts for Sports Day.「越來越多＋名詞」用 more and more + 複數名詞：班級是可數名詞，要寫 classes，動詞跟著用複數 design（不加 s）。中文沒有時間詞，說的是最近的趨勢，用現在式 design、現在進行式 are designing 或現在完成式 have designed 都可以。「自己的班服」是 their own class T-shirts（their 對應複數的 classes），「為運動會」用 for sports day。
2. Besides drawing the pattern, my classmates also write their own names on the back of each T-shirt.
也可以寫：Apart from drawing the design, my classmates will also write their names on the back of each shirt.「除了做 A，還做 B」用 Besides + V-ing …, S + also + V …：besides 是介系詞，後面的「畫」要用動名詞 drawing（也可以用 In addition to、Apart from + V-ing）；不要用 except（那是排除）。「還會」是 also 或 will also，「還」不是 still。主詞 my classmates 是複數，動詞用原形 write（不加 s），「自己的名字」寫 their (own) names。「在每件班服背後」用 on the back of each T-shirt（each 接單數）。
TP-05
1. In recent years, many old houses have been turned into bookstores and small studios instead of being torn down.
也可以寫：In recent years, instead of being torn down, many old houses have been turned into bookstores and small studios.「近年來」是現在完成式的訊號；老屋是「被」改造，用完成式被動 have been turned into（也可用 made into、transformed into）。「而不是被拆除」可以濃縮成介系詞片語 instead of being torn down 或 rather than being torn down：介系詞後面接動名詞，而且房子是被拆，所以用被動的 being + 過去分詞（tear-tore-torn）；這個片語也可以移到句首。「書店和小型工作室」都是可數名詞，用複數 bookstores and small studios。 「…，而不是被拆除」不必譯成另一個子句，改成介系詞片語 instead of／rather than + being torn down（介系詞＋被動動名詞）；主要子句用完成式被動 have been turned into。
2. Besides selling books and coffee, these bookstores are also often used to hold small exhibitions and concerts.
也可以寫：In addition to selling books and coffee, these bookstores are often used to hold small exhibitions and concerts as well.「除了…（也）…」是「另外加上」，用 besides／in addition to／apart from，這些都是介系詞，後面的動詞用動名詞 selling。書店是「被」拿來使用，用被動語態 are used to + 原形動詞（被用來做…），注意不要和「習慣於」的 be used to + V-ing 混淆。「也常」放在 be 動詞後面：are also often used。展覽、音樂會泛指時用複數 exhibitions and concerts。
TP-06
1. To leave no trace behind, we should carry all our trash back down the mountain.
也可以寫：In order not to leave any trace, we should take all our garbage down the mountain.「為了不…」是否定的目的，可以寫 To leave no trace（用 no 否定名詞），或 In order not to leave any trace（not 放在 to 前面）；no 和 any 不能同時用。「我們應該…」用 should + 原形動詞。「把所有垃圾帶下山」寫 carry all our trash back down the mountain；trash、garbage 是不可數名詞，不加 s。
2. If we leave the trails designed for visitors, fragile plants may be stepped on and damaged.
也可以寫：If we step off the trails designed for visitors, fragile plants might be damaged.「如果…」是條件子句，用 If + 現在式。「專為遊客設計的步道」中文修飾語在前，英文用過去分詞片語後位修飾：the trails designed for visitors（步道是被設計的，用 p.p.）。「脆弱的植物」是 fragile plants；植物是被踩的一方，「可能被踩壞」用被動 may be stepped on and damaged。 「專為遊客設計的步道」中文修飾語在前；英文先寫名詞 the trails，再用過去分詞片語 designed for visitors 放在後面修飾。「被踩壞」要用被動語態 be stepped on／be damaged。
TP-07
1. Many young people say that they eat a vegetarian diet not for religious reasons but to protect the environment.
也可以寫：Many young people say that they have become vegetarians not because of their religious beliefs but to protect the environment.「表示」後面接一整句話，用 say that + 子句（that 可省略）。「不是因為…，而是為了…」用 not … but …：not for religious reasons（或 not because of religion）but to protect the environment，but 後面接表目的的不定詞。「吃素」寫成 eat a vegetarian diet 或 are vegetarians，不是 eat vegetables。
2. This trend is changing people's eating habits, and many people are starting to care about where their food comes from.
也可以寫：This trend is changing the way people eat, and many have begun to care about where their food comes from.「正在改變」描述正在發生的趨勢，用現在進行式 is changing；「開始關心」也可以用進行式 are starting to care about。「食物從哪裡來」當 care about 的受詞，要寫成間接問句 where their food comes from（直述句語序、動詞加 s）。「飲食習慣」是 eating habits。 「食物從哪裡來」在中文是問句的形式，放進句子當 care about 的受詞時，要改成間接問句的直述句語序 where their food comes from；後半句沒有連接詞，英文要用 and 把兩個子句接起來。
TP-08
1. Because they envy the perfect lives of others online, many young people are no longer sure whether they are happy.
也可以寫：Envying other people's perfect lives online, many young people no longer know if they are happy.「因羨慕…」中文省略主詞，英文要寫成 Because they envy …（或分詞構句 Envying …）；envy 是動詞，「羨慕的」形容詞要用 jealous／envious。「網路上別人的完美生活」改成後位修飾：the perfect lives of others online。「不再」用 no longer（放在 be 動詞後或一般動詞前）。「是否快樂」是間接問句，用 whether／if＋主詞＋動詞：whether they are happy。 前半句「因羨慕…」中文沒有主詞，英文要補成 Because they envy …（或用分詞 Envying …）；「網路上別人的完美生活」要把地點修飾語移到名詞後面：the perfect lives of others online。
2. Therefore, they should not only learn how to build confidence but also reduce the time they spend on social media.
也可以寫：Therefore, they should not only learn how to be more confident but also spend less time on social media.「不但…也…」用 not only … but also，兩邊接同形式的原形動詞：should not only learn … but also reduce …。「如何建立自信」用疑問詞＋不定詞 how to build confidence（confidence 是名詞）。「使用社群媒體的時間」是前置修飾，改成 the time they spend on social media。 「使用社群媒體的時間」是前置修飾，英文要改成 the time they spend on social media（time 後面接省略 that 的形容詞子句）；「不但…也…」要讓 not only 和 but also 後面接同樣形式的原形動詞。
TP-09
1. For those who blindly pursue trends, mobile phones and clothes are often thrown away even though they can still be used.
也可以寫：For those people who always chase the latest fashions, phones and clothes are often thrown away even though they can still be used.「對於那些…的人來說」用 For those who＋V …；those 可以單獨當「那些人」，也可以當限定詞修飾 people，寫成 For those people who … 一樣正確。「流行」在這裡是名詞「潮流」，寫 trends／fashion。手機和衣服是「被丟掉」的，用被動 are thrown away；「還能用」也要用被動 can still be used（或 still work），用 even though 連接表讓步。 「手機和衣服往往還能用就被丟掉了」中文先說「還能用」再說「被丟掉」，英文主要子句放前面 are often thrown away，再用 even though they can still be used 補充讓步。
2. They live in a society where advertisements are all around, so no matter how much they possess, they never feel they have enough.
也可以寫：They live in a society where advertising is everywhere, so no matter how much they own, they always feel it is not enough.「廣告無所不在的社會」的修飾語是完整子句（廣告無所不在），缺的是「在那裡」，用關係副詞 where（＝in which）：a society where advertisements are all around。「無論擁有多少」用 no matter how much＋S＋V，指總量用 much。「總覺得還不夠」補上主詞：they never feel they have enough。 「廣告無所不在的社會」是有主詞、動詞的子句修飾名詞，英文改成放在名詞後面的關係副詞子句 a society where advertisements are all around；「總覺得還不夠」省略主詞，英文要補上 they。
TP-10
1. These days, no matter where we travel, we can easily find identical products sold by just a few global brands.
也可以寫：Nowadays, no matter where we go, we can easily find the same goods sold by a handful of international brands.「無論到哪裡」用 no matter where＋S＋V（＝wherever），中文省略主詞，英文要補 we，語序是直述句。「我們都能」的「都」只是加強語氣，不譯也可以，要譯就寫 we can all（we all can 也對）。「少數幾個國際品牌所販售的商品」修飾語放在名詞後面：products sold by just a few global brands（sold 是被動分詞，等於 that are sold）。「相同商品」是 identical products、the same products 或 the same goods。 「少數幾個國際品牌所販售的相同商品」中文修飾語放在名詞前，英文把被動分詞片語 sold by … 放到 products 後面；「無論到哪裡旅行」沒有主詞，英文要補 we，寫成 no matter where we travel。
2. Faced with the tide of globalization, many small towns have begun to promote traditional festivals and foods that are not found anywhere else.
也可以寫：Facing the wave of globalization, many small towns have started to promote traditional festivals and food that are unique to their area.「面對…」放句首，用分詞構句 Faced with／Facing＋名詞，邏輯主詞是後面的「許多小鎮」。「浪潮」是比喻，寫 the tide／wave of globalization（單數）。「只有當地才有的」改成放在名詞後面的關係子句 that are not found anywhere else／that are unique to their area，節慶與美食是被找到的，用被動。「開始推廣」是已經出現的變化，用現在完成式 have begun to promote。 「只有當地才有的傳統節慶和美食」修飾語放名詞後面，寫成關係子句 traditional festivals and foods that are not found anywhere else；「面對…浪潮」用分詞構句 Faced with the tide of globalization。
TP-11
1. This year's minimum wage is five percent higher than last year's, forcing many small shops to raise the prices of their products.
也可以寫：The minimum wage this year is 5 percent higher than it was last year, forcing many small businesses to raise their prices.「比去年高出百分之五」用比較級：This year's minimum wage is five percent higher than last year's，數量 five percent 放在 higher 前面；比較的是兩年的最低工資，所以寫 last year's（＝last year's minimum wage）或 that of last year。「迫使許多小店家調漲…」是前面整件事的結果，用句尾分詞 forcing many small shops to raise …（force + 人 + to VR）。「調漲價格」是 raise prices，不要寫成不及物的 rise。 「今年的最低工資比去年高出百分之五」比較的是兩年的最低工資，英文寫 than last year's（或 than that of last year），百分比 five percent 放在比較級 higher 前面；「迫使…」承接前面整件事，用句尾分詞 forcing … to VR，不另起一句。
2. For the owners of these shops, paying their employees higher wages every month is often seen as a heavy financial burden.
也可以寫：For these shop owners, paying higher salaries to their workers every month is often regarded as a heavy economic burden.「每個月付給員工更高的薪水」是一個動作，當主詞用動名詞 Paying their employees higher wages every month（或 paying higher wages to their employees），後面動詞用單數 is。「常被視為」用被動 is often seen／regarded as。「經濟負擔」是 financial／economic burden，economy 是名詞，不能直接修飾 burden。 中文以「每個月付給員工更高的薪水」這件事當主題，英文用動名詞主詞 Paying their employees higher wages every month，動詞用單數 is；「常被視為」是 is often seen／regarded as；「對…來說」放句首寫成 For the owners of these shops,。
TP-12
1. Testing new drugs on animals has long been controversial, because what works on laboratory mice does not necessarily work on humans.
也可以寫：Using animals to test new drugs has always been a controversial issue, since what works for lab mice may not work for humans.「用動物測試新藥」是一個動作，當主詞時用動名詞片語 Testing new drugs on animals（視為單數，助動詞用 has）。「一直備受爭議」從過去延續到現在，用現在完成式 has long been controversial 或 has been widely debated。「對實驗鼠有效的」省略了中心名詞，英文用 what works on laboratory mice（＝the things that work on laboratory mice）當子句主詞，what 子句視為單數，所以後面是 does not necessarily work。「實驗鼠」是 laboratory mice（lab mice），mouse 的複數是 mice。「未必」用 not necessarily，也可以寫 may not。 「用動物測試新藥」這個動作當主詞，英文改成動名詞片語 Testing new drugs on animals；「對實驗鼠有效的」沒有中心名詞，要改寫成 what 名詞子句 what works on laboratory mice，當 because 子句的主詞。
2. It is not easy to stop animal testing completely, but computer models have replaced some experiments, sparing many animals from suffering.
也可以寫：It is difficult to end animal testing entirely, but computer models have taken the place of some experiments, sparing many animals from pain.「要…不容易」用虛主詞 It is not easy to VR，把長的不定詞片語移到後面。「已取代部分實驗」用現在完成式 have replaced some experiments，也可以改用被動 some experiments have been replaced by computer models。「，使許多動物免於受苦」是前一件事帶來的結果，用句尾分詞 sparing／saving many animals from suffering（spare sb from N：使某人免於…）；from 後面要接名詞 suffering 或 pain。 「要完全停止動物實驗不容易」以動作當主詞，英文改用虛主詞 It is not easy to …；「，使許多動物免於受苦」不另起一句，用句尾分詞 sparing many animals from suffering 表示前面整件事帶來的結果。
