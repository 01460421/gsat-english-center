C2　篇章結構解法

篇章結構把幾個完整的句子從文章裡抽掉，要你從選項中選出句子放回原位。它幾乎不考難字，考的是「這一句為什麼能接在那一句後面」。句子之間靠代名詞、轉折語、時間順序和重複的名詞連在一起，這些線索都看得見、圈得出來。這個單元教你怎麼找線索，再用一篇短文逐格示範。

一、題型：4 個空格，填回完整的句子

項目內容
題數與配分一篇文章 4 個空格，每格 2 分，共 8 分；建議 6 分鐘內做完
選項4 或 5 個完整的句子；近年是 5 個選項選 4 個，有一句用不到
考什麼句子和句子怎麼連：指代、邏輯關係、段落結構
和文意選填的差別文意選填看空格前後幾個字；篇章結構要看空格的前一句和後一句
風險每個選項只用一次，放錯一句常會連錯兩格，一次丟 4 分

二、四步解法

  1. 先讀選項，圈線索字：代名詞、指示詞、轉折語、時間詞、例子標記、重複的名詞。
  2. 讀文章，看空格位置：段首、段中還是段尾，決定這一格需要哪一種句子。
  3. 前後都要接得上：選項的開頭要接前一句，選項的內容要讓後一句接得下去。
  4. 處理多的一句：最有把握的先填，最後剩兩句搶一格時，兩句都代入比較，再整篇讀一次。

步驟一：先讀選項，圈線索字

每個選項句都帶著「它前面一定要有什麼」的條件。讀選項時把下面這些字圈起來，並在旁邊用幾個字寫下條件。

  • 代名詞和指示詞：it、they、he、she、this、these、such。前一句必須有它指的對象，單複數要一致。
  • the＋名詞：the call、the hunters。用 the 多半表示前面已經提過（泛指一整類的 the honeyguide 是例外）；用 a／an 多半是第一次出現。
  • 轉折語和連接語：however、as a result、for example、on the other hand、instead。它規定了這一句和前一句的邏輯關係。
  • 時間詞：at first、later、since then、today。它規定了這一句在時間軸上的位置。
  • also、another、too：表示前面已經講過一個同類的東西。

The hunters, on the other hand, have the right tools for the job.另一方面，獵人則有合適的工具來做這件事。

圈 on the other hand 和 The hunters，旁邊寫：「前一句要講另一方、而且是做不到這件事」。帶著這個條件去讀文章，看到符合的地方就能立刻對上。

三、連貫線索總表

類型線索字看到就檢查兩句示範
指代it、they、this、these、such；the＋提過的名詞；another、also前一句有沒有它指的人、事、物；單複數合不合The city built a new bike lane along the river. It is now used by thousands of riders every day.
轉折however、but、yet、still、nevertheless、even so前後兩句方向相反：好和壞、預期和結果The medicine works quickly. However, its effects last only a few hours.
因果therefore、as a result、so、thus、that is why、for this reason前一句是原因，這一句是結果；順序不能反The bridge was damaged in the earthquake. As a result, drivers had to take a longer route for months.
舉例for example、for instance、such as、one of them前一句是概括的說法，這一句是具體的例子Some animals change color with the seasons. The Arctic fox, for example, turns white in winter.
時間at first、then、later、soon、finally、since then、today；年代、時態事件的先後；年代通常由早到晚At first, the shop sold only tea. Later, it began to offer cakes and sandwiches as well.
對比in contrast、on the other hand、while、unlike、instead兩個對象各說一句，內容相對City schools often have large classes. Rural schools, in contrast, may have fewer than ten students in a grade.

沒有線索字的時候，還有兩種線索：

  • 重複的名詞：同一個名詞、它的同義詞，或「大類和小類」（bird 和 honeyguide）會在相鄰的句子裡一再出現。選項裡的關鍵名詞在哪一段出現，它多半就屬於那一段。
  • 問和答：選項是問句，後一句常是 The answer…、One reason…；空格後一句若是回答，空格就是問句。

四、空格位置決定句子的功能

位置這一句通常是怎麼檢查
段首主題句：概括整段，或承接上一段、帶出新重點把整段讀完再選。這一句要能涵蓋後面每一句，通常不會是細節或例子。
段中支持句（解釋、例子、下一個步驟）或轉折前一句和後一句都要接得上。特別看後一句開頭的代名詞和轉折語。
段尾小結、結果，或帶出下一段看下一段的第一句。常見 in this way、as a result、thus。

最常見的錯：只看前一句就選。空格的後一句往往藏著更強的線索。後一句如果用 It、They、Instead、For example、In this way 開頭，你選的句子就必須提供它要的東西：指代的對象、被否定的說法、被舉例的概括句、被總結的內容。

小示範：兩句都接得上前面，只有一句接得上後面

Many teenagers believe they can study well while listening to music. ______ Songs with lyrics, in particular, compete with the textbook for the brain's attention.許多青少年相信自己可以邊聽音樂邊把書讀好。______ 尤其是有歌詞的歌，會和課本搶大腦的注意力。

  • 甲：They say that it helps them relax and stay at their desks longer.
  • 乙：Some teachers warn, however, that music can make reading harder.

只看前一句，甲的 They 指 teenagers、it 指聽音樂，乙用 however 轉折，兩句都接得上。再看後一句：in particular（尤其）是把前一句的說法縮小到一個特例，內容是負面的（搶注意力）。所以空格必須先說「音樂會妨礙讀書」。甲說的是音樂的好處，接到後一句方向就反了；只有乙前後都接得上。

五、多的一句長什麼樣

5 個選項選 4 個時，多出來的那一句不會是亂寫的。它和文章主題相關，甚至用了文章裡的關鍵字，但放進任何一格，前後至少有一邊接不上。常見三種：

  • 談到別的方向：講同一個主題的另一面，但文章沒有往那裡發展。
  • 線索字落空：句子裡有 As a result、For example、this，文章裡卻找不到對應的原因、概括句或指代對象。
  • 只接得上一邊：接前一句很順，後一句卻接不下去（或反過來）。

做法：不必一開始就猜哪一句是多的。先填最有把握的格，剩下兩句搶一格時，兩句都代進去，把「前一句＋選項＋後一句」連著讀，接不上的就是多的一句。

六、示範：逐格解一篇

For thousands of years, people have trained dogs to hunt and horses to carry heavy loads. Working side by side with a completely wild animal, however, is far less common. ______(1) In parts of Africa, this small brown bird helps people find something that both of them want: a wild bees' nest.

The partnership begins with a sound. When honey hunters walk into the forest, they give a special call to announce that they are ready to search. ______(2) It then flies from tree to tree, stopping now and then to make sure that the hunters are still following.

Once they arrive at the nest, each partner does what the other cannot. The bird knows exactly where the bees live, but it cannot break into the nest by itself. ______(3) They calm the bees with smoke and then cut the nest open with an ax.

When the work is done, the hunters carry the honey home. ______(4) In this way, both sides gain from the hunt, and neither could succeed so easily alone.

(A) The hunters, on the other hand, have the right tools for the job.
(B) The wax that they leave behind makes a rich meal for the bird.
(C) One remarkable exception is the honeyguide.
(D) Honey has been used as both food and medicine since ancient times.
(E) A honeyguide that hears the call often answers with a loud cry of its own.

第一步：讀選項，圈線索字

選項線索字前面一定要有什麼
(A)on the other hand；the right tools前一句講另一方，而且是「做不到」；後一句可能說用什麼工具
(B)they；the wax；leave behind前面有一群人拿走了東西；這一句講鳥得到什麼
(C)exception；the honeyguide前面要有一個通則；主角第一次出場，位置應該很前面
(D)沒有線索字，只有主題字 honey沒有條件，要小心，可能是多的一句
(E)the call；A honeyguide前面提過 call；後一句可以用 it 指這隻鳥

第二步：逐格判斷

  • 第 (1) 格（段中）　前一句是通則：和野生動物合作 far less common。後一句的 this small brown bird 表示空格剛介紹過一種鳥。(C) 的 exception 接前，the honeyguide 接後。
  • 第 (2) 格（段中）　前一句有 a special call。後一句 It then flies：It 是單數、會飛，指的是鳥，可是前一句的主詞是複數的 hunters，所以空格必須把鳥帶進來。(E) 的 the call 接前，A honeyguide 接後。
  • 第 (3) 格（段中）　前一句說鳥 cannot break into the nest。後一句的 They 會用煙、用斧頭，是人。(A) 用 on the other hand 把鳥和獵人對比，the right tools 又帶出後一句的 smoke 和 ax。
  • 第 (4) 格（段中，結論前）　前一句是獵人的收穫：carry the honey home。後一句 In this way, both sides gain 說雙方都有收穫，所以空格要補上鳥的收穫。(B) 的 they 指獵人，蜂蠟留給鳥吃。

第三步：確認多的一句

(D) 講蜂蜜自古的用途，和主題有關，但全文談的是人和鳥怎麼合作。把它放進第 (4) 格：前面有 honey，看起來接得上；後面的 In this way, both sides gain 卻接不下去，因為鳥還沒有得到任何東西。這就是「只接得上一邊」。

答案：(1) C　(2) E　(3) A　(4) B

練習 B　篇章結構（4 格 5 個選項，限時 5 分鐘）

先讀選項、圈線索字，再讀文章。有一個選項用不到。

Every autumn, forests in many parts of the world turn from green to brilliant shades of yellow, orange, and red. ______(1) The answer lies in the chemicals inside each leaf.

During spring and summer, leaves are full of chlorophyll, a green chemical that uses sunlight to make food for the tree. Leaves also contain yellow and orange chemicals, but there is so much chlorophyll that it hides them completely. ______(2) With less sunlight available, the tree stops making chlorophyll, and the green slowly fades. Only then can the yellow and orange that were there all along finally be seen.

______(3) Unlike yellow and orange, the red color is not hidden in the leaf during the summer. Instead, it is produced in the fall from sugar that is trapped in the leaf. Because sunshine helps leaves make sugar, a series of sunny autumn days often brings especially bright reds.

The show does not last long, though. ______(4) Without its leaves, the tree loses far less water and can rest through the cold months until spring returns.

(A) As a result, many people travel long distances just to enjoy the view.
(B) Red leaves, however, have a different story.
(C) But why do leaves change color before they fall?
(D) As winter approaches, the tree seals off its leaves, and they soon drop to the ground.
(E) This changes when the days grow shorter and cooler in the fall.
