W3　高分語言工具：句型變化、轉折詞、具體化與精準用字

練習 A　句子升級改寫

W3-01 (C) 倒裝、(E) 名詞子句。Only after the power went out 放句首，主要子句改成 did we notice；after 引導的子句本身不倒裝。how quiet our street could be 是 notice 的受詞，屬於名詞子句，用直述語序（how quiet＋主詞＋動詞）。只答出 (C) 算對一半：辨認練習的目的就是看出一句話裡疊了幾層結構。
W3-02 (F) 對比結構、(B) 關係子句。Unlike my older brother 是對比結構，比較的兩方都是「人」（哥哥和我），所以對等。, who spends hours gaming every night, 是非限定關係子句，前後有逗號、用 who 不用 that。另外注意 would rather + 原形動詞。
W3-03 (A) 分詞構句、(B) 關係子句。Frustrated by the slow Wi-Fi 是過去分詞構句，Kevin「被」慢速網路弄得很煩，所以用 p.p.，表原因。, which stays open until ten 是非限定關係子句，補充說明這座圖書館。若誤寫成 Frustrating by ...，主被動就錯了。
W3-04 Opening the box, I found a handwritten card from my aunt.兩個動作主詞都是 I，而且是主動，所以用 V-ing。可接受：Having opened the box,（強調先打開、後發現）；When I opened the box, 文法正確，但不算本題要求的分詞構句。常見錯誤：Opened the box（變成被動）、Open the box（變成祈使句）。
W3-05 My cousin, who moved to Kaohsiung last year, now works as a nurse there.非限定關係子句：前後逗號＋who。「表姊」是特定的人，只是補充資訊，所以不能用 that。時態保留過去式 moved（去年的事），主要子句用現在式 works。可接受：who has lived in Kaohsiung since last year。常見錯誤：that moved、漏掉逗號、寫成 who she moved（重複主詞）。
W3-06 Not until I entered senior high school did I understand the value of free time.until 後面的子句 I entered senior high school 用正常語序；主要子句倒裝成 did + I + 原形 understand。常見錯誤：did I understood（did 後接過去式）、I understood（忘記倒裝）、did I entered（倒錯子句）。
W3-07 What students need is clear instructions, not more rules.What 子句當主詞，考場上動詞一律用 is 最安全。可接受：What students need is not more rules but clear instructions.（not A but B 對比更強）。注意：What students need are ... 母語人士也有人這樣寫，但用法有爭議，考場上避免。真正的錯誤是 What students need it is ...（多了 it）。
W3-08 Built in 1960, the library now needs serious repairs.圖書館「被」建造，所以用過去分詞 Built；分詞的主詞和主要子句的主詞都是 the library。可接受：The library, built in 1960, now needs serious repairs.（分詞片語插在主詞後）。常見錯誤：Building in 1960（主動，意思變成圖書館去蓋東西）、Having built in 1960（同樣是主動）。Having been built in 1960 文法對，但太累贅。
W3-09 Our class won the relay race, which surprised everyone, including us.which 指「我們班贏了接力賽」這整件事，前面一定要有逗號，而且不能用 that。常見錯誤：..., it surprised everyone（逗號連接兩句，會被扣文法分）、..., that surprised everyone。另一種不用關係子句的高分寫法：To everyone's surprise, even our own, our class won the relay race.
W3-10 I can't decide whether I should join the debate team or the photography club.名詞子句當 decide 的受詞，用直述語序 whether I should，不是 whether should I。更簡潔的寫法：I can't decide whether to join the debate team or the photography club. 用 if 也算可接受，但 whether ... or ... 最清楚。常見錯誤：保留問號、寫成 I can't decide something whether ...。
W3-11 While the high-speed rail gets me to Taichung in about an hour, the bus takes nearly three hours but costs much less.While 子句放句首，結束時加逗號，主要子句前不可再加 but；後半句的 takes ... but costs 是同一個主詞的兩個動詞，用 but 連接沒有問題。可接受：The high-speed rail gets me to Taichung in about an hour, whereas the bus takes nearly three hours but costs much less.；或 Compared with the high-speed rail, which gets me to Taichung in about an hour, the bus is much slower but far cheaper.
W3-12 Not only does the new cafeteria serve healthier food, but it also charges lower prices.Not only 放句首，前半句倒裝成 does + 主詞 + 原形 serve；後半句 but it also charges 不倒裝。常見錯誤：Not only the new cafeteria serves（沒倒裝）、does ... serves（does 後接第三人稱單數）。不倒裝也正確的寫法：The new cafeteria not only serves healthier food but also charges lower prices.
W3-13 Walking along the river, we noticed a strange smell and covered our noses.原句是懸垂分詞：在河邊散步的是「我們」，不是「怪味」。改法一：讓主要子句的主詞變成 we（如上）。改法二：把分詞構句還原成完整子句：As we were walking along the river, a strange smell made us cover our noses. 注意 made us cover：使役動詞 make 後接原形。
W3-14 Only when I lost my wallet did I realize how careless I had been.Only when 放句首，主要子句要倒裝：did I realize。how careless I had been 是名詞子句，用直述語序；過去完成式 had been 表示「粗心」發生在「發現」之前。不倒裝的正確寫法：I did not realize how careless I had been until I lost my wallet.
W3-15 I still don't understand why my parents sold our old house.why 子句是 understand 的受詞，屬於間接問句，要用直述語序：拿掉 did，動詞改成過去式 sold。常見錯誤：why my parents sell（拿掉 did 卻忘了改時態）、why did my parents sold（雙重過去式）。

練習 B　轉折詞填空（用不到的兩個詞：however、on the contrary）

W3-16 Instead of visiting the museum, ...空格後面是動名詞 visiting，前面需要介系詞片語，只有 instead of 合乎語意（「沒去博物館，改去老街」）。句首介系詞片語後面原句已有逗號。常見錯誤：寫成 Instead of visit。
W3-17 Our school has many clubs, such as robotics, film, and street dance.空格後面是名詞列舉，用介系詞 such as，前面加逗號。不用 for example，因為後面不是完整句子；列舉完也不要再加 etc.。
W3-18 Despite the heavy rain, ...空格後面是名詞片語 the heavy rain，語意是讓步，所以用介系詞 despite。although 也是讓步，但後面要接子句（Although it rained heavily, ...）。常見錯誤：Despite of。
W3-19 The outdoor concert was postponed because of the typhoon.原因是名詞 the typhoon，用 because of，前面不加逗號。可接受（不在框內）：due to the typhoon。常見錯誤：because the typhoon（because 是連接詞，要接子句）。
W3-20 My sister is outgoing and talkative, while I am quiet and prefer to listen.兩個人的個性並列對照，用 while，前面加逗號。on the contrary 不適合：它用來「否定前面的說法」，不是比較兩個人。另一種可接受的寫法是斷成兩句：... talkative. However, I am quiet ...，但這樣 while 就沒用到，而 while 把兩人放在同一句，對照更緊密。
W3-21 I forgot to charge my phone last night. As a result, I couldn't use ...前因後果，用 as a result。它是連接副詞，前面要用句號（或分號：...last night; as a result, I couldn't ...），後面加逗號。常見錯誤：..., as a result, I couldn't ...（逗號連接兩句）。
W3-22 The moment I walked into the house, ...the moment＝as soon as（一…就），引導時間子句，子句結束加逗號（原句已有）。常見錯誤：寫成 At the moment I walked ...，at the moment 是「此刻」，意思不同。
W3-23 Learning a second language helps you make friends from other countries. In addition, it makes traveling much easier.補充第二個好處，用 in addition。它是連接副詞，前面用句號或分號，後面加逗號。常見錯誤：..., in addition it makes ...。若想用一句寫完，可改用連接詞：..., and it also makes traveling much easier.
W3-24 Small changes can save a lot of energy. For example, turning off classroom lights ...前一句是概括，後一句是具體例子，用 for example，前面句號、後面逗號。不能用 such as，因為後面是完整的句子（主詞 turning off classroom lights＋動詞 can lower）。這也是第三節「概括 → 具體」的典型寫法。
W3-25 Although the new homework app was free, few students used it ...「雖然免費，但很少人用」是讓步，空格後是完整子句，所以用 although，不能用 despite（despite 要寫成 Despite being free, the new homework app ...）。後半句前面不可再加 but。

練習 C　把空泛句寫具體（以下是示範，不是唯一答案。用每題的「評分重點」檢查自己的版本。）

W3-26 Every weeknight, I spend about three hours on homework, usually a set of math problems, a page of English translation, and notes for the next day's history quiz.評分重點：至少一個合理的數字（三小時）；把 a lot of 換成看得見的內容（三個科目各做什麼）。另一個方向：Last Monday alone, I had to finish two worksheets, a lab report, and fifty new vocabulary words before midnight. 常見失分：數字不合理（例如每天十小時作業）、只把 a lot of 換成 lots of，沒有真正具體。
W3-27 Every morning, the breakfast shop near my school is filled with the smell of fried radish cake and the sound of the owner calling out order numbers, and the line often stretches out the door.評分重點：至少一種感官（這裡用了嗅覺、聽覺和視覺）；用「排隊排到門外」證明 popular，而不是直接說 popular。其他感官細節：the hiss of eggs on the hot griddle、the sweet smell of soy milk。常見失分：It is very delicious.（delicious 是極端形容詞，而且仍然空泛）。
W3-28 Mrs. Wu, our seventy-year-old neighbor, waters our plants whenever we go on vacation and often hangs a bag of homemade dumplings on our doorknob.評分重點：有稱呼（Mrs. Wu）、有身分或年齡、有兩個具體行為，讀者自然得出「她很善良」，不必寫 kind。也可以加一句她說的話："Too many dumplings for one person," she always says. 常見失分：只換形容詞（a warm-hearted person），仍然沒有畫面。
W3-29 When I saw my score on the screen, I read it three times to make sure it was real, and then I ran downstairs to hug my mother.評分重點：完全不用 happy 或 very，用三個連續動作（看三次、跑下樓、擁抱）呈現情緒。其他寫法：I could not stop smiling for the rest of the day.／My hands were shaking as I took a screenshot to send to my best friend. 常見失分：換成 I was extremely happy，只是換了副詞，沒有改成動作。
W3-30 My phone saves me time in ways I hardly notice anymore. Every morning, a bus app tells me exactly when my bus will arrive, so I no longer wait at the stop for twenty minutes in the rain. At lunch, I pay for my noodles by scanning a code, which takes about two seconds. Not until my phone broke last month did I realize how much I depend on it.具體化：數字（二十分鐘、兩秒）、場景（雨中等車、午餐付款）。句型：which takes about two seconds（關係子句指整件事）、Not until ... did I realize（倒裝）。注意第一句沒有直接重複 convenient，而是換成「省時間」這個更具體的角度。其他方向：線上查資料、和住在外地的家人視訊。自評：至少兩種具體化技巧、至少一個升級句、沒有黑名單套語，三項都達到才算合格。
W3-31 Joining the photography club taught me to notice details I used to ignore. Every Friday afternoon, our club leader, Amy, gives us one task, such as photographing ten different shades of green on campus. Searching for those shades, I discovered moss on the old library wall and the bright new leaves of a tree I had walked past for a year. What I value most is not the photos but the habit of looking closely.具體化：人物（社長 Amy）、數字（十種綠、一年）、場景與視覺（圖書館牆上的青苔、新葉）。句型：Searching for those shades, I ...（分詞構句，主詞都是 I）、What I value most is not ... but ...（強調＋對比）。第一句就把 good experience 換成「學到注意細節」，這是整段的主題句。其他方向：熱舞社的成果發表、志工社帶國小學童。
W3-32 Ten years ago, my hometown in Yilan was a quiet farming town where most people went to bed by nine. Today, tour buses fill the main street every weekend, and the rice field behind my grandmother's house has become a parking lot for a hot-spring hotel. While tourism has brought jobs for young people, my grandmother misses waking up to the sound of frogs instead of car engines.具體化：數字（十年、九點）、場景（遊覽車、稻田變停車場）、感官（青蛙聲對比引擎聲）、人物（祖母）。句型：where most people went to bed by nine（關係副詞子句）、While ..., ...（對比）。時態：十年前用過去式，現在的改變用現在式與現在完成式。其他方向：老街變成網美景點、新的捷運站讓通勤時間縮短。
W3-33 Taking the bus instead of riding in my father's car cuts down on pollution, but it is not always easy. To reach school by 7:30, I have to leave home at 6:40 and change buses at a crowded transfer station. On rainy days, the bus is so packed that I can hardly reach the handrail, and my wet umbrella drips onto my shoes. Even so, I would rather spend fifty minutes on public transportation than add one more car to the morning traffic.具體化：數字（7:30、6:40、五十分鐘，前後一致）、場景與觸覺（擠到搆不到扶手、雨傘滴水）。句型與轉折：instead of riding（對比）、so packed that、Even so,（讓步）、would rather ... than ...。限時 8 分鐘的時間分配建議：前 2 分鐘列出細節（時間、地點、一個畫面），4 分鐘寫，最後 2 分鐘用本章自我檢核表檢查標點與動詞。常見失分：只寫「環保」的大道理，沒有寫出「不容易」的具體情況；兩邊份量要平均。
