W3　高分語言工具：句型變化、轉折詞、具體化與精準用字

W1 教你看懂題目，W2 教你搭段落骨架。這一章處理的是「句子本身」：同樣的內容，為什麼有人拿 13 分、有人拿 18 分？差別常常就在句子有沒有主從、邏輯有沒有標清楚、細節夠不夠具體、用字準不準。

本章工具對應的評分面向（作文看內容、組織、文法句構、字彙拼字四方面）

  • 句型變化 → 文法句構：長短句交錯、有主有從，而且每一句都正確。
  • 轉折詞 → 組織：句與句之間是因果、對比還是補充，讀者一看就懂。
  • 具體化 → 內容：有數字、有畫面、有人物，不只講道理。
  • 精準用字、刪除套語 → 字彙：用「對」的字，而不是「難」的字。

使用本章工具的三條原則

  1. 正確第一：一個寫錯的倒裝句，給閱卷老師的印象比五個正確的簡單句還差。沒有把握的句型，考場上不要用。
  2. 一段 1–2 個亮點就夠：一段約 80–110 字，放 1–2 個升級句，其餘用清楚的簡單句、複合句。句句都複雜，反而難讀。
  3. 升級是為了更清楚：每次改寫都問自己：改完之後，時間先後、因果或對比關係有沒有更明白？如果只是變長，就不算升級。

使用方式：每一節先讀說明與「基礎句 → 升級句」對照，再做章末練習。練習 A 對應第一節，練習 B 對應第二節，練習 C 對應第三、四節。答案一律在書末解答篇。

一、句型變化：六種升級工具

下面每種工具都附「基礎句 → 升級句」對照。基礎句不是錯句，只是平、散、重複主詞；升級句把兩、三個小句整合成一個有主從關係的句子。

1. 分詞構句

用途：兩個動作的主詞相同時，把其中一個動作縮成分詞，表示時間、原因、同時發生或結果。

  • 主動、同時發生、原因：V-ing ..., S + V
  • 被動：p.p. ..., S + V
  • 先發生、已完成：Having + p.p. ..., S + V
  • 否定：Not + V-ing ..., S + V（not 放在分詞前面）
  • 放句尾表結果：S + V ..., V-ing ...
  • 附帶狀況：with + O + V-ing／p.p.

前提：分詞的邏輯主詞＝主要子句的主詞。

基礎句升級句升級點
I was waiting for the bus. I reviewed my vocabulary notes.Waiting for the bus, I reviewed my vocabulary notes.同時發生，主動
The bike was left in the rain for a week. It was covered with rust.Left in the rain for a week, the bike was covered with rust.被動，表原因
I finished my report. Then I went to the gym with my teammates.Having finished my report, I went to the gym with my teammates.先後順序
I didn't know the way to the museum, so I asked a police officer.Not knowing the way to the museum, I asked a police officer for directions.否定，表原因
The earthquake struck at midnight. It woke up almost everyone in the dorm.The earthquake struck at midnight, waking up almost everyone in the dorm.句尾分詞，表結果
She walked into the exam room. Her hands were shaking.She walked into the exam room with her hands shaking.附帶狀況

常見扣分：懸垂分詞（分詞的主詞和主要子句的主詞對不上）

誤Walking into the classroom, the air conditioner was making a strange noise.（冷氣走進教室？）

正Walking into the classroom, I heard the air conditioner making a strange noise.

誤Having eaten dinner, the dishes were washed by my brother.

正Having eaten dinner, my brother washed the dishes.

另一個常見錯誤：because 後面不能直接接分詞。when、while、after、before 後面可以接 V-ing。

誤Because feeling tired, I went to bed early.

正Feeling tired, I went to bed early.／Because I felt tired, I went to bed early.

2. 關係子句

  • 限定用法：N + who／which／that + V，不加逗號，用來指出「是哪一個」。
  • 非限定用法：N, who／which + V, ...，前後加逗號，只是補充資訊；不能用 that。
  • 補充前面整件事：..., which + V（which 指前面整個子句）。
  • 所有格：N + whose + N + V
  • 地點、時間：the place where ...／the day when ...
  • 專有名詞、獨一無二的人（my mother）後面的關係子句，一律用非限定、加逗號。
基礎句升級句升級點
I have a classmate. He can solve most math problems in a few minutes.I have a classmate who can solve most math problems in a few minutes.限定，指出是哪一位
My grandmother lives in Hualien. She grows all her own vegetables.My grandmother, who lives in Hualien, grows all her own vegetables.非限定，補充資訊
The train was delayed for forty minutes. This made me miss my first class.The train was delayed for forty minutes, which made me miss my first class.which 指前面整件事
I met a girl at camp. Her dream is to become a wildlife photographer.At camp, I met a girl whose dream is to become a wildlife photographer.whose 所有格
We often study at a café after exams. It is only a five-minute walk from our school.The café where we often study after exams is only a five-minute walk from our school.where 修飾地點

誤My father, that works at a hospital, often comes home late.

正My father, who works at a hospital, often comes home late.（非限定不用 that）

誤Sun Moon Lake which is in Nantou attracts many visitors.

正Sun Moon Lake, which is in Nantou, attracts many visitors.（專有名詞後要加逗號）

誤This is the book which I told you about it.

正This is the book which I told you about.（關係代名詞已經代替 it，不可重複）

誤The trip was canceled, it made everyone upset.

正The trip was canceled, which made everyone upset.（逗號不能連接兩個完整句子）

3. 倒裝

規則：否定或限定的副詞放句首時，後面的主要子句改成疑問句語序：助動詞＋主詞＋原形動詞。

  • Not until + 時間／子句 + 助動詞 + S + V（直到…才）
  • Only + when／after／by ... + 助動詞 + S + V（只有…才）
  • Never／Rarely／Seldom + 助動詞 + S + V（從未、很少）
  • Not only + 助動詞 + S + V, but S also V（不但…而且）
  • So + adj.／adv. + be／助動詞 + S + that ...（如此…以致於）

注意：Not until 和 Only when 後面那個子句不倒裝，倒裝的是主要子句。

基礎句升級句升級點
I didn't realize how much my mother did for our family until she was sick in bed.Not until my mother was sick in bed did I realize how much she did for our family.強調「領悟的時間點」
You can see the stars clearly only when you get away from city lights.Only when you get away from city lights can you see the stars clearly.強調條件
I have never felt so proud of my team.Never have I felt so proud of my team.強調情緒強度
The app saves time. It also helps users manage their spending.Not only does the app save time, but it also helps users manage their spending.兩個優點，後者更重要
The line was so long that we gave up and went to another stall.So long was the line that we gave up and went to another stall.強調程度

誤Not until I got home I realized that my phone was missing.

正Not until I got home did I realize that my phone was missing.

誤Never I have seen such a beautiful sunrise.

正Never have I seen such a beautiful sunrise.

誤Only after the exam did I felt relaxed.

正Only after the exam did I feel relaxed.（did 後面接原形）

倒裝是「重音」，一篇作文最多一個，放在真正要強調的句子，例如第二段的領悟句或轉折句。每段都倒裝，就像每句話都用驚嘆號。

4. 強調

  • 分裂句：It is／was + 被強調的部分 + that + 其餘部分
  • What 分裂句：What + S + V + is／was + N／to V／that 子句
  • 助動詞強調：do／does／did + 原形動詞（「確實、真的」，常用在讓步之後）

檢查法：把 It is 和 that 拿掉，剩下的部分要能還原成完整句。

基礎句升級句升級點
The smell of my grandmother's braised pork reminds me most of home.It is the smell of my grandmother's braised pork that reminds me most of home.強調主詞
A single comment from my teacher changed my mind.It was a single comment from my teacher that changed my mind.強調關鍵原因
I need more sleep, not more cram school classes.What I need is more sleep, not more cram school classes.What 分裂句，對比更鮮明
Nobody noticed the mistake. That surprised me.What surprised me was that nobody noticed the mistake.把情緒放在前面當焦點
Online classes are convenient, but they have problems.Online classes do save time, but they make it harder for students to stay focused.先讓步再轉折，同時把 problems 寫具體

誤It was in the library where I first met her.

正It was in the library that I first met her.（被強調的是介系詞片語時，用 that）

誤What I learned from the trip it was patience.

正What I learned from the trip was patience.（What 子句已經是主詞，不可再加 it）

誤He did went to the meeting.

正He did go to the meeting.

5. 名詞子句

  • What 子句當主詞或受詞：What + S + V
  • whether 子句：whether + S + V (or not)，當主詞時用 whether，不用 if。
  • 疑問詞子句：how／why／where／when + S + V（直述句語序，不倒裝）
  • that 子句：The fact that + S + V；The problem is that + S + V
  • 名詞子句當主詞時，動詞用單數。
基礎句升級句升級點
How could I tell my parents about my grades? I didn't know.I didn't know how I could tell my parents about my grades.間接問句，直述語序
Our coach cares about one thing most. Do we show up on time?What our coach cares about most is whether we show up on time.What 子句＋whether 子句
He had never played the guitar before. This surprised everyone.The fact that he had never played the guitar before surprised everyone.The fact that 當主詞
Will the school allow phones in class? Students are still discussing it.Whether the school will allow phones in class is still under discussion.whether 子句當主詞
There is a problem. Many students rely too much on answer apps.The problem is that many students rely too much on answer apps.先點出問題，再說內容

誤I wonder why is she always late.

正I wonder why she is always late.

誤Can you tell me where is the station?

正Can you tell me where the station is?

誤What he said make me angry.

正What he said made me angry.（名詞子句當主詞，動詞用單數；這裡是過去的事，用過去式）

6. 對比結構

  • While／Whereas + S + V, S + V：兩種情況並排對照。
  • Unlike + N, S + V：後面接名詞，而且比較的兩方要對等（人比人、物比物）。
  • Instead of + V-ing／N, S + V：不做 A，改做 B。
  • S + V + rather than + V／N：寧可 A 而不 B。
  • not A but B：不是 A 而是 B，A、B 詞性要一致。
  • Compared with + N, S + be + 比較級
基礎句升級句升級點
My brother likes to study late at night. I prefer to study early in the morning.While my brother likes to study late at night, I prefer to get up early and study before breakfast.兩人習慣對照
My old phone was slow. My new phone can open apps in a second.Unlike my old phone, which took ages to load anything, my new one opens apps in a second.Unlike＋關係子句
I didn't take a taxi. I rode a YouBike to the station.Instead of taking a taxi, I rode a YouBike to the station.Instead of＋V-ing
Success comes from daily practice. It doesn't come from talent.Success comes not from talent but from daily practice.not A but B，兩個介系詞片語對稱
Twenty years ago, most students used paper dictionaries. Today most students use apps.Twenty years ago, most students carried paper dictionaries, whereas today almost all students look up words on their phones.過去與現在對照

誤Unlike my sister, my room is always neat.（拿「姊姊」比「房間」）

正Unlike my sister's room, mine is always neat.／Unlike my sister, I always keep my room neat.

誤Instead of take the bus, I walked to school.

正Instead of taking the bus, I walked to school.（of 是介系詞，後接動名詞）

誤While I like math, but my sister likes English.

正While I like math, my sister likes English.（while 已經是連接詞，不能再加 but）

二、依功能分類的轉折詞

轉折詞用錯，最常見的不是「意思錯」，而是「標點錯」。標點規則由詞性決定，所以先分清楚四種詞性。

四種詞性決定標點

詞性常見的字標點與位置示範
對等連接詞and, but, so, or, yet連接兩個完整子句時，前面加逗號；正式作文少放句首The test was hard, but most of us passed.
從屬連接詞because, although, even though, when, while, since, if, unless, as soon as從屬子句在前：子句結束加逗號；在後：通常不加Although it was raining, the game went on.
連接副詞however, therefore, moreover, in addition, for example, as a result, in contrast不能連接兩個子句；前面用句號或分號，後面加逗號The bus was late. However, we still arrived on time.
介系詞（片語）because of, due to, despite, in spite of, such as, instead of, in addition to後面接名詞或動名詞，不接子句Despite the rain, the game went on.

四個最常被扣分的轉折錯誤

誤The bus was late, however, we arrived on time.

正The bus was late. However, we arrived on time.／The bus was late; however, we arrived on time.

誤Despite it was raining, we went hiking.

正Although it was raining, we went hiking.／Despite the rain, we went hiking.

誤Because I overslept. I missed the bus.（because 子句不能單獨成句）

正Because I overslept, I missed the bus.

誤Although he practiced every day, but he lost the game.

正Although he practiced every day, he lost the game.／He practiced every day, but he lost the game.

1. 舉例

轉折詞詞性位置與標點例句
for example／for instance連接副詞句首加逗號；或插在句中，前後加逗號Many club activities teach practical skills. For example, members of the cooking club learn to plan a budget for every dish.
such as介系詞接名詞列舉；前面通常加逗號Our school offers several language electives, such as Japanese, Korean, and French.
including介系詞接名詞，表「包括」，列的是一部分Five students, including two freshmen, made it to the final round.
Take ... for example.祈使句獨立成一句，後面接具體說明Take my cousin, for example. She saved enough money from her part-time job to pay for a trip to Japan.
A good example is ...完整句直接當主詞＋動詞使用A good example is the bike lane along the river, which is now full of commuters every morning.
  • such as 後面接名詞，不接完整句子：such as I go jogging 是錯的，要寫 such as jogging。
  • 要在句首舉例，用 For example,，不要用 Such as 開頭。
  • such as 列舉之後不再加 etc. 或 and so on，意思重複。
  • 作文裡不寫縮寫 e.g.，寫完整的 for example。

2. 因果

轉折詞詞性位置與標點例句
because／since／as從屬連接詞放句首時子句後加逗號Since the school library closes at five, we usually do our group work at a café nearby.
so對等連接詞前面加逗號，連接兩個子句I forgot my umbrella, so I waited at the bus stop until the rain stopped.
therefore／as a result連接副詞前面用句號或分號，後面加逗號Our club lost half its members last year. As a result, we had to cancel the spring performance.
because of／due to介系詞後接名詞The game was put off because of the heavy rain.
lead to／result in動詞片語直接當動詞，後接名詞或動名詞Staying up late often leads to poor concentration in class.
  • because 和 so 只能擇一，不可同時出現在一個句子。
  • therefore 是副詞，不能像 so 一樣用逗號連接兩句：I was sick, therefore I stayed home. 是錯的，要改成 I was sick; therefore, I stayed home. 或 I was sick, so I stayed home.
  • due to 後面接名詞，不接子句：due to it rained 錯，due to the rain 對。
  • 用 lead to、result in、cause 這類動詞寫因果，句子更短、更像高分寫法。
  • so ... that（如此…以致於）表結果；so that（以便）表目的，兩者不同。

3. 對比

轉折詞詞性位置與標點例句
but／yet對等連接詞前面加逗號The dorm room is small, yet it has everything I need.
while／whereas從屬連接詞對比兩方；放句中時前面常加逗號My father reads the news on paper, while my mother reads it on her tablet.
however連接副詞句首加逗號；放句中時前後都加逗號My parents, however, did not agree with my plan.
in contrast／on the other hand連接副詞句首加逗號City parks are often crowded. In contrast, the trails near my grandparents' home are quiet even on weekends.
unlike／compared with介系詞後接名詞，比較的兩方要對等Compared with last year's fair, this year's school fair raised twice as much money.
  • on the contrary 用在「否定前面的說法，事實正好相反」，不是單純比較兩個人：My sister is outgoing. On the contrary, I am shy. 不恰當，改成 In contrast, I am shy. 或 I, on the other hand, am shy.
  • while 有「當…的時候」和「然而」兩種意思；表對比時，前面加逗號比較不會被誤讀。
  • however 不等於 but：but 可以用逗號連接兩句，however 不行。

4. 讓步

轉折詞詞性位置與標點例句
although／though從屬連接詞放句首時子句後加逗號Although the trail was steep, my seventy-year-old grandfather reached the top before me.
even though從屬連接詞語氣比 although 強，接已發生的事實Even though I had studied for weeks, I still panicked when I saw the first question.
even if從屬連接詞接假設的情況；表未來時子句用現在式Even if it rains tomorrow, the sports day will go on in the gym.
despite／in spite of介系詞後接名詞或動名詞Despite feeling nervous, she gave a clear and confident speech.
still／even so副詞still 放一般動詞前、be 動詞與助動詞後；Even so, 放句首加逗號The tickets were expensive. Even so, we never regretted going.
  • although／though 不和 but 同時用，但可以和 still 搭配：Although ..., S still V.
  • 沒有 despite of 這種寫法；in spite of 才有 of。
  • though 可以放句尾當副詞，前面加逗號：The test was hard. I enjoyed it, though.（偏口語，作文中少量使用）

5. 補充

轉折詞詞性位置與標點例句
in addition／moreover／furthermore連接副詞句首加逗號Walking to school keeps me fit. In addition, it gives me time to plan my day.
besides副詞／介系詞副詞放句首加逗號；介系詞後接名詞或動名詞Besides cleaning the beach, the volunteers counted every plastic bottle they found.
in addition to介系詞to 是介系詞，後接名詞或動名詞In addition to taking notes, I record a short summary on my phone after each class.
also副詞放 be 動詞、助動詞之後，一般動詞之前The program is also open to parents.
not only ... but also對等連接詞前後接相同詞性The workshop was not only free but also surprisingly useful.
  • besides（此外、除了…還有）和 beside（在…旁邊）意思不同，拼字別混。
  • in addition to 的 to 不是不定詞：in addition to clean 錯，in addition to cleaning 對。
  • moreover、furthermore 語氣正式，一段用一次就夠；後面補充的內容要比前一點更有份量，否則不如用 and。
  • 正式作文避免用 Also, 放句首，改用 In addition, 或把 also 放進句中。

6. 時間

轉折詞詞性位置與標點例句
when／while／before／after／until從屬連接詞放句首時子句後加逗號After the class meeting ended, we stayed to decorate the classroom.
as soon as／the moment從屬連接詞「一…就」The moment the bell rang, everyone rushed to the cafeteria.
at first ... later副詞句首加逗號，表示前後改變At first, I hated running. Later, it became the best part of my day.
then／after that／eventually副詞不能連接兩個子句；用句號隔開，或加 andWe searched the park for an hour. Eventually, we found the puppy hiding under a bench.
during介系詞後接名詞During the summer vacation, I volunteered at a local library.
  • then 是副詞：I finished my homework, then I went to bed. 是錯的，改成 ..., and then I went to bed. 或 After I finished my homework, I went to bed.
  • during 接名詞，while 接子句：during I was in Japan 錯，改成 while I was in Japan 或 during my stay in Japan。
  • at first 表「起初（後來改變了）」，first 表「第一點、首先」；at last 表「等了很久終於」，列舉最後一點要用 finally。
  • 時間子句表未來時用現在式：When I go to college, I will live in a dorm.（不寫 When I will go）。

7. 結論

轉折詞詞性位置與標點例句
For these reasons,／That is why副詞片語／子句把結論和前面的理由綁在一起That is why I now leave my phone in the living room when I study.
In the end,副詞片語故事或經驗的結果In the end, we missed the last train and had to call my uncle for a ride.
Overall,／In short,副詞句首加逗號，概括前面的內容Overall, the trip taught me more about planning than any class had.
In conclusion,／To sum up,副詞片語長篇議論文的總結段才需要學測兩段式作文通常不需要，見下方提醒
  • 學測作文通常分兩段，至少 120 個單詞，建議寫 150–220 字，不必另寫結論段，也不必用 In conclusion 開頭；第二段最後一句直接收束即可。
  • in the end（結果、最後）用在敘事；in conclusion（總之）用在議論，不能互換。
  • 收尾句不要只把第一句換個說法重講一次，要加一點延伸：下一步的打算、一個建議，或這件事帶來的改變。

轉折詞的份量：一段 3–4 個就夠。句與句之間的連貫，另外還可以靠代名詞（this, these, such）和重複關鍵詞來完成（詳見 W2）。每句開頭都是 Moreover／However／Therefore，讀起來像在背清單，反而不自然。

三、具體化技巧

閱卷老師讀一段文字，很快就能分出「講道理」和「有畫面」。空泛的段落，內容面向通常只能得到中等評價；有一兩個清楚畫面的段落，才進得了高分。具體化的檢驗問題只有一個：這句話能不能拍成一個鏡頭？

具體化四把鑰匙

  • 數字：時間、次數、距離、金額、人數、分數。
  • 感官：看到、聽到、聞到、摸到、嘗到的東西。
  • 人物：一個特定的人（身分、稱呼）、他說的一句話、他的一個動作。
  • 場景：什麼時候、在哪裡、正在發生什麼。

「概括 → 具體」三步法

  1. 寫出概括句（主題句或觀點）。
  2. 追問：誰？何時？何地？多少？看到或聽到什麼？
  3. 選出最有說服力的一個細節，寫成 1–2 句；必要時再加一句說明它的意義（例如 This showed me that ...）。

1. 數字

誤I spend a lot of time on my phone.（不是文法錯，是內容空泛）

正On a typical school day, I spend about four hours on my phone, mostly watching short videos after ten p.m.

Getting to school takes me fifty minutes, including two bus transfers.概括說法：我家離學校很遠。

More than eighty students joined the beach cleanup, and in two hours we filled thirty bags with plastic bottles and old fishing nets.概括說法：很多學生參加了淨灘。

數字要合理，例如「一天念書 20 小時」只會讓人懷疑。數字寫法：一到九拼成英文字，10 以上可用阿拉伯數字（也可以 100 為界，全篇統一即可，詳見附錄 A）；句首的數字一律拼出來。

2. 感官

At the night market, vendors shouted prices over the sizzle of grilled squid, and the smell of stinky tofu drifted through the crowd.概括說法：夜市很熱鬧。

Before the speech contest, my palms were sweating, and I read the first line of my speech again and again.概括說法：比賽前我很緊張。

情緒不要只寫 very nervous／very happy，改寫「身體反應」或「動作」，讓讀者自己感受到。這是作文從「說」變成「演」的關鍵。

3. 人物

After I failed the first mock exam, Ms. Lin handed back my paper with a note: "Your ideas are good. Now work on your grammar."概括說法：老師鼓勵我。

At seventy-two, my grandfather still gets up at five every morning to water the vegetables in his backyard.概括說法：我爺爺很勤勞。

4. 場景

After the class party, paper cups and pizza boxes covered every desk, and a few balloons were still floating against the ceiling.概括說法：派對後教室很亂。

Every morning at 6:40, I squeeze into a packed MRT car, holding my backpack in front of me so that it does not hit anyone.概括說法：通勤很累。

具體化的四個陷阱

  • 離題的細節：細節要服務主題句。寫「通勤很累」，就不必描述早餐吃什麼。
  • 只堆細節，不說意義：細節之後，用一句話點出它證明了什麼。
  • 數字或事實不合理：與常識不符的數字會讓整段失去可信度。
  • 流水帳：I woke up. I brushed my teeth. I ate breakfast. 不是具體，是沒有選擇。只挑最能說明重點的那一刻。

四、精準用字：取代 make、get、have、do、good、bad、very

這七個字本身沒有錯，問題在於什麼都用它們，意思就模糊了。原則是：固定搭配照用（make a decision, get up, have breakfast, do homework），只有在意思不清楚時才換成精準的字。

五、套語黑名單 15 條

套語的問題是：放進任何題目都成立，等於沒說；閱卷老師一看就知道是背來的，還佔掉寶貴的字數。下面 15 條，左邊是常見套語，右邊是替代做法。

編號套語問題在哪改成什麼（示範）
1In a nutshell, ...口語又空泛；短文不需要「總而言之」直接寫結論內容：That is why I now keep my phone in another room while I study.
2Every coin has two sides.任何題目都能用，等於沒說直接點出兩面：Online shopping saves time, but it also makes impulse buying much easier.
3As the saying goes, ...用諺語代替自己的論點用自己的經驗說出道理：I learned this when I lost a whole weekend of work because I never saved a backup.
4With the rapid development of technology, ...大而無當的開場，和題目沒有直接關係從具體現象開始：In my class, almost everyone checks the bus arrival time on an app before leaving school.
5Nowadays, more and more people ...開場沒有資訊量說出誰、在哪裡、做什麼：Every Saturday morning, the trail behind my school is full of families with young children.
6As we all know, ...假設讀者都同意，論點反而變弱刪掉，直接陳述，再補一個證據或例子
7In my opinion, I think ...意思重複二選一：In my view, ... 或 I believe ...；也可以直接陳述
8Last but not least, ...已成固定套語用 Finally,，或點出份量：The most important reason, however, is ...
9There is no denying that ...語氣誇大，後面常接人人皆知的事直接寫主張，並附理由
10Time flies.和內容無關的感嘆寫出具體的時間與改變：In just two years, I have gone from a shy freshman to the captain of our debate team.
11... plays an important role in our daily life.只說重要，沒說怎麼重要說出具體作用：My phone is my alarm clock, my dictionary, and my bus schedule.
12It was an unforgettable experience.只下結論，沒有證據用細節證明難忘：Even now, I can still hear the crowd counting down the last ten seconds of the game.
13Only in this way can we make the world a better place.誇大、空洞，是背來的倒裝句寫出範圍小、做得到的結果：If every class did this, our school could use far less paper each semester.
14Different people have different opinions.沒有立場，浪費一句表明立場並給理由：I support the new rule because it gives us one quiet hour to finish homework.
15Do you know ...? Let me tell you.和讀者對話的口語開場，沒有內容第一句就寫出主題句：The best place to study in my town is a small library above the fish market.
套語測試：把這句話搬到另一個完全不同的題目裡。如果它還說得通，它就是套語，請刪掉或改成「只屬於這篇文章」的內容。

六、綜合示範：一段話的升級

主題：參加社團學到的事。先看基礎版，再看升級版。注意升級版只用了兩個「亮點句型」，其餘的進步來自具體化、轉折與用字。

基礎版（約 50 字）

I joined the school band last year. It was very hard. I had to practice a lot. I was very tired every day. But I learned many things. For example, I learned responsibility. It was an unforgettable experience. In a nutshell, joining the band made me a better person.

升級版（約 115 字）

When I joined the school band last September, I never expected it to take up so much of my time. Besides two rehearsals a week, I practiced the trumpet for thirty minutes every evening, so I often did not start my homework until nine. What tested me most, however, was the week before our first concert, when our section leader caught the flu. Having played for only three months, I suddenly had to lead the other four trumpet players through a difficult piece. When the final note faded and the audience began to clap, I realized that responsibility is not something you are taught; it is something you take on when others are counting on you.

  • 具體化：last year → last September；practice a lot → 每週兩次團練、每晚練 30 分鐘、作業常常拖到九點才開始；加入「組長得流感、要帶小號組另外四個人」的關鍵場景。
  • 亮點句型：(1) What tested me most ... was ...（強調）；(2) Having played for only three months, ...（分詞構句）。只有兩個，而且都放在故事的關鍵處。
  • 轉折：Besides 補充、however 插在句中標出轉折，when 子句交代時間。
  • 刪除：very hard、very tired、It was an unforgettable experience、In a nutshell 全部拿掉，改用事件本身證明「辛苦」與「成長」。
  • 收尾：不重複「我變成更好的人」，而是說出具體學到的道理，並用分號對照兩個子句。

八、本章自我檢核

  • 我寫的每個分詞構句，分詞的主詞和主要子句的主詞相同。
  • 非限定關係子句用 who／which，不用 that，而且前後有逗號。
  • 倒裝句的助動詞時態正確，後面接原形動詞。
  • 間接問句（why／how／where 子句）用直述語序。
  • however、therefore、as a result 前面用句號或分號，後面加逗號。
  • because 與 so、although 與 but 沒有出現在同一句。
  • such as、despite、because of、during 後面接的是名詞，不是子句。
  • 每段至少有一個具體細節：數字、感官、人物或場景。
  • 情緒用動作或身體反應表現，而不是只寫 very happy、very nervous。
  • 全篇沒有 very＋極端形容詞（例如 very exhausted）。
  • 全篇沒有任何一條黑名單套語。
  • 每段只有 1–2 個升級句，而且我能確定它們文法正確。
