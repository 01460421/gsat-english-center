21. (C) experimentbegan to ___ with bags made of paper and cloth：不定詞 to 後面要原形動詞；experiment with（用……做實驗）是固定搭配。下一句「把這些袋子放在火上，袋子就鼓起來往上飄」正是兄弟倆實驗的內容。
22. (F) thintoo ___ to breathe（太……而無法呼吸）中間要形容詞，形容 the air up there。thin 最常見的意思是「薄的、瘦的」，形容空氣時是延伸義「稀薄的」（thin air）：沒有人知道高空的情況，所以有人擔心那裡的空氣「稀薄」到無法呼吸。
23. (J) stand in foranimals would ___ human travelers：would 後面要原形動詞，受詞是 human travelers；stand in for（代替）是片語動詞。前面說沒有人知道高空的情況，所以決定先讓動物「代替」人類乘客上天；後面說羊的身體被認為和人類相似，正是選羊當替身的理由。
24. (E) similarbe similar to（和……相似）。fairly 是副詞，後面要形容詞；to a human’s 是 to a human’s body 的省略。羊的身體被認為和人類的「相當相似」，所以拿羊來測試高空對身體的影響；while the two birds were added for comparison（兩隻鳥是加進來作比較的）也呼應這個實驗設計。
25. (G) audiencea huge ___ of curious people：需要單數可數名詞，而且是「由很多人組成」的集合名詞；audience 指「一群觀眾」，這些好奇的民眾聚集在宮殿是為了觀看氣球升空。spectator 意思相近，但指一位觀眾，不能說 a huge spectator of curious people。
26. (B) spectatorthe most important ___ of all 需要單數名詞，冒號後說明這個人就是國王路易十六本人；句首 Among those watching（在觀看的人當中）表示他是「觀眾」之一。spectator（觀眾、旁觀者）指一個人，正好對應國王一人；audience 意思相近，但指一群觀眾，不能用來指國王一個人。
27. (D) crewthe balloon’s unusual ___ 需要名詞。crew 原指船或飛機上的「全體乘員、工作人員」，這裡借指搭乘氣球的三隻動物；後半句 all three animals were found alive 就是用另一個說法指同一群「乘員」（詞彙聯結）。說牠們是 unusual crew（不尋常的乘員）帶有幽默意味。
28. (H) settledhad ___ an important question 是過去完成式，需要過去分詞；選項中只有 settled 是過去分詞。settle 常用義是「安頓、定居」，settle a question 是延伸義「解決（問題）、使有定論」：冒號後就是答案——生物能在空中旅行後存活，呼應第二段的 a serious question to answer。
29. (I) confidencethe ___ they needed to take the next step 需要名詞；give sb. the confidence to V（給某人做……的信心）。動物平安降落，證明生物能在空中存活，兄弟倆因此有了進行下一步的「信心」——下一句兩個月後就有人搭乘氣球飛行。
30. (A) unlikelythe ___ heroes 中，冠詞 the 和名詞 heroes 之間需要形容詞。前半句 They made history（那兩個人創造了歷史），but 轉折：真正先上天的卻是羊、鴨和公雞，是誰也想不到的英雄。unlikely hero（意想不到的英雄）是常見說法。

1. Compared with traditional stores, stores run by machines have lower labor costs and longer business hours.
也可以寫：Compared to traditional stores, those operated by machines have lower labor costs and can stay open longer.「和…相比」放句首用 Compared with／to + 名詞：Compared with traditional stores（用過去分詞 compared，不用 compare 或 comparing），後面的述語用比較級 lower、longer。「由機器經營的商店」中文把修飾語放在名詞前面，英文用過去分詞片語後位修飾：stores run (operated) by machines；商店是「被」經營，run 是過去分詞（意思等於 stores that are run by machines 省略 that are，本題練習較精簡的分詞片語）。為了不和前面的 stores 重複，也可以寫 those run by machines。「人力成本較低」是 have lower labor costs（或 spend less on labor）；「營業時間也較長」可以接在 have 後面寫 and longer business hours，或寫成 and can stay open longer。 「由機器經營的商店」是前置修飾，英文改成名詞＋過去分詞片語 stores run by machines（「…的＋名詞」改後位修飾）。
2. After trying these stores, many senior citizens find that the more often they go, the more confident they feel.
也可以寫：After trying out such stores, many seniors find that the more often they go there, the more confident they become.「試過這種商店後」沒有主詞，試的人就是後面的「許多長輩」，英文用連接詞＋分詞 After trying these stores,（after 後面省略主詞時接 V-ing；也可以寫成分詞構句 Having tried these stores,），主要子句的主詞一定要是 many senior citizens（elderly people），否則會變成懸垂分詞。「發現自己越常去，就越有信心」是 find (that) 後面接「越…越…」：the more often they go, the more confident they feel；often、confident 要跟著 the more 移到各自子句的最前面，兩個 the 都不能省；「自己」不必譯出。 「試過這種商店後」沒有主詞，英文改成 After trying these stores（連接詞＋分詞），分詞的意義主詞就是主要子句的 many senior citizens。

  • 第 1 段　一個節慶禮盒拆開後，包裝堆成了一座小山。　細節：紙提袋、印著滿月的外盒、金色內盒與紅色緞帶；分成四格的托盤裡只有四塊月餅；女生皺著眉頭看著這堆包裝　收尾：包裝比食物多出好幾倍。
  • 第 2 段　說明過度包裝可能帶來的影響。　細節：影響：浪費資源、垃圾難以回收、讓禮盒變貴；自己家裡收到禮盒、處理包裝的經驗；你的看法：禮盒應該怎麼包裝、商家與消費者可以怎麼做　收尾：提出你認為合適的送禮方式。
參考範文（322 字）

A festival gift box has just been unwrapped on a living room table, and what remains looks less like a present than a small mountain of waste. Piled on the table are a paper shopping bag, a stiff outer box printed with a full moon, a shiny golden inner box, and a long red ribbon that once tied everything together. Beside the pile, a plastic tray divided into four sections holds just four small mooncakes, one in each section. Compared with such a colorful pile, the snacks almost look like an afterthought. Sitting at the table, a girl frowns as she looks from the mountain of packaging to the tiny cakes, perhaps wondering why so little food needs so much protection.

Such excessive packaging has effects that go far beyond one messy table. The most obvious is waste: most of these boxes, trays, and ribbons are thrown away within minutes, and because many of them combine paper with plastic or metal foil, they are difficult to recycle. When this happens in millions of households during every Mid-Autumn Festival and Lunar New Year, cities are left with mountains of extra garbage to handle. The cost also falls on consumers, who end up paying for layers they will never use. Last year, for instance, my family received five gift boxes, and the packaging filled two large recycling bags while the snacks were gone within two days. Admittedly, attractive packaging serves a purpose. A beautiful box shows respect for the person who receives it, and in a culture that values gift-giving, appearance matters. However, respect should not be measured in layers of cardboard. In my view, companies should design boxes that use fewer layers and easily recycled materials, and consumers should reward them by choosing simpler gifts. In the end, if both sides change their habits, a festival gift can carry the same warm wishes without placing a heavy burden on the environment.

圖中「一座小山」與「只有四塊月餅」是最強的對比，第一段寫清楚，第二段就能自然接到浪費與看法；想寫得更深入，可以讓步承認漂亮的包裝代表心意，再說明心意不必靠層層包裝。
