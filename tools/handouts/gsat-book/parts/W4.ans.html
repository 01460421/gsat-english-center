W4　同一段落三個等級示範

W4-01 基礎。說明：(1) 有文法錯誤：There was many students 應為 There were many students；(2) 兩個 very，內容籠統，讀者不知道你讀了什麼、多累；(3) 三句都是簡單句，用 and 串接。要升到穩健：先改錯，再補一個具體細節，例如讀了哪一科、讀到幾點。
W4-02 穩健。說明：文法正確，用了 so 表結果、Although 表讓步，邏輯清楚；但細節仍偏一般（other students、a little noisy），讀完沒有畫面。要升到高分：把「擠」和「吵」寫成具體場景，比較 W4-03 就知道差別。
W4-03 高分。說明：(1) 細節具體又有趣：七點、只剩一個空位、旁邊男生躲在課本後吃飯糰、閉館廣播；(2) 句構有變化：分詞片語 quietly eating …、句首過去分詞 Squeezed between …、關係子句 the only empty chair I could find；(3) 用字精準：managed to 帶出「在不利情況下還是完成」的意思。
W4-04 穩健。說明：立場清楚、結構完整、沒有錯誤，所以不是基礎；但兩個理由都很籠統（express our personalities、help us focus），沒有任何例子，而且 First, … Second, … 是最機械的排列方式。要升到高分：每個理由附一個學校生活的實例，並用句意銜接取代編號。
W4-05 基礎。說明：(1) Every coin has two sides. 是罐頭句，內容分不會加；(2) good points and bad points 沒說是哪些優缺點；(3) in a correct way 不自然，也沒有內容，可改為 use them wisely 並具體說明怎麼做。三句加起來等於什麼都沒說。
W4-06 高分。說明：(1) 第一句用 yet 做出「不花錢卻有效」的對比，論點清楚；(2) 第二句用一個具體人物（平常數學課睡覺的男生）當證據，比列理由更有說服力；(3) 句構多樣：動名詞主詞 Letting students …、名詞子句 how we feel、關係子句、形容詞片語 proud of …、過去完成式 had found。
W4-07 參考答案：Broken umbrellas, fallen branches, and a knocked-over scooter are scattered across the flooded street.說明：關鍵是把 many things 換成三樣看得見的東西，並用 be scattered across 寫出「散落一地」的狀態。看圖題用現在式，寫成 were 會被視為時態錯誤。可接受的其他寫法：The street is covered with leaves and branches, and a broken umbrella lies upside down in a puddle. 常見錯誤：There are many broken things.（換湯不換藥）；fall branches（應為 fallen branches）。
W4-08 參考答案：When I opened the door, I found my dog waiting right behind it, his tail wagging so hard that his whole body shook.說明：find + 受詞 + V-ing 表示「發現某人正在做某事」；句尾 his tail wagging … 是獨立分詞構句，用尾巴的動作表現狗的興奮，不必寫 he was happy。可接受的其他寫法：Opening the door, I saw my dog sitting by the shoe rack, wagging his tail as if he had been waiting all afternoon. 常見錯誤：Opening the door, my dog was waiting for me.（分詞構句的主詞必須是「我」，否則變成狗開門，屬於句構錯誤）。
W4-09 參考答案：The math test was so hard that I left the last three questions blank and stared at the clock for the final ten minutes.說明：so + 形容詞 + that 帶出結果；用「空著三題、盯著時鐘」這兩個動作讓讀者自己感受到沮喪，比 I was sad 有力。可接受的其他寫法：It was such a difficult test that I didn't say a word on the bus ride home. 常見錯誤：such hard test（such 後面要 a/an + 形容詞 + 名詞）；so hard that I was very sad（結構對了，內容沒升級）。
W4-10 參考答案：My grandmother can turn leftover rice and a few eggs into a dinner the whole family fights over, and she has patiently taught me how to make most of the dishes I know today.說明：用 turn A into B 具體展現廚藝，取代 very good at cooking；用 has taught me how to make most of the dishes 說明「怎麼幫我」，取代 helps me a lot。可接受的其他寫法：My grandmother is an excellent cook who never measures anything, and every winter break she lets me help her make dumplings for the whole family. 常見錯誤：只把 very good 換成 extremely excellent（重複又不自然）；She cooks delicious.（delicious 是形容詞，不能修飾動詞，可改為 Her cooking is delicious.）。
W4-11 參考答案：Although it rained all morning, our class still showed up at the beach in raincoats and filled twenty bags with trash by noon.說明：Although 子句交代不利條件，主要子句用 still 強調「照樣去」，再用數字（二十袋、中午前）寫出結果。可接受的其他寫法：Despite the morning rain, all thirty of us went ahead with the beach cleanup and collected far more plastic than we had expected. 常見錯誤：Although it rained all morning, but we …（although 和 but 不能同時用）；Despite it rained（despite 後面接名詞或 V-ing，不接子句）。
W4-12 參考答案：Since that trip, I have stopped asking my parents to plan everything for me; last month, I even booked the train tickets for our family trip to Hualien.說明：Since that trip 搭配現在完成式，表示改變延續到現在；分號後用一個實際行動當證據，比 we should be independent 這種對全人類說教的句子具體得多。主詞也從 we 改成 I，回到自己的經驗。可接受的其他寫法：That trip gave me the confidence to travel on my own, and I now take the train to visit my grandmother every summer without my parents. 常見錯誤：Since that trip, I stopped …（since 表「自從」時，主要子句通常用現在完成式；描述現在的習慣或狀態也可以用現在式，例如示範二高分版的 Since that night, I no longer …，但不能用過去式）。
W4-13 高分版範例（125 字）：

The moment Kevin arrived at the school gate, he reached back for his bag and felt nothing but his own jacket. His heart sank. Inside the bag was the history report due that morning, along with his wallet. Without wasting a second, he called the bus company's hotline and gave them the route number and the time he had gotten off. Twenty minutes later, the same bus pulled up across the street, and the driver held the bag out the window with a grin. Kevin thanked him over and over and sprinted to class just as the bell rang. From that day on, he always set an alarm on his phone to go off one stop before school, just in case he dozed off again.

說明：先改錯，再從四個面向升級。
  • 改錯：故事全用過去式。find → found；his bag is not on his back 與 his report is in the bag 的 is → was；runs → ran；say thank you → said thank you；bus station（總站）→ 路邊站牌應為 bus stop。
  • 內容：基礎版的情節有斷層：司機怎麼會剛好把書包交給他？高分版補上「打客服電話、說出路線和下車時間」，結局才合理。也寫出書包裡有什麼，讓「慌張」有理由。
  • 組織：發現 → 反應 → 行動 → 結果 → 後續，一句一個步驟；刪掉 From this story, we know we should be careful. 這種說教，改用 Kevin 之後的具體改變收尾。
  • 句構：The moment …（一…就…）、倒裝 Inside the bag was …、介系詞＋動名詞 Without wasting a second、過去完成式 had gotten off，再穿插短句 His heart sank. 製造節奏。
  • 用字：very worried → His heart sank.；went to school on time → sprinted to class just as the bell rang；very happy and say thank you → thanked him over and over。
  • 其他可行的結局：同學在 LINE 群組幫忙問、公車到總站後司機把書包交到失物招領處、Kevin 最後遲交報告但學到教訓。只要合理、有細節、有收尾都可以。
  • 常見失分：現在式和過去式混用；結局只寫 He got his bag back. 沒交代怎麼拿回來；把圖 1、圖 2 的內容又寫一遍，浪費字數。
W4-14 高分版範例（127 字）：

The table, based on a survey of 600 students at a high school, reveals a clear pattern: the older students get, the less they sleep. On school nights, tenth graders sleep an average of 7.1 hours, but the figure drops to 6.5 hours for eleventh graders and to just 5.8 hours for twelfth graders. Even more worrying is the share of students who sleep less than six hours a night, which climbs from 15 percent among tenth graders to 46 percent among seniors, or nearly one in two. The reasons for staying up late also change over the three years. While tenth graders stay up mainly to scroll through their phones, eleventh graders are kept busy by cram school and homework, and twelfth graders by exam preparation.

說明：先改錯，再從四個面向升級。
  • 改錯：Grade 12 students only sleeps → sleep；Many students sleep less than six hours, it is very serious. 是逗號硬接兩句；The reasons is → are。
  • 內容：先用一句話歸納整體趨勢（the older students get, the less they sleep），再挑重點數字支持；把 46% 換算成「將近每兩個高三生就有一個」，讓數字有意義；三欄資料都用到，沒有漏掉「晚睡原因」。
  • 組織：趨勢 → 平均時數 → 睡眠不足比例 → 原因變化，由大到小。刪掉 We should sleep more.，建議屬於第二段；圖表段只描述、不下指令。
  • 句構：冒號帶出歸納；the + 比較級 …, the + 比較級 …；倒裝 Even more worrying is …；While … 對比；最後一句省略重複的動詞（and twelfth graders by exam preparation）。
  • 用字：描述數據的動詞要有變化：drops to、climbs from … to …；an average of、the share of students who … 是圖表題的實用搭配。This table is about sleep. 改為 reveals a clear pattern。
  • 其他可行寫法：開頭也可以直接點出最驚人的數字（Nearly half of the seniors surveyed sleep less than six hours a night.），再往回說明趨勢。
  • 常見失分：把表格每一格數字照念一遍，沒有歸納；數字前後不一致或抄錯；在描述段就開始寫原因分析或建議。
W4-15 高分版範例（141 字）：

I fully support replacing our daily nap time with a free period. For many of us, the thirty-minute nap is not real rest at all. Lying face down on a desk often leaves me with a stiff neck and a red mark on my forehead, and I usually feel even more tired afterward. A free period, on the other hand, would let students decide what they actually need. A classmate on the basketball team could practice free throws, while someone preparing for a math contest could work through old contest problems in the library. Those who truly need sleep would lose nothing, since they could still rest at their desks. In other words, the change would turn a fixed rule into a real choice, and students who make their own choices are more likely to return to class awake and focused.

說明：先改錯，再從四個面向升級。
  • 改錯：I agree this idea → I agree with this idea 或 I support this idea；Because nap time is boring. 是不完整句（只有從屬子句）；we can do many things, for example, we can … 與 some students still want to sleep, they can … 都是逗號硬接兩句。
  • 內容：理由從「午睡很無聊」升級為「趴睡根本休息不到」，並附上自己的具體感受（脖子痠、額頭壓出紅印）；同時預先回應反方（想睡的人怎麼辦），論點更完整。例子用同學而不用自己，把「我會怎麼運用」留給第二段，兩段不重複。
  • 組織：立場 → 理由一（午睡沒效）→ 理由二（自由時間的好處＋兩個例子）→ 回應反方 → 收尾句。on the other hand 標出對比，In other words 帶出總結。
  • 句構：動名詞主詞 Lying face down on a desk …；leave + 人 + with …；假設語氣 would let、could practice（學校還在考慮，用 would/could 表示假設的結果）；while 對比；since 表原因。
  • 用字：I agree → I fully support；do many things → 具體的 practice free throws、work through practice problems；It is good for students → return to class awake and focused。
  • 反對立場也可以拿高分：例如主張午睡能讓下午的課更專心，或擔心自由時間太吵影響想休息的同學。關鍵仍是理由具體、有例子、有回應反方。
  • 常見失分：立場前後搖擺（一下支持、一下反對）；只寫 it is good、it is bad 而沒有原因；把第二段「我會怎麼用」整段提前寫完。
W4-16 高分版範例（126 字，不含稱呼）：

Dear Emma,

Our whole class has been counting down the days until you arrive, so let me give you a preview of a typical day here. School starts at 7:30, but most of us show up a little early to sweep the classroom and hallway, since students here do the cleaning themselves. After four morning classes, we eat lunch at our desks, and then the lights go off for a thirty-minute nap. It may sound strange, but you will probably be grateful for it by the second week. Afternoons are for subjects like P.E. and music, and when the last bell rings at around four, the campus suddenly comes alive with club activities. I am in the dance club, and you are more than welcome to join us.

說明：先改錯，再從四個面向升級。
  • 改錯：many student → many students；Our school is very big and beautiful, I think you will like it. 是逗號硬接兩句。
  • 內容：基礎版只有時間表（七點半上學、很多課、午睡）；高分版挑出對外國學生「最不一樣」的細節：學生自己打掃、在座位上吃午餐、關燈午睡，並預先說明她可能的感受。這才是收信人真正想知道的資訊。
  • 組織：熱情開場（全班在倒數）→ 早上 → 中午 → 下午 → 放學後社團，依時間順序；最後用邀請她加入社團收尾，語氣親切，也自然接到第二段的提醒事項。
  • 句構：現在完成進行式 has been counting down；since 說明原因；It may sound strange, but … 先讓步再轉折；when 子句搭配 comes alive。
  • 用字：I am very happy you will come → counting down the days until you arrive；very busy、many classes → 具體的作息；you are more than welcome to … 是寫給朋友的自然邀請。
  • 其他可行內容：升旗或朝會、福利社、午餐團購、放學後的晚自習。挑兩三個「美國學校少見」的特色寫細，比全部列出更好。
  • 常見失分：用 How are you? I am fine. 這類寒暄占字數；把第二段的「要準備什麼」提前寫；信末忘了署名 Jamie，或簽了自己的真名。
