T2c　高頻句型 21–30：讓步、條件、因果、數量與搭配

T2-41 No matter how late you come home, you should text your parents in advance.關鍵結構：no matter how ＋形容詞／副詞＋主詞＋動詞，late 必須緊跟在 how 後面。也可寫 However late you come home，come 可換成 get。常見錯誤：No matter you come home how late（語序錯）、No matter how late you will come home（讓步子句表未來用現在式）；兩者都屬結構錯誤，該半句不給分。
T2-42 Although the midterm exam covered a lot of material, most of my classmates finished reviewing it within a week.關鍵結構：although 子句＋主要子句，中文的「還是」可加 still，但不能加 but。finish 後接 V-ing；「在一週內」用 within a week。其他寫法：Even though there was a lot to study for the midterm, most students still finished their review in a week.／Despite the large amount of material on the midterm, most of my classmates reviewed all of it within a week. 常見扣分：although … but 並用；finish to review；「範圍很大」直譯成 the range was very big，語意不清。
T2-43 If I had saved my files to the cloud, I wouldn't have lost all my work when my computer crashed.關鍵結構：與過去事實相反，if 子句用 had ＋ p.p.，和主要子句的 wouldn't have lost 對應。其他寫法：had backed up my files online、had stored my files in the cloud。常見錯誤：saved（只退一格，變成與現在事實相反）、would have saved（if 子句不用 would）。時態錯誤會讓整個條件語意單位失分。
T2-44 As long as you practice for twenty minutes every day, your English listening skills will improve noticeably within a semester.關鍵結構：「只要」用 as long as，子句用現在式，主要子句用 will。「明顯進步」可用 improve noticeably／improve a lot／make clear progress。其他寫法：If you practice twenty minutes a day, you will see a clear improvement in your English listening within one semester. 常見扣分：As long as you will practice（子句用 will）；your English listening will progress obviously（obviously 不修飾進步程度，搭配不自然）；寫成 only if 意思變成「唯有」。
T2-45 This app can not only translate menus but also tell you where the nearby convenience stores are.關鍵結構：not only 和 but also 後面都接原形動詞（前面有 can），兩邊對等。後半的「…在哪裡」是名詞子句，用直述句語序 where … are，不寫 where are the stores。其他寫法：show you where to find convenience stores nearby。常見錯誤：but also tells you（can 後面要原形）、where are the convenience stores（名詞子句倒裝錯誤）。
T2-46 Neither students nor teachers are allowed to use their phones in the exam room.關鍵結構：neither A nor B 當主詞，動詞跟靠近的 teachers，所以用複數 are；句中已有 neither，不可再加 not。「不可以」用 be allowed to 的否定意義（由 neither 承擔）。其他寫法：Neither students nor teachers may use their cell phones during the exam. 常見扣分：Neither students nor teachers are not allowed（雙重否定，意思相反）；Both students and teachers are not allowed（易被理解成「不是兩者都」，本題指定用 neither … nor 也會被視為未達要求）。
T2-47 The earlier you start preparing for the GSAT, the less panicked you will feel before the exam.關鍵結構：後半也要以 the ＋比較級開頭，形容詞 panicked 跟著 the less 移到前面。其他寫法：the less you will panic、the calmer you will be、the less stressed you will feel。常見錯誤：you will feel less panicked（沒有用 the 比較級對應，結構不完整）；the less you will feel panic（語序不自然）。
T2-48 The more time we spend on social media, the less time we spend chatting with our families face to face.關鍵結構：the more ＋名詞（time）＋主詞＋動詞，the less ＋名詞＋主詞＋動詞；spend time V-ing。後半第二個 spend 可換成 have：the less time we have to talk with our families in person。常見扣分：The more we spend time …（time 要跟著 more 移到前面）；we will chat less with our families（後半沒有 the 比較級）；spend time to chat（spend 後接 V-ing）。
T2-49 After I moved to Taipei, it took me several months to get used to taking the crowded MRT.關鍵結構：「習慣」是 get used to，to 是介系詞，後面接 V-ing；get 表示「變得習慣」的過程，和「花了好幾個月才」最搭配。其他寫法：get used to riding crowded MRT trains、get used to the crowded MRT（直接接名詞）。常見錯誤：get used to take（接原形）、used to take（變成「以前常搭」）。
T2-50 This classroom used to be a computer lab, but now it is used as an office for school clubs.關鍵結構：「以前是」用 used to be；「被用來當作」用被動 be used as ＋名詞。其他寫法：This room was once a computer classroom; now it serves as the club office.／… but it is now used for club meetings and activities. 常見扣分：is used to an office（漏 as，語意變成「習慣辦公室」）；was used to be（把「以前」和被動混在一起）。
T2-51 As soon as I got home, I put my phone in a drawer and started on my homework.關鍵結構：「一…就…」用 as soon as；主要子句是過去式（put、started），子句也用過去式 got。其他寫法：The moment I got home、Once I got home、As soon as I arrived home。注意：home 當副詞時直接接在 arrive、get 後面，arrived home 比 arrived at home 自然；got to home 則是錯的。常見錯誤：When I got home immediately（語序與用字都不自然）。
T2-52 Since I started high school, I have been using my commuting time to memorize English vocabulary.關鍵結構：since ＋過去式子句，主要子句用現在完成進行式（強調「一直」持續到現在），也可用現在完成式 have used。「利用…來…」用 use … to V 或 make use of … to V。其他寫法：Ever since I entered senior high school, I have made use of my time on the bus to learn English words. 常見扣分：Since I started high school, I used …（主要子句用過去式，時態錯）；memorize English words by commuting time（介系詞誤用）。
T2-53 Due to a breakdown on the MRT line, many students were late for school.關鍵結構：題目要求名詞片語開頭，所以用 due to／because of ＋名詞。其他寫法：Because of an MRT breakdown、Owing to the MRT malfunction。若不限名詞片語，也可寫子句 Because the MRT broke down。常見錯誤：Due to the MRT broke down（due to 後接子句）；Because of the MRT was broken（同樣錯誤）。
T2-54 Looking down at your phone for long periods of time may lead to neck pain and even make your eyesight worse.關鍵結構：動名詞片語當主詞（Looking down at your phone），動詞 may lead to ＋名詞；「甚至使…變差」用 make ＋受詞＋形容詞比較級，和 lead to 用 and 平行連接，都接在 may 後面用原形。其他寫法：Spending hours looking down at a phone can cause neck pain and even damage your eyesight. 常見扣分：lead to neck hurt（hurt 不當名詞用）；makes your eyesight become worse（may 後要原形，become 可省）；Look down at phone for a long time may …（原形動詞不能當主詞、漏冠詞）。
T2-55 The number of students at our school who ride bikes to school has doubled in the past three years.關鍵結構：主詞是 the number，動詞用單數；「我們學校」是一個語意單位，不可漏譯（at our school 也可寫 in our school）；「在過去三年」配現在完成式 has doubled；「增加了一倍」用 double 最簡潔。其他寫法：has increased twofold、has increased by 100 percent。常見錯誤：have doubled（主詞看錯成 students）；has increased one time（中文直譯，意思不清）；doubled（有 in the past three years 時，用現在完成式較佳）。
T2-56 A survey shows that nearly two thirds of the students in our class use their phones for more than four hours a day.關鍵結構：show that ＋名詞子句；「將近三分之二」是 nearly two thirds，分母加 s；of 後面是複數 students，所以動詞用複數 use；「超過四小時」用 more than four hours。其他寫法：According to a survey, almost two-thirds of my classmates spend over four hours on their phones every day. 常見扣分：two third／two-three（分數寫法錯）；uses（主詞一致錯）；more than four hours every day use phone（語序與冠詞錯）。
T2-57 My brother is hardly ever late for school, but he often forgets to bring his lunch box.關鍵結構：be 動詞句的頻率副詞放在 be 後面（is hardly ever late）；一般動詞句的頻率副詞放在動詞前（often forgets）。「幾乎從不」用 hardly ever 或 almost never。forget to V 表示「忘記去做」。其他寫法：is almost never late、often forgets（直接接 his lunch box 也可）。常見錯誤：hardly ever is late、forgets often（副詞位置錯）；forgets bringing（意思變成「忘記帶過了」）。
T2-58 My parents seldom ask me to rank first in my class, but they always remind me to be honest.關鍵結構：seldom、always 都放在一般動詞前；ask ＋受詞＋ to V、remind ＋受詞＋ to V。「考第一名」可寫 rank first、come in first、be at the top of my class。其他寫法：My parents rarely expect me to be the top student, but they keep reminding me to be honest. 常見扣分：My parents ask me seldom（副詞位置錯）；get the first name（中文直譯）；remind me being honest（remind 後接 to V）。
T2-59 Everyone should make good use of the library's resources.關鍵結構：make use of ＋名詞，「好好」用 good 或 full 修飾 use。其他寫法：make full use of、take full advantage of。常見錯誤：make good use the library's resources（漏 of）；use well（語意可懂但不夠精準，高分群應寫出固定搭配）。
T2-60 Taking part in volunteer work has not only given me valuable experience but also taught me how to get along with people of different ages.關鍵結構：動名詞片語當主詞（Taking part in volunteer work），動詞用單數；現在完成式表示到目前為止的收穫；not only 和 but also 後面都接過去分詞（given／taught），對等。「和…相處」用 get along with。其他寫法：Volunteering not only gave me valuable experience but also taught me to get along with people of all ages. 常見扣分：Participate in volunteer activities 當主詞（原形動詞不能當主詞）；get along well people（漏 with）；not only … but also teach（兩邊時態不對等）；「獲得經驗」寫成 learn experience。
T2-61 Although the cram school is far from my home, I go there three times a week.錯誤：although 和 but 並用。刪掉 but，或刪掉 although 改成 The cram school is far from my home, but I go there three times a week. 只能二擇一。
T2-62 If I had known about the quiz, I would have studied last night.錯誤：if 子句不用 would。與過去事實相反時，if 子句用 had ＋ p.p.。也可寫倒裝 Had I known about the quiz, I would have studied last night.
T2-63 The number of students who bring their own cups has increased this semester.錯誤：主詞是 the number，動詞用單數 has。who bring 的 bring 是修飾 students，維持複數不變。若改成 A growing number of students bring their own cups，動詞才用複數。
T2-64 Since I joined the guitar club, I have made a lot of new friends.錯誤：since 子句（過去式）搭配的主要子句要用現在完成式，表示從那時到現在的累積結果。made 改為 have made。
T2-65 I am used to staying up late, so I often feel sleepy in the morning.錯誤：be used to（習慣於）的 to 是介系詞，後接 V-ing。若想說「我以前常熬夜」，則要寫 I used to stay up late，但後半的現在式 feel 就不合邏輯，所以本句應改成 staying。
T2-66 The more English news you read, the better you will understand the world.錯誤：後半沒有用 the ＋比較級。前半最好也把名詞 English news 跟著 the more 移到前面；保留 The more you read English news 也可接受，但後半一定要改成 the better you will understand the world。
T2-67 Although I'm not used to speaking in front of the whole class, the more I practice, the more confident I become.句型：讓步（although，不加 but）＋ be used to V-ing ＋ the more …, the more …。評分重點：although 子句 1 個單位、the more 句型 1 個單位。其他寫法：I'm not used to speaking in front of my classmates, but the more I practice, the more confident I feel.（用 but 就不能再用 although）。常見扣分：not used to speak；the more I practice, I become more confident；the more I am confident。
T2-68 If I had left home earlier, I wouldn't have missed the express train, and I wouldn't have had to wait on the platform for forty minutes.句型：與過去事實相反的假設語氣。評分重點：if 子句 had left、主要子句 wouldn't have missed、wouldn't have had to wait 三處時態都要對。「直達車」可用 express train 或 direct train。其他寫法：Had I left home a little earlier, I wouldn't have missed the direct train or had to wait forty minutes on the platform. 常見扣分：If I left home earlier（只退一格）；wouldn't have to wait（第二個主要子句也要用完成式 wouldn't have had to wait）；in the platform（月台用 on）。
T2-69 Since our school started promoting plastic reduction, the number of students who bring their own utensils has almost doubled.句型：since 子句＋現在完成式、the number of ＋單數動詞、double 表倍數。「減塑」可寫 reducing plastic use／cutting down on plastic。其他寫法：Ever since our school began encouraging students to use less plastic, nearly twice as many students have been bringing their own utensils. 常見扣分：have almost doubled（主詞一致錯）；almost increased one time（中文直譯）；主要子句用過去式。
T2-70 Because final exams are coming up, our club seldom practices on weekdays, but everyone always shows up on time on weekends.句型：原因子句（because ＋子句；若寫 due to 要改成名詞 Due to the upcoming final exams）＋頻率副詞位置（seldom practices、always shows up）。「出席」用 show up 或 attend。其他寫法：With final exams approaching, our club rarely practices on weekdays, but all the members always arrive on time on weekends. 常見扣分：Because …, so …；our club practices seldom；everyone always show up（everyone 是單數，要用 shows）。
