R1　閱讀測驗題型與解法

閱讀測驗有三篇文章，每篇 4 題，共 12 題、24 分，一題 2 分，是整張考卷配分最高的大題。文章的題材每年不同，題目的問法卻很固定：歸納起來只有六種，錯的選項也只有五種錯法。這個單元先教你認出誘答，再一種一種拆解題型，接著講讀文章的方法、同義改寫，以及近年常見的圖表與多文本。

讀完之後，每一題你都要能說出三件事：這是哪一種題、答案在文章的哪一句、另外三個選項各錯在哪裡。

一、先認識誘答：錯的選項只有五種錯法

每一題有三個錯的選項。它們不是隨便寫的，而是拿文章的內容「加工」出來的，加工的方法就是下面五種。先讀這段短文：

Three years ago, Greenfield High School removed the vending machines that sold bottled drinks and set up water stations around the campus. Since then, the amount of plastic waste collected at the school has dropped by nearly half, although a few students still complain that the stations are too far from the gym.

誘答類型選項會這樣寫錯在哪裡、怎麼檢查
太廣Schools everywhere are cutting down on plastic waste.文章只講一所學校。主旨題常見：選項的範圍比文章大。
太窄Some students think the water stations are too far from the gym.文章確實有寫，但只是一個細節，當主旨就錯。主旨題常見：選項只對得上某一句。
文中沒提The water stations were paid for by parents.聽起來合理，但文章找不到任何一句當證據。
說反Plastic waste at the school has nearly doubled.減半說成加倍。核對方向：增或減、肯定或否定、先或後、因或果。
偷換對象A few teachers complain that the vending machines are too far away.抱怨的是學生，太遠的是飲水機。核對主詞、受詞、時間、地點、數字。

正The school now produces much less plastic waste than before.（dropped by nearly half 換成 much less，字不同，意思相同。）

刪去法要留下理由

  • 每刪一個選項，就在旁邊寫一個字：廣、窄、無、反、換。
  • 寫不出理由的選項不要刪。剩下兩個難以取捨時，回文章找證據句，不要憑印象。
  • 「太窄」只在主旨題算錯；在細節題裡，「文章有寫的細節」反而可能就是答案。先看清楚題目問什麼。
  • 錯的選項裡常有文章出現過的字，看起來很眼熟。字眼出現過不代表選項正確；正解多半把原文換一種說法（見第四節）。
  • 後面各題型提到的「拼湊」是偷換對象的變形；「過度推論」「常識陷阱」「太極端」都是文中沒提的變形：文章沒有說到那個程度。

二、六種題型

看到題幹，先判斷它是哪一種題，因為六種題的答案藏在文章不同的地方。下面每一種都照同樣的順序說明：常見問法 → 答案在哪裡 → 誘答怎麼設計 → 示範。

題型 1　主旨與標題

常見問法

  • What is the passage mainly about?
  • What is the main idea of the passage?
  • Which of the following is the best title for the passage?
  • What is the second paragraph mainly about?（問單一段落的主旨）

答案在哪裡

  • 主旨＝第一段的主題句＋各段第一句＋最後一段的結論。這三處說的是同一件事。
  • 文章如果用故事、問句或「一般人以為……」開頭，那是引子。主旨在引子之後，常在 but、however 後面。
  • 蓋得住測驗：把選項拿去對每一段。每一段都跟它有關，留下；有一段跟它無關，是太窄；選項提到文章沒寫的範圍，是太廣。
  • 標題題多一個要求：好標題同時含有「主題」（講什麼）和「重點」（講它的哪一面）。

誘答

  • 太窄：只是某一段的內容，或只是開頭的引子。
  • 太廣：把主題放大成整個領域。
  • 偷換重點：主題對了，面向不對。文章講好處，選項說成歷史或做法。

示範

When a toaster stops working, most people simply throw it away and buy a new one. At a repair café, however, volunteers help visitors fix their broken items for free. The first such café opened in Amsterdam in 2009, and the idea has since spread to thousands of communities around the world. Visitors do not just drop off their things and leave; they sit beside the volunteers and learn how the repair is done. Supporters say the cafés do more than save money. They keep usable goods out of landfills, pass on practical skills, and bring neighbors together.

Which of the following is the best title for the passage?

(A) How to Fix a Broken Toaster(B) Repair Cafés: Mending Things, Connecting People(C) The World's Growing Waste Problem(D) Amsterdam: A City of Volunteers
  1. 第一句的 toaster 是引子；第二句用 however 轉折，主題 repair café 才出現。
  2. 替後面幾句寫標籤：起源與擴散 → 怎麼運作 → 好處（省錢、減少垃圾、傳授技能、拉近鄰居）。
  3. 對選項：(A) 太窄，烤麵包機只是引子，文章也沒教怎麼修。(C) 太廣，垃圾問題只在最後一句的 landfills 沾到邊。(D) 偷換重點，阿姆斯特丹只是第一家的地點。(B) 有主題（repair cafés）也有重點（修東西、連結人），蓋得住每一句。

題型 2　細節

常見問法（六種裡題數最多，統計見 S0）

  • According to the passage, which of the following is true about …?
  • Which of the following is NOT mentioned in the passage?
  • Why did …?／How does …?／When was …?

答案在哪裡：三步定位

  1. 圈出題幹的定位字：專有名詞、數字、年份、引號裡的字、段落編號。
  2. 回文章找到這個字（或它的同義說法），讀那一句，再讀前後各一句。
  3. 先用自己的話回答，再看選項。選意思相同的，不選字面最像的。
  • 細節題的順序大致跟著文章走：第 2 題的答案通常在第 3 題的答案前面。找不到時，從上一題的位置往後找。
  • NOT／EXCEPT 題：三個選項是文章有寫的。先把 NOT 圈起來，逐一回文章打勾，沒得勾的就是答案。這種題最花時間，可以留到該篇最後做。

誘答

  • 偷換對象（最多）：甲做的事說成乙做的；去年的數字放到今年。
  • 說反：增加說成減少，原因說成結果。
  • 拼湊：把文章裡兩個不相干的字湊成一句，看起來很眼熟。
  • 文中沒提：合乎常識，但找不到證據句。

示範

Mangroves are trees that grow along tropical coasts, where their tangled roots stand half in salt water. For a long time, these muddy forests were considered useless, and huge areas were cleared to make room for shrimp farms and hotels. Scientists now know better. The roots break the force of incoming waves, so villages behind a mangrove forest suffer less damage in storms. The calm water among the roots also serves as a nursery, where young fish can hide from bigger fish until they are big enough to swim out to sea. For these reasons, several countries have begun replanting the forests they once cut down.

According to the passage, how do mangroves help coastal villages?

(A) They provide wood for building hotels.(B) They reduce the power of waves in storms.(C) They keep large fish away from shrimp farms.(D) They turn salt water into fresh water.
  1. 定位字是 villages，回文章找到第四句：The roots break the force of incoming waves, so villages … suffer less damage in storms.
  2. 自己先回答：樹根擋住海浪，村子受損比較少。
  3. 對選項：(B) 的 reduce the power of waves 就是 break the force of incoming waves 的改寫。(A) 拼湊，hotels 是當初砍樹的原因。(C) 拼湊加偷換對象，文章說的是小魚躲大魚，和養蝦場無關。(D) 文中沒提。

題型 3　推論

常見問法

  • What can be inferred from the passage?
  • Which of the following can be inferred about …?
  • What does the author imply by saying "…"?
  • Which of the following would the author most likely agree with?

答案在哪裡

  • 推論題的答案文章沒有直接寫出來，但一定有一句話可以當證據。正解＝文章的事實＋一步邏輯，只走一步，不多走。
  • 做法：四個選項逐一問「證據在哪一句？」指不出句子的選項就刪。
文章這樣寫可以推論（一步）不能推論（走太遠）
The shop no longer opens on Sundays.以前星期天有營業。星期六也不營業。
Unlike most of her classmates, Amy walks to school.大多數同學不是走路上學。Amy 家離學校最近。
The principal finally agreed to meet the students.校長沒有一開始就答應。校長接受了學生的要求。

誘答

  • 過度推論：把「有些」說成「全部」，把「可能」說成「一定」。看到 all、every、always、never、only、must、completely 要回文章確認有沒有說得這麼滿。
  • 常識陷阱：符合常識、聽起來很有道理，但文章沒有證據。推論題最容易錯在這裡。
  • 判斷技巧：正解的語氣通常比較保守（may、some、tend to、not necessarily）。兩個選項難以取捨時，先懷疑說得比較滿的那一個。

示範

When Lincoln High moved its first class from 7:30 to 8:30 last September, some teachers worried that students would simply stay up an hour later. A survey taken in December told a different story. On average, students were going to bed at almost the same time as before but sleeping about fifty minutes longer. The number of students arriving late had dropped by a third, and the school nurse reported fewer visits for headaches. Not everyone is pleased, though. Sports teams now finish practice after dark, and several parents have had to rearrange their work schedules.

Which of the following can be inferred from the passage?

(A) What the teachers feared did not happen.(B) All parents are against the new schedule.(C) Students' grades improved after the change.(D) The sports teams have stopped practicing.
  1. (A) 證據：老師擔心學生會晚一小時睡（第一句），調查卻顯示上床時間幾乎不變（第三句）。事實加一步邏輯：擔心的事沒有發生。
  2. (B) 過度推論：文章只說 several parents 得調整工作時間，沒說 all，也沒說他們反對。
  3. (C) 常識陷阱：睡得多、成績變好很合理，但文章沒提成績。
  4. (D) 說反：球隊是練到天黑，不是不練。

題型 4　字義與指涉

常見問法

  • What does "they" in the second paragraph refer to?
  • What does "this" in the last sentence most likely refer to?
  • Which of the following is closest in meaning to "…" in the third paragraph?
  • What does the author mean by "…" in the last paragraph?

指涉題：往前找，代回去

  1. 看代名詞的形式：單數或複數？指人或指物？
  2. 往前找：先找同一句的前半，再找前一句。把單複數相符的名詞都列為候選。
  3. 把候選一個一個代回原句讀。語意通順的才是答案；離得最近的名詞不一定對。
  4. this、that、it、such 可以指「前面整句話說的那件事」。選項如果是一件事而不是一個東西，就往這個方向想。

字義題：考的是「在這裡」的意思

  1. 就算認識這個字，也要回去看上下文。題目常考熟字的另一個意思（見 V4），或是你沒看過的片語。
  2. 在前後句找線索：重述（冒號、破折號、or、that is、in other words 後面的解釋）、對比（but、unlike、while、instead 的兩邊意思相反）、舉例（such as、for example）、因果（because、so）。
  3. 把四個選項代回原句，看哪一個讓前後句說得通。

誘答

  • 字義題：這個字最常見的意思或字面的意思（在這篇文章裡不是）；和正解意思相反的字。
  • 指涉題：離代名詞最近的名詞；同一句裡另一個單複數相符的名詞。

示範

Many smartphone apps are designed to be hard to put down. Endless video feeds, for example, remove every natural stopping point, so users keep scrolling long after they meant to stop. Some designers now admit that such features take advantage of the way the human brain responds to rewards. A few companies have begun to add tools that remind users to take a break, but critics argue that these are little more than a fig leaf: they make the companies look responsible while the basic design stays the same.

(1) What does "these" in the last sentence refer to?

(A) Rewards(B) Video feeds(C) Companies(D) Reminder tools

(2) Which of the following is closest in meaning to "a fig leaf"?

(A) a kind of plant(B) a real solution(C) a cover for a problem(D) a serious warning
  1. 第 (1) 題：these 是複數、指物。同一句前半的候選是 companies 和 tools（users 指人，選項裡也沒有）。代回去：冒號後面說 they make the companies look responsible，「它們讓公司看起來負責」，所以 these 不是公司，而是提醒休息的工具，選 (D)。(A)、(B) 離得遠，代回去語意也不通。
  2. 第 (2) 題：冒號是重述的記號。後面解釋：讓公司看起來負責，但基本設計沒變。可見 a fig leaf 是「遮住問題的東西」，選 (C)。(A) 是字面的意思；(B) 說反，批評者正是認為問題沒解決；(D) 文中沒提。

題型 5　作者態度與寫作目的

常見問法

  • What is the author's attitude toward …?
  • How does the author feel about …?
  • What is the purpose of the passage?
  • Why does the author mention … in the second paragraph?

答案在哪裡

  • 態度：找帶評價的字，在旁邊標「＋」或「－」。包括形容詞、副詞（fortunately、sadly、surprisingly）和助動詞（should、must）。
  • 轉折之後才是作者的看法：Supporters say A, but B. 作者站在 B 這一邊。最後一段通常是作者的結論或建議。
  • 分清楚是「誰」的看法：supporters、critics、some people 說的話，不等於作者說的話。
  • 全文只陳述事實、沒有評價字，態度就是 objective 或 neutral。
  • 寫作目的：看文體。說明事實或介紹新知是 to explain／introduce／inform；有 should、must 並提出建議是 to argue／persuade／warn；講一段經歷是 to share／tell；公告或廣告是 to announce／promote。
  • 為什麼提到某個例子：例子、數字、引言的功能是支持它前面那一句。回去讀例子的前一句，答案就是那一句的改寫，不是例子本身的內容。
態度選項常用的字
正面supportive 支持的、approving 贊成的、optimistic 樂觀的、enthusiastic 熱衷的、admiring 讚賞的
負面critical 批評的、disapproving 不贊成的、doubtful／skeptical 懷疑的、concerned 擔心的、pessimistic 悲觀的
中立或有保留objective 客觀的、neutral 中立的、cautious 謹慎的、reserved 有保留的
很少是正解indifferent 漠不關心的（不關心就不會寫這篇文章）、hostile 有敵意的（太極端）

誘答

  • 太極端：作者只是有保留，選項說成強烈反對。
  • 偷換對象：把文中某一方的態度當成作者的態度。
  • 太窄：目的題選到某一段的細節。

示範

Supporters of shared electric scooters describe them as a clean answer to city traffic, and in one way they are right: a short scooter ride produces less pollution than the same trip by car. The trouble is that, according to surveys in several cities, many scooter rides replace walking or public transportation, not driving. Meanwhile, scooters left lying across sidewalks have become a daily obstacle for wheelchair users and parents with strollers. None of this means that scooters should be banned. It does mean that cities need clear parking rules and reliable data before they welcome thousands more.

What is the author's attitude toward shared electric scooters?

(A) Enthusiastic(B) Cautious(C) Hostile(D) Indifferent
  1. 標評價字：「＋」in one way they are right；「－」The trouble is、a daily obstacle。有正有負。
  2. 看最後兩句的結論：不該禁止，但要先有停放規則和可靠的資料。這是有條件的接受，選 (B)。
  3. (A) 偷換對象，a clean answer 是支持者的說法。(C) 太極端，作者明說不必禁止。(D) 作者提出了具體建議，不是不關心。

同一段可以再問：Why does the author mention "wheelchair users and parents with strollers"? 回去看這個例子所在的句子，它要說明的是「亂停的滑板車成了路上的障礙」。答案就是這個論點的改寫，例如 To show that badly parked scooters cause trouble for other people.

題型 6　文章結構與下一段

常見問法

  • How is the passage organized?
  • How does the author begin the passage?
  • What is the function of the third paragraph?
  • What is most likely to be discussed in the paragraph that follows?
  • Which of the following shows the correct order of the events?（排序，見第五節）

答案在哪裡

  • 結構題：每讀完一段，在旁邊寫 2 到 4 個字的標籤（現象、原因、例子、反對意見、解決方法、結論）。把標籤連起來，就是文章的組織方式。
  • 段落之間的關係看段首的轉折語：However 是轉向或反駁，For example 是舉例，As a result 是結果，Another 是並列。
  • 問開頭方式時，選項不外乎：一個問題、一個小故事、一個驚人的事實或數字、一般人的看法、一句引言。
  • 下一段題：只看最後一段，尤其是最後一句。最後一句「提出來但還沒講」的東西，就是下一段的內容。線索包括新出現的名詞、問句、冒號、another、however。
組織方式選項的說法文章裡的線索
時間順序in order of time年份、later、then、today
問題與解決by presenting a problem and its solutionproblem、challenge、solve、solution
原因與結果by explaining causes and effectsbecause、lead to、as a result
比較與對照by comparing two thingswhile、unlike、similarly、in contrast
總說與舉例by giving examplesfor example、one …, another …
一般看法與反駁by challenging a common beliefMany people think … However, …

誘答

  • 下一段題：回頭講前面已經講完的內容；跳到文章從沒提過的新話題；只跟全文主題有關，卻接不上最後一句。
  • 結構題：把某一段的寫法說成全文的組織方式（太窄）。

示範

Every school day, about a third of the food served in the cafeteria at Riverside Senior High used to end up in the trash. Last year, a group of students decided to find out why. After weighing the leftovers for two weeks, they discovered that most of the waste was rice and vegetables: students were simply being given more than they could eat. The group persuaded the kitchen to let students choose their own portion sizes, and food waste fell by forty percent in a single semester. Encouraged by this result, the students are now turning to a harder problem: the hundreds of single-use containers the cafeteria throws away every day.

(1) How is the passage organized?

(A) By comparing two schools.(B) By listing rules in order of importance.(C) By presenting a problem and how it was solved.(D) By arguing for and against an idea.

(2) What is most likely to be discussed in the paragraph that follows?

(A) Why students dislike vegetables.(B) How the leftovers were weighed.(C) Ways to cut down on disposable containers.(D) What other schools serve for lunch.
  1. 第 (1) 題：逐句寫標籤：問題（三分之一的食物被丟掉）→ 找原因（秤廚餘）→ 原因（給太多）→ 解決與成果（自選份量，減少四成）→ 下一個問題。選 (C)。
  2. 第 (2) 題：只看最後一句。冒號後面提出 single-use containers，還沒展開，下一段就講它，選 (C)。disposable 是 single-use 的改寫。(B) 回頭講已經講完的事；(A)、(D) 是文章沒提過的新話題，接不上最後一句。

三、讀文章的方法

1. 先看題目，還是先看文章？

先把文章從頭讀完，不知道該注意什麼，找答案時等於再讀一次；先把題目連選項全部讀完，每篇 16 個選項裡有 12 個是錯的，等於先把錯的資訊裝進腦袋。建議的做法是：先看題幹，不看選項。題幹告訴你這篇要找什麼。

一篇 7 分鐘的流程

  1. 看題幹（30 秒）：圈出定位字，在題號旁邊寫題型（主、細、推、字、態、構）。NOT 圈起來。
  2. 讀第一段（1 分鐘）：找出主題，猜文章接下來會怎麼走。
  3. 讀其餘各段（3 分鐘）：每段寫標籤。讀到定位字所在的句子，就停下來作答那一題。
  4. 回答剩下的題（2 分鐘）：主旨、態度、結構題最後答，用各段的標籤作答。
  5. 檢查（30 秒）：每一題都指得出證據句；確認 NOT 題沒有選成「有提到的」。

2. 每段找主題句

主題句是一段裡最概括的那一句，其他句子都在解釋它或替它舉例。它通常出現在四個位置：

  • 第一句：最常見。先讀每段第一句，就能抓到全文的骨架。
  • 轉折之後：段落用 Many people think、It is often said、For years、In the past 開頭時，預期後面會有 but、however、yet。主題句在轉折之後。
  • 問句之後：段落用問句開頭，問句的答案就是主題句。
  • 最後一句：段落先舉例或說故事，最後才用 therefore、in short、this shows that 收結論。

(1) For years, the public library near our school was known mainly as a quiet place to borrow novels. (2) That has changed since it reopened last spring: the library now lends far more than books. (3) Members can, for example, take home a sewing machine, a telescope, or a set of camping gear for up to two weeks. (4) As a result, the number of visitors under twenty has more than doubled.

  • (1) For years … was known：過去的情況，是引子。預期後面有轉折。
  • (2) That has changed：轉折。主題句在這裡：圖書館現在借的不只是書。
  • (3) for example：例子，支持第 (2) 句，第一遍快讀。(4) As a result：結果。
  • 題目問這一段的主旨，答案是第 (2) 句的改寫；問 Why does the author mention a telescope?，答案也要回到第 (2) 句：說明圖書館出借各式各樣的物品。

3. 邊讀邊標記

看到怎麼標為什麼
轉折：but、however、yet、although、in fact、instead在轉折後面的半句畫底線重點在後面。主旨題、態度題的答案常在這裡。
例子：for example、such as、including、冒號後的列舉用括號括起來第一遍快讀，題目問到再回來。
因果：because、since、so、therefore、lead to、result in、due to畫箭頭，從原因指向結果細節題常問 Why，選項常把因果說反。
列舉：first、second、another、in addition、finally標 1、2、3NOT 題的選項常從這裡出。
專有名詞、年份、數字圈起來細節題的定位字；排序題、時間軸要用。
評價字：unfortunately、surprisingly、should、harmful標「＋」或「－」態度題的證據。
定義：is called、known as、refer to、括號、破折號、同位語在生字旁邊寫「＝」生字的意思就在旁邊，不必慌。

不要整篇畫滿。一段畫超過三處，就等於沒畫；標記是為了回頭找得到。

4. 時間不夠時的取捨

  • 一題想超過 1 分鐘：刪到剩兩個，先選一個，在題號旁打問號，往下走。全部做完再回來。
  • 三篇的順序：三篇配分相同。先掃一眼三篇的第一句，題材陌生的那篇放最後。
  • 只剩 3 分鐘，還有一整篇沒讀：不要從頭讀。照下面的順序搶分：
  1. 字義題、指涉題：題幹有位置，只讀那一句和前後句。
  2. 有專有名詞或數字的細節題：用定位字掃文章，只讀那幾句。
  3. 主旨題：讀第一段和每段第一句。
  4. 推論題、NOT 題：最花時間，放最後。來不及就從還沒刪掉的選項裡選一個。

單選題答錯不倒扣，答題卷不要留白。猜之前至少刪掉一個選項：說得太滿的（all、never、only）和文章完全沒提過的，優先刪。

四、選項的對應技巧：同義改寫

正解幾乎不會照抄原文。它把原文換一種說法：字換了，意思沒換。認不出改寫，就會覺得「四個選項文章都沒寫」。下表整理六種改寫手法，共 20 組。練習時遮住右邊，先自己想：這句話還能怎麼說？

手法文章這樣寫選項這樣說
同義字、片語替換The bridge was completed ahead of schedule.The bridge was finished earlier than planned.
同義字、片語替換Heavy rain forced the organizers to call off the concert.The concert was canceled because of bad weather.
同義字、片語替換The app was originally designed for doctors.At first, the app was meant for doctors.
同義字、片語替換He turned down the job offer.He refused to take the job.
反義字加否定The museum is open every day except Monday.The museum is closed on Mondays.
反義字加否定The medicine is not without side effects.The medicine has some side effects.
反義字加否定Scientists have yet to find a cure for the disease.No cure for the disease has been found so far.
反義字加否定She was the only one in her family to attend college.No one else in her family went to college.
數字與比較The number of visitors has doubled since 2015.There are now twice as many visitors as in 2015.
數字與比較Fewer than half of the students passed the test.Most of the students failed the test.
數字與比較The festival dates back to the twelfth century.The festival has a history of over 800 years.
數字與比較Unlike his brother, Tom rarely eats out.Tom's brother eats out more often than Tom does.
換主詞、換詞性Few people could afford a car at that time.Cars were too expensive for most people then.
換主詞、換詞性Fruit growers depend heavily on bees.Bees play a key role in fruit farming.
換主詞、換詞性The price of rice rose sharply last year.Rice became much more expensive last year.
換主詞、換詞性The mayor's plan met with strong opposition from residents.Residents were strongly against the mayor's plan.
因果換一邊說The drought led to a poor harvest.The harvest was poor because there had been too little rain.
因果換一邊說Thanks to the new bus line, fewer students are late for school.The new bus line has helped students arrive on time.
具體與概括互換Apples, pears, and peaches are grown in the valley.The valley produces various kinds of fruit.
具體與概括互換He has worked as a waiter, a taxi driver, and a tour guide.He has had several different jobs.

假改寫：看起來像，意思卻變了

原文：Some studies suggest that drinking green tea may lower the risk of heart disease.

誤Green tea prevents heart disease.（「可能降低風險」變成「能預防」，程度變了。）

誤Most studies show that green tea is good for the heart.（some 變成 most，範圍變了。）

誤People with heart disease should drink green tea.（文章沒有建議誰該喝，對象變了。）

正According to some research, green tea might be good for the heart.

核對改寫時檢查四點：程度（some 或 most、may 或 will）、方向（增或減、誰因誰果）、對象（誰、何時、何地）、範圍（only、also、except、at least 有沒有被加上或拿掉）。

五、圖表與多文本

近年的閱讀測驗和混合題，有時會在文章旁邊放一張表、一條時間軸，或把兩篇短文放在一起問。這類題的讀法和一般文章不同：不是從頭讀到尾，而是帶著題目去查。混合題的作答方式（填空、簡答、配對）在 M1 說明，這裡只講怎麼讀。

1. 表格：先讀欄名和備註，再對條件

  1. 先讀標題、欄名、列名、單位和備註（Note、星號、括號）。陷阱常在備註和括號裡。
  2. 把題幹的條件一個一個圈出來（身分、時間、數量），每個條件對到一欄或一列。
  3. 需要計算或比較時，把算式寫在旁邊，不要心算。留意 at least、up to、no more than、per person 這些字。

示範　City Science Museum: Tickets

TicketPriceIncludes
RegularNT$200Main exhibition
Student (ID required)NT$150Main exhibition
ComboNT$320Main exhibition + one 3D film
Evening (Fri. and Sat., after 5 p.m.)NT$100Main exhibition

Note: A ticket for one 3D film alone is NT$160. Children under six enter free.

Mia is a high school student with a student ID. She wants to see the main exhibition and one 3D film on a Sunday afternoon. What is the least she has to pay?

(A) NT$260(B) NT$310(C) NT$320(D) NT$360
  1. 圈條件：student ID、main exhibition 加 3D film、Sunday afternoon、the least。
  2. 對表格：學生票 150；電影單買 160（寫在備註裡）；150＋160＝310。套票 320，比較貴。
  3. 夜間票 100 只限週五、週六下午五點以後，星期天下午不能用，所以 (A) 260（100＋160）是陷阱。(D) 360 是全票加電影，沒用到學生身分。答案是 (B)。

2. 時間軸與排序：把相對時間換成年份

  • 讀文章時把年份圈起來，旁邊寫兩三個字的事件。three years later、a decade earlier 這種相對時間，當場換算成年份。
  • 敘述的順序不等於發生的順序。By the time、before、earlier、previously 和過去完成式（had 加過去分詞）都表示「更早」。
  • 排序題先找最早和最晚的事件，用頭尾刪選項。

By the time the first world championship was held in Budapest in 1982, the Rubik's Cube had already become a worldwide craze. Yet its inventor, Erno Rubik, had never planned to make a toy. In 1974, the young Hungarian teacher built the first cube out of wood as a teaching tool for his design students. It went on sale in Budapest toy shops three years later as the "Magic Cube." Only after it was renamed and introduced to the international market in 1980 did sales take off.

Which of the following shows the correct order of the events?

a. The cube was sold abroad under a new name.　b. The first world championship took place.　c. The cube appeared in shops in Budapest.　d. Rubik made a wooden cube for his students.

(A) d → c → a → b(B) b → d → c → a(C) d → a → c → b(D) c → d → a → b

把年份排出來：1974 做出木頭方塊（d）→ 1977 在布達佩斯上市（c，three years later 是 1974＋3）→ 1980 改名並進入國際市場（a）→ 1982 世界錦標賽（b）。答案是 (A)。(B) 是文章敘述的順序：第一句就講 1982 年，但那是四件事裡最晚發生的。

3. 兩篇短文的對照：畫一張「同與異」的表

  1. 每讀完一篇，用一句話寫下「立場加最主要的理由」。
  2. 列出相同點和不同點。讓步的句子（I agree that …、It is true that …）最常是相同點的來源。
  3. 題目問 both，兩篇都要找得到證據；問 unlike、differ，只有一篇有。

Kevin: I think our school should collect phones every morning. I have seen classmates checking messages under their desks all through math class. Without phones, we would also talk to each other more at lunch. And if there is an emergency, parents can always call the school office.

Rachel: Collecting phones treats every student like a rule-breaker. I use mine to look up words in English class and to take photos of the board in chemistry. I agree that some students get distracted, but the answer is to teach self-control, not to take a useful tool away.

Which of the following do Kevin and Rachel both agree on?

(A) Phones can be useful in class.(B) Phones distract some students.(C) Parents should call the school office.(D) Students should be taught self-control.

答案是 (B)，兩個人的短文裡都找得到證據。(A)、(D) 只有 Rachel 說；(C) 只有 Kevin 說。

多文本題最常見的誘答是偷換對象：把甲的理由放到乙的名下。作答時在每個選項旁邊寫上是誰說的。

第一篇（R1-01 至 R1-04）

On a Norwegian island about 1,300 kilometers from the North Pole, a narrow concrete entrance sticks out of a snowy mountainside. Behind it, a tunnel runs more than 100 meters into the rock to the Svalbard Global Seed Vault. Since it opened in 2008, the vault has received more than a million seed samples—rice, wheat, beans, and many other crops—that come from nearly every country on Earth.

The vault is not a store that farmers visit when they need seeds. It is a backup. Around the world, more than 1,700 seed banks keep collections of food crops, but many of them are threatened by floods, fires, wars, or simply a lack of money. Each bank can send copies of its seeds to Svalbard, where they are kept at minus 18 degrees Celsius. Even if the electricity failed, the frozen ground around the vault would keep them cold for a long time. The seeds still belong to the bank that sent them, and no one else may open its boxes.

The value of this system became clear in 2015. A major seed bank near the Syrian city of Aleppo, which held wheat and other crops suited to dry climates, could no longer operate normally because of the war. Its scientists, who had moved abroad, asked for some of their boxes back. With those seeds, they planted new fields in Lebanon and Morocco, harvested fresh seeds, and in 2017 began sending new copies to the vault.

The vault is often called the "Doomsday Vault," a name that suggests it will be opened only after some worldwide disaster. What happened in Syria shows that it does not have to wait that long. Its real job is less dramatic: making sure that when one collection is lost, the crops in it are not lost forever.

第二篇（R1-05 至 R1-08）

In cities from London to Tokyo, beehives have appeared on the roofs of hotels, offices, and schools. Rooftop honey is sold in gift shops, and companies proudly mention their bees in advertisements. The owners of these hives usually have the best of intentions. Having read that bees are disappearing, they believe that every new hive is a small step toward saving them.

Many scientists, however, see the trend differently. The bee in trouble, they point out, is not the honeybee. Honeybees are farm animals, raised in huge numbers like chickens or cows, and the world has more hives today than it did fifty years ago. The bees that are truly at risk are the wild ones: thousands of species of bumblebees and solitary bees that nest in the ground or in dead wood and belong to no one.

For these wild bees, a rooftop hive is not a rescue but a rival. A single hive can hold tens of thousands of honeybees, all of which must collect nectar and pollen from the same limited supply of city flowers. When hives multiply faster than flowers, it is the wild bees that go hungry. Honeybees can also pass diseases on to their wild cousins when they visit the same blossoms.

City beekeepers are not the villains of this story. Their hives produce honey, show children where food comes from, and get people talking about nature. But keeping honeybees to save bees is a little like raising chickens to save wild birds. If the goal is to protect the bees that really need help, experts say, there are better things to do with a roof or a garden than to put a hive on it.
